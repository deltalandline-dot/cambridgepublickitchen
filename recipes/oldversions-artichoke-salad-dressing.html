Artichoke Salad Dressing

1.5qts  

  • 33oz marinated artichoke hearts add all including oil)
  • 1t tamari (omit if no-soy people on the retreat, probably need more salt at the end..)
  • 1T Dijon mustard 
  • ½ t honey
  • ½  t white pepper (or more to taste..)
  • ½  c water
  • ½  c white wine vinegar (or rice vinegar)
  • 1  c canola oil
  • Salt to taste

Add all except oil to the blender.  Blend.

If room in the Vitamix, drizzle in the oil as you keep blending. If no room, transfer to a larger container and whisk in the oil

Taste (drizzle a little into a separate spoon) and adjust salt, pepper or other seasonings

For one meal: fill 2 small pitchers.  Serve with smallest ladles.
