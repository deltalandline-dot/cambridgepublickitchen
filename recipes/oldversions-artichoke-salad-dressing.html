Artichoke Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~1.5 quarts

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Add all ingredients except the oil to the blender. Blend.
  2. If there's room in the Vitamix, drizzle in the oil as you keep blending. If there's no room, transfer to a larger container and whisk in the oil.
  3. Taste (drizzle a little into a separate spoon) and adjust salt, pepper, or other seasonings.
  4. For one meal: fill 2 small pitchers. Serve with the smallest ladles.
