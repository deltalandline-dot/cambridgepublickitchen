Preparation

  1. Be sure the spinach is out to thaw early.
  2. Sauté onions, garlic, and mushrooms with 2 tsp salt. Cook until the liquid is mostly gone.
  3. Mix with bottled pasta sauce to total about 7.5 qts (put the mushroom mix in an 8 qt container and add sauce to fill). Note: may want to use some plain pasta sauce for the bottom layers.
  4. Strain and press the spinach (maybe with gloves, wring it out). Set aside 1 cup for the alt version.
  5. Make cheese mix: ricotta + goat cheese + nutmeg + beaten eggs + squeezed-dry spinach; maybe add 1-2 cups breadcrumbs (leave 2 cups total for the topping).
  6. Make alt filling: blend tofu, lemon juice, and nutmeg, then mix with spinach.
  7. Mix 4 cups parmesan, 2 cups mozzarella, and 2 cups bread crumbs for the topping.
  8. Preboil the GF noodles?
  9. Maybe set up a ramekin of cheese and sauce for the no-soy, no-gluten person.

Layers for 1 hotel pan (consider using a 4" deep pan just to avoid spillover): 2 cups sauce (maybe plain, without chunky mushrooms), 7½ sheets TJ's lasagna noodles, 5 cups cheese/spinach mix, 3 cups sauce, 3 cups mozzarella, 7½ sheets TJ's lasagna noodles, 5 cups cheese/spinach mix, 3 cups sauce, 7½ sheets TJ's lasagna noodles, 3 cups sauce, and topping (~4 cups per pan).
  11. Layers for alt version (need 6-7 servings?): ½ cup sauce, sheets of GF noodles, ½ the alt filling, 1 cup sauce, a soy cheese layer(?), more noodles, the remaining ½ alt filling, 1 cup sauce, another soy cheese layer(?).
