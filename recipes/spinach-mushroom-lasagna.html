Spinach Mushroom Lasagna

Tags: Vegetarian, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Spinach Mushroom Lasagna

Equip: 2 hotel pans @24 each (cut 6x4), plus an 8x8 no-dairy-GF alt.

Ingredients:

  • 5lbs (Half case) mushrooms cut chunky
  • 4lbs onions, med. dice
  • 20cl garlic, chopped in chopper
  • As needed bottled pasta sauce (note how much we use..) to make 8 qts sauce (3c is for alt)
  • 10 x10oz frozen spin bags (2 is for alt version)
  • 5x32oz ricotta
  • 4 tubs TJ goat = 2cups
  • 8 eggs, beaten
  • 2T nutmeg
  • 4c bread crumbs (2c for topping, maybe 2c for cheese mix)
  • 8c shredded mozzarella
  • 4c parmesan

For alt version:

  • Maybe soy cheese?
  • 2 lemons
  • 1T  nutmeg
  • 1 box GF noodles
  • 3lbs firm tofu
  • (and will use some sauce & spinach as noted above)

Lasagna

Instructions:

Be sure spinach is out to thaw early.

Saute onions, garlic & mushrooms w/ 2t salt.  Cook till liquid is mostly gone.

Mix with bottled pasta sauce to amount to ~7.5qts total (put mushr mix in 8qt container and open sauce bottles to fill).  WE MIGHT USE SOME PLAIN PASTA SAUCE FOR THE BOTTOM LAYERS..

Strain, press spinach (maybe with gloves, wring it out).  Set aside 1cup for alt version.

Make cheese mix:  

    • Ricotta + goat + nutmeg + beaten eggs + squeezed dry spinach, 
    • maybe add 1-2 c. breadcrumbs (leave 2 cups total for topping).

Make alt filling: blend tofu, lemon juice, nutmeg & mix with spinach.

Mix 4c parmesan, 2c moz, 2c bread crumbs for topping.

Preboil GF noodles?

Maybe: setup a ramekin of cheese & sauce for no-soy no-gluten person?
