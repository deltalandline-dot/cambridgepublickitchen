Moroccan Chickpeas and Chard

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings with supper (or ~36 servings if planning supper leftovers)

Equipment

  • Rondo for stew

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Note: the chickpeas are not cooked separately, just add them into the stew at the ~10:15 step below.
  2. Sauté: Heat oil in Rondo pan over medium heat. Add onion and jalapeño and sauté until limp, a few minutes.
  3. Add garlic, ginger, salt, turmeric, paprika, cinnamon, cumin, black pepper and cayenne and sauté until they release their fragrance, about 2 minutes.
  4. Add tomato paste and sauté for another minute, until darkened but not burned. (If tomato paste looks too dark too quickly, lower heat.)
  5. Add fennel, chard stems, carrot and turnip/potatoes and continue to sauté until vegetables start to soften, about 20 minutes.
  6. Add chickpeas: By about 10:15, rinse and drain the soaking chickpeas and add to the stew. Add fresh water to cover.
  7. Return heat to high if you lowered it and bring to a simmer. Partly cover pot, lower heat to medium low, and simmer for about 1 1/2 to 2 hours (i.e. for the rest of the morning), until chickpeas are softened. Add more water as needed (this should be like a stew and nearly fill the Rondo once the chard is added).
  8. Finish: About 11:30, add apricots.
  9. About 11:50, add chard leaves and lemon juice & zest to taste, continue simmering until chard is tender, about 5 minutes longer.
  10. Season with more salt if desired, and serve garnished with reserved fennel fronds.
  11. Serve with Greek yogurt and cilantro on the side.
