Loaded Nachos

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~50 servings (or ~36) — typically 2 large sheets dairy + ½ sheet vegan for 50, or 1½ sheets dairy + ½ sheet vegan for 36

Equipment

  • 2 large baking sheets
  • 1 half-sheet, for the no-cheese version

Ingredients

Preparation (during the morning we just get ready — the dish is assembled at 5:00)

  1. Set up for later use (storage containers are fine, don't need to be pretty): beans, cheddar, black olives, green chilies, scallions.
  2. Set up nicely for serving on the side: jalapenos, sour cream.
  3. (At 3:50, KM) Set up pans with a thick layer of chips; stage the rest of the ingredients nearby.
  4. (At 5:00, KM) Turn both ovens on to 400°F. Sprinkle the chips with beans, scallions, olives, chilies, salsa, and cheese.
  5. (~5:15) Put into the hot ovens for ~10-15 minutes, until warm and the cheese is melted.
