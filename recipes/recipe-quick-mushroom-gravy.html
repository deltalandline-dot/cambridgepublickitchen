Instructions:

Brown mushrooms & onions

Use the 2 largest skillets and brown mushrooms and onions at the same time.   Cook the mushrooms at least until all the water is gone.  Ideally get the onions a bit browned, but at least very soft.  When done, you could “deglaze” the pans: put  a cup or so of stock water in the pans and stir it up to capture the flavored oil and caramelized bits, pour it back into the stock pot.

Stock

Mix the bouillon and water until dissolved (for ~1qt of the water, use some hot tea-urn water to dissolve the bouillon).

Add rest

Add all other ingredients except cornstarch.

Thicken

Mix cornstarch with enough cold water to dissolve it.  Add to the  gravy.  Bring to a boil for 3-5 minutes until it thickens.  Return to simmer.  Taste and adjust seasoning if needed.
