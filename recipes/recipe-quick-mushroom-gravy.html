Quick Mushroom Gravy

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings with supper leftovers (the alternate quantity column covers ~48 servings with no supper leftovers, or ~36 servings with supper).

Equipment

  • 2 largest skillets
  • 10 qt stock pot (or the largest of the small pots)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Brown mushrooms & onions: Use the 2 largest skillets and brown mushrooms and onions at the same time. Cook the mushrooms at least until all the water is gone. Ideally get the onions a bit browned, but at least very soft. When done, you could "deglaze" the pans: put a cup or so of stock water in the pans and stir it up to capture the flavored oil and caramelized bits, then pour it back into the stock pot.
  2. Stock: Mix the bouillon and water until dissolved (for ~1 qt of the water, use some hot tea-urn water to dissolve the bouillon).
  3. Add rest: Add all other ingredients except the cornstarch.
  4. Thicken: Mix cornstarch with enough cold water to dissolve it. Add to the gravy. Bring to a boil for 3-5 minutes until it thickens. Return to a simmer. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed.
