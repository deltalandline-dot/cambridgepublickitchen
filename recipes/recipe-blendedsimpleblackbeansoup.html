Blended Simple Black Bean Soup

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

If using dry beans: soak overnight, and allow ~1-2 hours to cook them before adding to the soup. The smaller batch leaves some leftover as a base for a supper soup with lots of added veggies, broth, tomatoes, etc.

Yield: ~50 servings (~19 qts), or ~36 servings if planning a supper leftover batch

Equipment

  • 32 qt (largest) stock pot, with pot-saver under it

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Prepare ingredients: cook the beans if not using canned. Empty the soak water, cover by 2" with fresh water, and save (and measure, if possible) the cooking water to use as part of the soup liquid.
  2. Dice the onions and carrots by hand, and chop the garlic in the mini-cuisinart.
  3. Juice the limes (juicer is near the rice-cooker); maybe zest a couple and set the zest aside.
  4. Mix the bouillon in 2 qts hot tea-urn water.
  5. Note: this is not a long-cooking soup — about 1 ½ hrs from start to sauté to finished blending. The beans are cooked separately.
  6. Make soup: be sure the heat-saver disk (on the stove shelf) is placed under the stock pot.
  7. Coat the bottom of the pot with olive oil to about ⅛" deep.
  8. Sauté (fry in a little oil, uncovered, stirring occasionally) the onions, garlic, and carrots until the onions are getting soft and translucent (not brown).
  9. Drain the beans, saving the liquid to use as part of the water measure. Add the rest: black beans, bouillon, water, oregano, coriander, cumin, cayenne, and salt. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, cover, and simmer ~30 minutes. Check that the carrots are soft.
  10. Puree the soup until smooth and creamy (there will still be little flecks of beans) using the immersion blender.
  11. Stir in the lime juice and taste (perhaps more salt, pepper, lime zest, or cayenne).
  12. Serve with any or all of: cilantro, sour cream and/or vegan green chili cream, diced avocado, and roasted pumpkin seeds.
