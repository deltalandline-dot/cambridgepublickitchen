Instructions

Prepare 

Ingredients

Cook the beans if not using canned. Empty soak water, cover by 2” with fresh water, save (& measure?) cooking water to use as part of the soup liquid.

Dice onions, carrots by hand, chop garlic in mini-cuisinart

Juice limes.  Juicer is near rice-cooker.  Maybe zest a couple and set zest aside.

Mix bouillon in 2 qts hot tea-urn water.

NOTE: THIS IS NOT A LONG-COOKING SOUP, about 1 ½ hrs from start to saute to finish blending.  Beans are cooked separately.

Make

Soup

Be sure heat-saver disk (on stove shelf) is placed under stock pot 

Coat the bottom of the pot with olive oil to about ⅛” deep

Sauté (=fry in a little oil uncovered, stirring occasionally) the onions, garlic and carrots until the onions are getting soft and translucent (not brown).

Drain beans saving liquid and use the liquid as part of the water measure.  

Add rest: black beans, bouillon, water, oregano, coriander, cumin, cayenne and salt.  Bring to a boil.  Reduce heat, cover and simmer ~30 minutes.  Check that carrots are soft.  

Puree the soup until smooth and creamy (there will still be little flecks of beans) using the immersion blender.

Stir in lime juice and taste (perhaps more salt, pepper, lime zest?, cayenne?)

Serve with any or all of: cilantro, sour cream and/or vegan green chili cream,   diced avocado and roasted pumpkin seeds.
