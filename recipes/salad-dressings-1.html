Balsamic Salad Dressing

Yield: ~1.5 qts (enough for a full house of 44 for one meal), or ~3 qts for 2 meals

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Poach the whole peeled garlic cloves in one cup of the oil (one cup out of the amount called for) in a small saucepan or skillet on medium heat for ~5 minutes to make them more mild.
  2. Add everything except the rest of the oil to the blender (poached garlic cloves and their oil can be added unchopped). Blend.
  3. If there's room in the Vitamix, drizzle in the oil as you keep blending; if not, transfer to a larger container and whisk in the oil.
  4. Taste (drizzle a little into a separate spoon) and adjust salt, pepper, or other seasonings.
  5. For one meal: fill 2 small pitchers. Serve with the smallest ladles.

Orange Ginger Salad Dressing

Yield: ~1.5 qts

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Using a microplane zester or box grater, scrape off just the colored layer of rind from the limes and oranges, trying not to get much of the white pith (it's more bitter). Be careful with your fingers and knuckles — this tool is very sharp!
  2. Using the electric juicer, juice the limes and oranges.
  3. Add everything except the oil to the blender. Blend.
  4. If there's room in the Vitamix, drizzle in the oil as you keep blending; if not, transfer to a larger container and whisk in the oil.
  5. Taste (drizzle a little into a separate spoon) and adjust salt, pepper, or other seasonings.
  6. For one meal: fill 2 small pitchers. Serve with the smallest ladles.
