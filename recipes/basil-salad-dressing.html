Basil Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Recipe: Basil Salad Dressing                                       

Equipment  Vitamix, 2 pitchers, (extra 2qt square container with green lid for second batch)

Ingredients

For 1 batch 

(~48 servs)

1 cup lemon juice

4-5 lemons

½ cup

Rice Vinegar

1 ½ cup

water

1 tsp

garlic powder

¼ cup

dijon mustard

¼ cup

Agave 

½ tsp

salt

¾ cup packed down

fresh basil leaves (pick off from stems)

(= ~¾ of a bunch)

¼  cup packed down

fresh parsley (cut off main trunk of stems, ok to use rest) 

(=~¼  of a bunch)

3 ½  cups

Light olive oil

Instructions

Rinse herbs, no need to chop, vitamix will do it.

Add all except oils in vitamix container and mix on low.

If making 2 batches, measure a second batch except oils into square container.

Drizzle oils into Vitamix mixing while you do.  Gradually increase speed until all emulsified.    Divide between 2 small pitchers.

For second batch: pour measured container ingredients into Vitamix and blend.  Add second batch of oils as above.

Pour back into container, label, date and store in fridge for later.
