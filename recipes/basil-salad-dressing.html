Basil Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings (1 batch)

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • 2 pitchers
  • Extra 2-qt square container with green lid (for a second batch)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Rinse the herbs — no need to chop, the Vitamix will do it.
  2. Add everything except the oils into the Vitamix container and mix on low. If making 2 batches, measure a second batch (except oils) into the square container.
  3. Drizzle the oils into the Vitamix while mixing. Gradually increase speed until fully emulsified. Divide between 2 small pitchers.
  4. For a second batch: pour the measured container ingredients into the Vitamix and blend. Add the second batch of oils as above. Pour back into the container, label, date, and store in the fridge for later.
