Cowboy Cookies Vegan GF

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

From Moosewood Restaurant Favorites.

Yield: Approximately 60 large cookies (4x6=24 on each of 2 large sheets, plus 3x4=12 on a half-size sheet)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Preheat both ovens to 350.
  2. Mix vegan egg with water per directions on package, let sit until ready to use.
  3. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  4. In a separate large bowl, use an electric mixer to beat sugars with Earth Balance until very well mixed. Beat in vegan eggs and vanilla.
  5. If the red table mixer is available, use it for the next step. Else use a large spoon & spatula — it's pretty stiff.
  6. Add the flour mixture to the sugar mixture. Add oats.
  7. Put on latex gloves and mix in all the goodies (nuts, fruit, chips) by hand.
  8. Line 2 large sheet pans and 1 half sheet pan with parchment.
  9. Make large scoops (~¼ cup or a little more) with your gloved hands, flatten onto the sheet pans.
  10. Bake ~20 mins, time varies, start checking at 15. Should be medium brown on bottom, very light brown on top.
  11. Cool on racks. Store in red-snap lid boxes (kept above kitchen desk).
