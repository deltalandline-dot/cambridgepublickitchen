Cowboy Cookies (Vegan, GF)

From Moosewood Restaurant Favorites

Yield: Approximately 60  large cookies

Put 4x6=24 each on 2 lg + 3x4=12 on half-size baking sheets = 60

5 cups GF flour

2 ½ tsp baking soda

1 ½ tsp baking powder

1 ½ tsp salt

----

2 ½ c Earthbalance

1 ¼ c sugar

1 ¼ c brown sugar

5 “vegan eggs” look for packets in pantry

2 ½ tsp vanilla

---

4 c. oats (should be GF, warn if not)

2 c. coconut (ideally large flake, else whatever)

3 c. choc chips

2 c. raisins or mix of raisins & cranberries

3 c. chopped nuts (any or mix of walnuts, pecans, slivered almonds)

DIRECTIONS-->OVER

Directions:

Preheat both ovens to 350.

Mix vegan egg with water per directions on package, let sit until ready to use.

In a large bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. 

In a separate large bowl, use electric mixer to beat sugars with Earthbalance until very well mixed.   Beat in vegan eggs and vanilla.

If the red table mixer is available, use it for next step. Else use large spoon & spatula.  It’s pretty stiff:

Add the flour mixture to the sugar mixture.  Add oats.

Put on latex gloves and mix in all the goodies (nuts, fruit, chips) by hand.

Line 2 large, 1 half sheet pan with parchment.

Make large scoops (~¼ cup or a little more) with your gloved hands,  flatten onto the sheets pans.

Bake ~20 mins, time varies, start checking at 15.  Should be medium brown on bottom, very light brown on top.

Cool on racks.  Store in red-snap lid boxes (kept above kitchen desk).
