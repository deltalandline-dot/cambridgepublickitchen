Instructions

Preheat oven to 400 (after assist has toasted seeds)

Slice each ~pound of tofu into 12 long-way rectangular slices (ie 6 per half-pack in our usual brand).  

Layout flat on sheet pans.

(prep yogi usually does this)

Find and set out all the sauce ingredients

Rough cut the ginger into chunks about ½ inch long.  No need to peel it.  

Using one of the blender pitchers (you can share the machine base with the assist cook if needed):

Put ginger chunks, garlic, miso, sesame oil, agave, rice vinegar and tamari (ie all sauce ingredients except water) in the blender and process gradually raising the speed until smooth.  Pour into large plastic pitcher and add 4 cups of water, whisk.

Pour the sauce over the tofu.  Let the tofu sit in the marinade until time to bake it.  

Put the pans in the oven uncovered for 45-60 minutes total.  

Set a timer to baste every 20 mins or so.   

If looking too dark, turn heat down or off.

If not getting brown by last 20 minutes, turn heat up.

Just before serving, garnish pans with scallions.
