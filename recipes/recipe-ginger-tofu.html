Ginger Tofu

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: 48 servings (lunch, plus some leftovers set aside for a yam side dish)

Equipment

  • Two (and maybe a half) large baking trays
  • Blender
  • Large 4 qt pitcher

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Preheat oven to 400° (after the assist has toasted the seeds).
  2. Slice each ~pound of tofu into 12 long rectangular slices (i.e., 6 per half-pack in our usual brand). Lay flat on sheet pans. (Prep yogi usually does this.)
  3. Find and set out all the sauce ingredients.
  4. Rough-cut the ginger into chunks about ½ inch long — no need to peel it.
  5. Using one of the blender pitchers (you can share the machine base with the assist cook if needed): put the ginger chunks, garlic, miso, sesame oil, agave, rice vinegar, and tamari (i.e., all sauce ingredients except water) in the blender and process, gradually raising the speed until smooth. Pour into a large plastic pitcher, add 4 cups of water, and whisk.
  6. Pour the sauce over the tofu. Let the tofu sit in the marinade until time to bake it.
  7. Put the pans in the oven, uncovered, for 45-60 minutes total. Set a timer to baste every 20 minutes or so. If it's looking too dark, turn the heat down or off. If it's not getting brown by the last 20 minutes, turn the heat up.
  8. Just before serving, garnish the pans with scallions.
