Orange Vinaigrette

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Recipe: Orange Vinaigrette                                  

Equipment  Vitamix, microplane zester (or box grater), electric juicer, 2 pitchers

Ingredients

For 1 batch 

(~48 servs)

~1 Tbsp

zest of 2 limes //zest is just the outermost colored layer of the peel

~2 Tbsp

zest of 2 oranges

~1-2 Tbsp

juice of the two limes

2 ½  cups

See if there is store-bought fresh OJ, if not, juice ~6 oranges

½ cup

Apple cider vinegar

½  cup

Dijon Mustard

½ cup

agave

1 cup

olive oil

2 cups

Light olive oil

1 T

salt

1 Tbls 

Black pepper

Instructions

Zest limes and oranges with zester.  BE CAREFUL! VERY SHARP!

Juice limes and oranges with juicer (next to rice cooker).

Add all except oil in vitamix container and mix on low.

Drizzle oil into Vitamix mixing while you do.  Gradually increase speed until all emulsified.    Divide between 2 small pitchers.

Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
