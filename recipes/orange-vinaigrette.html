Orange Vinaigrette

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings (1 batch)

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • Microplane zester (or box grater)
  • Electric juicer
  • 2 pitchers

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Zest the limes and oranges with the zester. Be careful, very sharp!
  2. Juice the limes and oranges with the juicer (next to the rice cooker).
  3. Add all ingredients except the oils to the Vitamix container and mix on low.
  4. Drizzle the oil into the Vitamix while mixing. Gradually increase speed until fully emulsified. Divide between 2 small pitchers.
  5. Cover and store in the fridge (if not using today, label and date also).
