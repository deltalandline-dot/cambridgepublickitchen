Cashew Ranch Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Recipe: Cashew “Ranch” Salad Dressing                                       

Equipment  Vitamix, 2 pitchers

Ingredients

For 1 batch 

(~48 servs)

4 cups

raw cashews

2 ¼ cups

light olive oil

¾ cup

apple cider vinegar

3 cups

water

2 cloves (or ½ tsp)

garlic cloves (or powder)

1 ½ tsp

salt

1 ½ Tbsp

dill, fresh or dried

2 ½ Tbsp

parsley leaves, chopped

1 ½ Tbsp

oregano

1 tsp

rosemary (check garden outside kitchen door, finely chop)

1 tsp

pepper

Instructions

Add all ingredients to vitamix container and mix on low, slowly increasing speed until all pureed.

Taste and adjust seasonings if needed.

Divide between 2 small pitchers (save any extra in storage container).

Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
