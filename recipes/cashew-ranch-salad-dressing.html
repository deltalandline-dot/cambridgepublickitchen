Cashew Ranch Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings per batch

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • 2 pitchers

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Add all ingredients to Vitamix container and mix on low, slowly increasing speed until all puréed.
  2. Taste and adjust seasonings if needed.
  3. Divide between 2 small pitchers (save any extra in a storage container).
  4. Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
