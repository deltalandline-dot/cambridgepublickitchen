Apple Crisp

Tags: Vegetarian, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Below shows quantities for 1 ½ hotel pans with wheat & butter, plus a half-pan with GF flour and Earth Balance.

Yield: ~1 ½ full hotel pans (wheat/butter version) plus a half-pan GF/Earth Balance version (total ~16 lbs apples for 2 pans)

Ingredients — filling

Ingredients — topping

Preparation

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees. (Peel and) core apples and cut them into large uniform chunks. (Can keep apples overnight in lemon water.) Place apples in a bowl and toss with lemon zest, lemon juice, granulated sugar, flour, salt and cinnamon. Transfer to either 5 one-cup ramekins or an 8″ square baking dish.
  2. For the topping: Combine flour, sugars, salt, oats, and cold (or, easier, melted) butter. Mix until well combined and crumbly.
  3. Spread evenly over fruit mixture and pat down slightly.
  4. Place baking dish on a cookie sheet and bake for one hour (allow for up to an hour 15 mins), until top is browned and fruit is bubbly. (You can also refrigerate, covered, and bake later.)
  5. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream.
