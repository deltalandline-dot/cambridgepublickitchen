Apple Crisp

Tags: Vegetarian, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Apple Crisp 

Below shows for 1 ½ hotel pans with wheat & butter,  plus a half-pan with GF flour and Earth Balance.   

Ingredients

For the filling:

For 1 ½ hotel pans:  For ½ pan GF: 

30-36 (~12+lbs) 10-12 (~4+lbs) large apples   (total for 2 pans=16lbs)

(half Granny Smiths half something sweeter)

juice of a couple of lemons in water to save apples as you work

1 ½ ½  cup granulated sugar

¾ c 4 tablespoons flour/GF flour

6t 2 teaspoon cinnamon

¾ t    ¼ t of kosher salt

For the topping:

4 ½ 1 ½  cup flour/GF flour

1 ½ ½  cup granulated sugar

2 ¼ c ¾ cup brown sugar

1 ½ t ½  teaspoon kosher salt

41/2 1 cup oats  //adjusted up to 3/4

3cups=6 sticks 1 c butter/Earth Balance, melted

Directions:

Preheat oven to 350 degrees.  (Peel and) core apples and cut them into large uniform chunks.  [Can keep apples overnight in lemon water.]  Place apples in a bowl and toss with lemon zest, lemon juice, granulated sugar, flour, salt and cinnamon. Transfer to either 5 one-cup ramekins or an 8″ square baking dish.

For the topping:

Combine flour, sugars, salt, oats, and cold (easer: melted) butter. Mix until mixture is well combined and crumbly.

Spread evenly over fruit mixture and pat down slightly.

Place baking dish on a cookie sheet and bake for one hour (allow for an hour 15 mins), until top is browned and fruit is bubbly. (You can also refrigerate, covered, and bake later.

Serve warm with vanilla ice cream.
