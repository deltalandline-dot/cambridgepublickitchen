Sweet Potato Hummus

Sweet Potato Hummus

adapted from Martha Stewart on the web

INGREDIENTS for 4 cups

  • 1 pound sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into 1-inch pieces
    • assuming we are starting from leftover baked ones:  just peel and mash, try about 2 cups
  • 1 can (19.5 ounces) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice (from 1 lemon)
  • 1/4 cup tahini
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons ground cumin
  • 1 garlic clove, chopped
  • Coarse salt and ground pepper
  • Whole-wheat pita and crudites such as red pepper and broccoli
