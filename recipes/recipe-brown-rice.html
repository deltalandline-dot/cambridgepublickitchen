Instructions

~10:30-11

Rinse the rice well using the large double-mesh strainer (may take several batches).  Put the damp rice in the cooker.

About 11:00

Add water to the rice in cooker per above.

Plug in and turn on the cooker.  It will take about 50-60mins to cook, then it switches to warm.  Before serving, check that is has switched to warm.  If so, carefully open the lid and fluff it with a wood or plastic tool.   

Unplug the machine to turn off the “warming”.

Serve with wooden spoons so as not to scratch the container.
