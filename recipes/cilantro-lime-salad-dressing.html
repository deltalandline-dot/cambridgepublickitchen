Cilantro Lime Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Note: taste this and adjust the recipe as needed — it still needs testing.

Yield: ~48 servings per batch (may need to be doubled — see Preparation for how to make a second batch)

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • 2 pitchers
  • Extra 2 qt square container with green lid, for a second batch

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Add all ingredients except the oils in the Vitamix container and mix on low.
  2. If making 2 batches, measure a second batch (except oils) into the square container.
  3. Drizzle oils into the Vitamix while mixing. Gradually increase speed until all emulsified. Divide between 2 small pitchers.
  4. For a second batch: pour the measured container ingredients into the Vitamix and blend. Add the second batch of oils as above. Pour back into the container, label, date, and store in the fridge for later.
