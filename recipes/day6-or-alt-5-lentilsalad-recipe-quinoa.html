Quinoa

Day 6 (or ALT 5)                                          Recipe: Quinoa                                Page 1 of 1

Equipment  rice cooker

Ingredients

serves ~50 with 

Supper leftover

If no supper

Or for 36

9 cups

8c

Quinoa (red, white or mix,whatever we have, all cooks the same)

11 ¼  cups

10c

water (1Q:1.25W)

Instructions

Rinse the quinoa very thoroughly  or it has a bitter taste, using the large double-mesh strainer (may take several batches).  

You can do this early and let it sit in the turned-off rice cooker.

About 11:00:

Add water.

Plug in and turn on the cooker.  It will take about 45mins to cook, then it switches to warm.

Let it sit for a few minutes, then carefully open the lid and fluff it with a wood or plastic tool. 

Unplug the machine to turn off the “warming”.

Serve with wooden spoons so as not to scratch the container.
