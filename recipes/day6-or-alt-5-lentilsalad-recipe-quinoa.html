Quinoa

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~50 servings with a supper leftover batch, or ~36 if no supper leftovers

Equipment

  • Rice cooker

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Rinse the quinoa very thoroughly (or it has a bitter taste) using the large double-mesh strainer (may take several batches). You can do this early and let it sit in the turned-off rice cooker.
  2. (About 11:00) Add water.
  3. Plug in and turn on the cooker. It will take about 45 minutes to cook, then it switches to warm.
  4. Let it sit for a few minutes, then carefully open the lid and fluff it with a wood or plastic tool.
  5. Unplug the machine to turn off the "warming." Serve with wooden spoons so as not to scratch the container.
