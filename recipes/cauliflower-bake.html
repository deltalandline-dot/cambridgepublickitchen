Cauliflower Bake

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

From Hollyhock Cookbook.

Equipment

  • Large skillet
  • 2 hotel pans, 2" deep
  • Container for no-dairy portion (to be reheated on stove top)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Try to do this during the veg chop period: set up a steamer to steam the cauliflower for ~5 minutes (not all the way done).
  2. Perhaps even do this in the veg period: in the large skillet, sauté the onion and leeks in the oil until transparent.
  3. Mix all ingredients except cheese in a very large bowl.
  4. Set aside the no-cheese portion in a smaller put-away container (this will be reheated with some stock on the stove top later). Estimate ~1 cup each for no-dairy people.
  5. Mix cheese into the remaining mixture in the big bowl.
  6. Spray the hotel pans with oil.
  7. Divide between the 2 hotel pans. Sprinkle with a bit of paprika for garnish. Cover & refrigerate.
  8. At night: bake at 350 for 30-40 minutes (put it in cold, into an un-preheated oven, before the evening talk — should be ok for an hour). Mix some bouillon (from a jar) and add enough to moisten the no-cheese part, then heat in a sauce pan on the stove.
