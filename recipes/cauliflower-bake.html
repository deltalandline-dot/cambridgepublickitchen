Cauliflower Bake

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Cauliflower Bake  //for a supper

From Hollyhock Cookbook

Equip: lg skillet, 2  2” deep hotel pans,  container for no-dairy portion (to be reheated on stove top) 

Ingredients

Enough canola Oil to saute the onions (~1/2 cup?)

3qts chopped onions

8 leeks rinsed & chopped

¼ cup crushed garlic

6 coarsely chopped heads of cauliflower

2T dried dill //or ¼ cup fresh if some is around

4 cups rough chopped Kalamata olives (or no more than one jar..)//be sure no pits!

1 cup chopped parsley

4 tsp black pepper

2 tsp salt

8 cups grated cheese (cheddar or feta or swiss/gruyere mix)

12 cups cooked quinoa or rice

Paprika to garnish

Directions

KM: try to do this during veg chop period: Set up a steamer set to steam the cauliflower for ~5 minutes (not all the way done).

KM: perhaps even do this in the veg period: In the large skillet, saute the onion and leeks in the oil until transparent.

Mix all ingredients EXCEPT CHEESE in a very large bowl.

Set aside no-cheese portion in smaller put-away container (this will be reheated with some stock on the stove top later). Est. ~ 1 cup each for no-dairy people.

Mix cheese into remaining mixture in the big bowl.

Spray the hotel pans with oil.

Divide between the 2 hotel pans.  Sprinkle with a bit of paprika for garnish.  Cover & fridge.

AT NIGHT: Bake at 350 for 30-40 minutes (so put in cold into un-preheated oven before talk…should be ok for an hour).    Mix some bouillon (from jar) and add enough to moisten the no-cheese part…heat in a sauce pan on the stove.
