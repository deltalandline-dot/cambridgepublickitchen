Orange Ginger Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~1.5 quarts

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Using the microplane zester or box grater, scrape off just the colored layer of rind from the limes and oranges, trying not to get much of the white (it is more bitter). Be careful with your fingers and knuckles — this tool is very sharp!
  2. Using the electric juicer, juice the limes and oranges.
  3. Add all except the oil to the blender. Blend.
  4. If there's room in the Vitamix, drizzle in the oil as you keep blending. If no room, transfer to a larger container and whisk in the oil.
  5. Taste (drizzle a little into a separate spoon) and adjust salt, pepper, or other seasonings.
  6. For one meal: fill 2 small pitchers. Serve with the smallest ladles.
