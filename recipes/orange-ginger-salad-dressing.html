Orange Ginger Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Orange Ginger Salad Dressing

1.5qts  

  • ~1T zest of 2 limes
  • ~2T zest of 2 oranges
  • ~1-2T juice of the 2 limes
  • 3 c juice about 4 oranges and top off with apple cider vinegar
  • 2T chopped ginger (about a 2” piece…coarse chop it and let the Vitamix chop it fine along     with the rest)
  • 1 ½ T ground cumin (pre-ground is fine)
  • 1 c olive oil
  • 2 ½  c canola oil

Using the microplane zester or box grater, scrape off just the colored layer of rind from the limes and oranges, try not to get much of the white (it is more bitter).  Be careful with your fingers and knuckes, this tool is very sharp!

Using the electric juicer, juice the limes and oranges.

Add all except oil to the blender.  Blend.

If room in the Vitamix, drizzle in the oil as you keep blending. If no room, transfer to a larger container and whisk in the oil

Taste (drizzle a little into a separate spoon) and adjust salt, pepper or other seasonings

For one meal: fill 2 small pitchers.  Serve with smallest ladles.
