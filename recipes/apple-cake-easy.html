Apple Cake Easy

Tags: Vegetarian, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Good with non-diet ingredients, so-so with GF flour, rather weird with vegan egg. Maybe make it non-diet and offer an oat-crumble or baked apples for diets — so not so "easy" if dietary needs are involved.

Yield: scales via 4x (2 full pans, original for a full retreat) / ½x (8x8 pan) / 1x (orig, 9x13 pan)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Quarter and core the apples, then use RoboCoupe to thin-slice the apples.
  2. In a large bowl, beat the sugar, oil, eggs and vanilla until well blended. Combine the flour, baking powder, salt, cinnamon and baking soda; gradually beat into sugar mixture until blended. Fold in apples (and nuts). This is a stiff batter — best to mix using (gloved) hands to get it well-mixed.
  3. Transfer to a greased pan. Bake at 350° for 45-60 minutes or until browning on top & a toothpick inserted near the center comes out clean. Serve with whipped topping.
  4. Make cashew cream (recipe in Other Recipes folder) for non-dairy.
