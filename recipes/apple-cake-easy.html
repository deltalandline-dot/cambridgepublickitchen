“Easy” Spiced Apple Cake

NOTE: this is good with non-diet ingredients, so-so with GF flour, rather weird with vegan-egg.  Maybe make it non-diet and offer an oat-crumble or baked-apples for diets.  So not so “easy” if dietary needs.

 (4x, orig for full retreat)

4x ½x 1x

2 full pans 8x8 Orig: 9x13

  • 3c ½ still too sw 1  (orig: 1-3/4 cups sugar  ??? too much!!)
  • 4c ½ 1 cup vegetable oil
  • 12 1 ½ 3 eggs  // wouldn’t work without eggs, make baked apples for no-egg side?
  • 4t ½ t 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 8c 1c 2 cups all-purpose flour//fine with GF
  • 5 t ½ + 1/8 tsp 1-1/4 teaspoons baking powder
  • 4t ½ t 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2t 1   t ½  t Cinnamon
  • 1t ½  t ¼  t Cardamom
  • 1t ½  t ¼ t Nutmeg
  • 1t 1/8 t 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
  • 12-20 2-3 3-5 Granny Smith apples (quantity depends on size of apples)
  • (4 ½ 1 cup chopped walnuts or pecans—OPTIONAL)
  • 3 pints 1-1/4 cups whipped topping

DIRECTIONS

Quarter and core the apples, then use RoboCoupe to thin-slice the apples.

In a large bowl, beat the sugar, oil, eggs and vanilla until well blended. Combine the flour, baking powder, salt, cinnamon and baking soda; gradually beat into sugar mixture until blended. Fold in apples [and nuts]. This is a stiff batter, best to do this using (gloved) hands to get it well-mixed.

Transfer to a greased pan.. Bake at 350° for 45-60 minutes or until browning on top & a toothpick inserted near the center comes out clean. Serve with whipped topping.  

Make Cashew cream (recipe in Other Recipes folder) for non-dairy.
