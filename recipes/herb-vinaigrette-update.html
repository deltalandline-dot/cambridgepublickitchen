Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Herb Vinaigrette (also used for “Tarragon” in menus)

~1 ½ qts ~3qts (for 2 meals)

  • 1 ½  c 3c Rice vinegar
  • ¼ c ½ cup honey
  • ¼  c ½ cup Dijon mustard
  • 6 cl 12 cloves garlic
  • ~¾   c ~1 ½  cups fresh herbs
    • Note: for our “Herbal” use 1 bunch basil, ½ bunch parsley
    • For our “Tarragon” use 1 clamshell of tarragon
  • 4 c 8 c Canola oil
  • ½ c 1 c water
  • Salt  to taste

Rinse the herbs & pick the leaves off the stems.

Poach the whole peeled garlic cloves in one cup of the oil (one cup out of the amt. called for) in the small saucepan or skillet on medium heat for ~5 minutes to make them more mild.

Add all except the rest of the oil to the blender (poached garlic cloves and their oil can be added unchopped).  Blend.

If room in the Vitamix, drizzle in the oil as you keep blending. If no room, transfer to a larger container and whisk in the oil or use the emersion blender.

Taste (drizzle a little into a separate spoon) and adjust salt, pepper or other seasonings.

For one meal: fill 2 small pitchers.  Serve with smallest ladles.
