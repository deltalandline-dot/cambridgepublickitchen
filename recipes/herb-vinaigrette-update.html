Herb Vinaigrette--update

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Note: this recipe is also used for "Tarragon" on menus (see herb substitution below).

Yield: ~1½ qts (or ~3 qts for 2 meals)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Rinse the herbs & pick the leaves off the stems.
  2. Poach the whole peeled garlic cloves in one cup of the oil (one cup out of the amount called for) in a small saucepan or skillet on medium heat for ~5 minutes to make them more mild.
  3. Add all except the rest of the oil to the blender (poached garlic cloves and their oil can be added unchopped). Blend.
  4. If room in the Vitamix, drizzle in the oil as you keep blending. If no room, transfer to a larger container and whisk in the oil or use the immersion blender.
  5. Taste (drizzle a little into a separate spoon) and adjust salt, pepper or other seasonings.
  6. For one meal: fill 2 small pitchers. Serve with smallest ladles.
