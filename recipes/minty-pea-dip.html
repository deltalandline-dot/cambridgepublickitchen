Minty Pea Dip

Minty Pea Dip/Spread

From Martha Stewart website via Lael

Vegan, nut free

For full    For ~36   Orig

12            9                 3  cups frozen peas

1             ¾                 ¼ cup mint leaves

4             3                   1 lemon, zest and juice of

4             3                   1 clove garlic

8             6                   2 Tbls olive oil

4             3                   1 Tbls tahini

To taste           salt & pepper

Blanch peas in salted boiling water <~1 minute, cool & drain.

Pulse peas in Robo Coupe with all other ingredients.

Season to taste with salt & pepper.

Serve, maybe garnish with drizzle of olive oil, mint sprig.
