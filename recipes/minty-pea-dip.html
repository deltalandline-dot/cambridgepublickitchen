Minty Pea Dip

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Adapted from a Martha Stewart recipe, via Lael. Vegan, nut free.

Yield: ~36 servings for the mid-size batch (the ingredient list also gives quantities for a larger "full" batch and for the original single batch).

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Blanch peas in salted boiling water for <~1 minute, then cool & drain.
  2. Pulse the peas in the Robo Coupe with all other ingredients.
  3. Season to taste with salt & pepper.
  4. Serve, maybe garnished with a drizzle of olive oil and a mint sprig.
