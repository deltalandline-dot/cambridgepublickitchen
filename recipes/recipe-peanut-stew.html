Peanut Stew

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings with supper leftovers (or ~36 servings with supper leftovers)

Equipment

  • Rondo

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Note: the stew will thicken once kale is added.
  2. Cook chickpeas: These should have been soaked overnight or "quick soaked" earlier. Drain soaking water, rinse, cover with fresh water. Add ~3 bay leaves and bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Let them cook while you get the rest of the stew ready, checking occasionally for doneness.
  3. Sauté: Sauté onion until translucent. Add garlic & ginger and sauté another minute.
  4. Add remaining ingredients in steps: Add yams, tomato, peanut butter, cayenne, and veggie broth (as needed).
  5. Bring to a boil and lower to a simmer. Cook until sweet potatoes are tender.
  6. (~11:15) Add zucchini and drained chickpeas.
  7. (~11:30) Add kale and half the whole peanuts. Check seasoning.
  8. Garnish with the rest of the chopped (or whole) peanuts to serve.
