Instructions

Note: will thicken once kale is added.

Cook

Chickpeas

These should have been soaked overnight or “quick soaked” by KM earlier.  Drain soaking water, rinse, cover with fresh water.  Add ~3 bay leaves & bring to a boil, reduce to simmer.  Let them cook while you get the rest of the stew ready, checking occasionally for done.

Saute 

Saute onion until translucent.  Add garlic & ginger and saute another minute.

Add remaining ingredients in steps

Add yams, tomato, peanut butter, cayenne, & veggie broth (as needed).  

Bring to boil and lower to a simmer. Cook until sweet potatoes are tender. 

11:15 add zucchini and drained chickpeas

About 11:30, add kale & half the whole peanuts.  Check seasoning.

Garnish with rest of chopped (or whole) peanuts to serve.
