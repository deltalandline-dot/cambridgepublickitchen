Instructions

Cook Lentils

Rinse the lentils, put lentils, water, salt and bay leaves in a stock pot.  

Bring to a boil and lower to an active simmer.

Stir occasionally and add water if it gets low.  

Simmer about 20-30 minutes until lentils are cooked but not mushy. 

When the lentils are done, strain them in a colander and remove the bay leaves if you can find them.

Make Leeks and Sauce

Sauté the leeks in the olive oil until soft, using the large skillet or the rondo.

Make a sauce of the mustard and sherry vinegar and a bit more olive oil and mix (note: we now add this to the warm lentils, not sauteed with the leeks).

Mix Salad

Mix the lentils, mustard sauce, grapes, mint, parsley and sauteed leeks in a large metal or plastic bowl.   

Just before serving: **Check nut allergies, maybe set aside a no-nut portion.    Add nuts to main portion.

Serve in a large plastic or metal bowl.                                         

Serve with crumbled feta cheese on the side if there is enough.
