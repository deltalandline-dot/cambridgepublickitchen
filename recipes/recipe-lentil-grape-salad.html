Lentil Grape Salad

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~50 servings with a supper-leftovers reserve (or the smaller amounts in parentheses below for either 50 servings with no supper reserve, or 36 servings if also serving supper)

Equipment

  • 12 qt stock pot
  • Large skillet
  • Large metal mixing/serving bowl

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Cook lentils: Rinse the lentils; put the lentils, water, salt, and bay leaves in a stock pot. Bring to a boil and lower to an active simmer. Stir occasionally and add water if it gets low. Simmer about 20-30 minutes until the lentils are cooked but not mushy. When the lentils are done, strain them in a colander and remove the bay leaves if you can find them.
  2. Make leeks and sauce: Sauté the leeks in the olive oil until soft, using the large skillet or the rondo. Make a sauce of the mustard, sherry vinegar, and a bit more olive oil, and mix (note: we now add this to the warm lentils, not sautéed with the leeks).
  3. Mix salad: Mix the lentils, mustard sauce, grapes, mint, parsley, and sautéed leeks in a large metal or plastic bowl. Just before serving: check for nut allergies, maybe set aside a no-nut portion, then add the nuts to the main portion. Serve in a large plastic or metal bowl, with crumbled feta cheese on the side if there's enough.
