Vegan (GF) Banana Choc Chip Cupcakes

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

From Vegan Cupcakes Take Over the World.

Yield: ~48 cupcakes (4 dozen) — fine made all gluten-free, or make 4 dozen regular plus 1 dozen gluten-free. Recipe also scales down to 1 dozen or up to 5 dozen (see ingredient notes).

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Preheat oven to 350.
  2. Line 4 muffin tins with paper cupcake liners.
  3. Mash bananas until quite smooth.
  4. Sift/mix flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt and sugar in a large bowl and mix (or just mix — we never sift).
  5. In a separate bowl, whisk together the oil, rice milk, vanilla, (almond), and mashed banana.
  6. Fold wet ingredients into dry ingredients, mixing to just combine (don't over mix).
  7. Fold in chocolate chips.
  8. Fill liners ⅔ full. You could add a couple of choc chips to top each one.
  9. Bake for 20-22 minutes (maybe more in our right oven) until inserted knife or toothpick comes out clean.
  10. Cool completely on wire rack. (If not for today, store in a lidded ant-proof container!)
  11. Optional Chocolate Icing: Place sugar and cinnamon in a small bowl. Add the melted Earth Balance, soy milk, and vanilla and stir with a fork until smooth. Let it come back to room temp to judge consistency — the mixture should look opaque; if it looks too liquid, add a little extra powdered sugar. Best kept at room temp unless overnight, then refrigerate and bring back to room temp before using. Pipe on with a sandwich-type plastic bag with one tip-end cut off a small bit. Enough for a squiggle on each of 5 dozen.
