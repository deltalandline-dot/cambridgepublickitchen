Vegan (GF) Banana Choc Chip Cupcakes

Vegan (can be GF) Banana Chocolate Chip Cupcakes

From Vegan Cupcakes Take Over the World

Ingredients for 4 dozen = 48..these are fine made all GF.  or you can make 4dz reg plus 1 dz GF.

1dz   5 dz 4 dz

½ c 2 ½ c 2 cups mashed very ripe bananas

1 ¼ c 6 ¼ c 5 cups flour (all-purpose or Bob’s 1-1 Gluten Free)

¼ t 1 ¼ t 1 tsp baking soda

1t 5 t 4 tsp baking powder

½ t 2 ½ t 2 tsp salt

¾ c 3 ¾ c 3 cups granulated (regular white) sugar

¼ c+1 ½ T 1 ⅔ c 1 ⅓ cup canola oil

½ c+3T 3 ⅓ c 2 ⅔ cups rice milk

2t 8 t 6 tsp vanilla extract

½ t (2 tsp almond extract...don’t think we have missed skipping this, but good with!)

¾ c 3 ¾ c 3 cups dark or semi-sweet chocolate chips

  • Preheat oven to 350
  • Line 4 muffin tins with paper cupcake liners.
  • Mash bananas until quite smooth.
  • Sift/mix flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt and sugar in a large bowl and mix. (or just mix..we never sift).
  • In a separate bowl, whisk together the oil, rice milk, vanilla, (almond), and mashed banana.  
  • Fold wet ingredients into dry ingredients, mixing to just combine (don’t over mix).
  • Fold in chocolate chips.
  • Fill liners ⅔ full.  You could add a couple of choc chips to top each one.
  • Bake for 20-22 minutes (maybe more in our right oven)...until inserted knife or toothpick comes out clean.
  • Cool completely on wire rack.  (If not for today, store in lidded ant-proof container!)

Optional Chocolate Icing:

2 ½  cups confectioners’ (aka powdered) sugar 

1 ½ tablespoons cocoa

½  teaspoons ground cinnamon 

7 tablespoons Earth Balance, melted (~40 secs in microwave)

3 tablespoons soy or rice milk  //check diets

1 ½  teaspoons vanilla extract 

DIRECTIONS 1. Place sugar and cinnamon in a small bowl. Add the melted Earth Bal, soy milk, and vanilla and stir with a fork until smooth. Let it come back to room temp to judge consistency. The mixture should look opaque.   If looks too liquid, add a little extra powdered sugar. Best kept at room temp unless overnight, then fridge and bring back to room temp. Pipe on with a sandwich type plastic bag with one tip-end cut off a small bit.  Enough for a squiggle on each of 5dz.
