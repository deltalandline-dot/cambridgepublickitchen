Split Pea Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48-52 servings

Equipment

  • Largest 32 qt soup pot

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Sauté: Sauté onions over medium heat until softened.
  2. Add carrots and celery, and sauté over medium heat (~30 mins).
  3. Add turmeric, cayenne, marjoram, smoked paprika, and salt. Sauté ~5 more minutes.
  4. Mix 2 qts hot water (use tea water) with a jar of Better Than Bouillon vegan "chicken" broth.
  5. Add the peas, the bouillon mix, and the other 9 qts of water.
  6. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer. Simmer an hour or more until the peas are soft and creamy. Taste and adjust seasonings as needed.
  7. If needed, puree partially with an immersion blender (leave some chunks of carrot and celery).
  8. Around 12:00: leave the soup at ~180°F with the burner turned off — it should stay hot.
  9. Finish (~5:15): Stir in the lemon juice and parsley, and adjust seasonings (especially — probably add more salt).

Adapted from Vegetarian Times, December 2013.
