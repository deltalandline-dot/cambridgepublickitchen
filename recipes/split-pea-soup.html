Split Pea Soup

Supper Recipe:  Split Pea Soup     P 1 of 2

Equipment:  largest 32qt soup pot

Ingredients

For ~48-52

For saute

Olive oil

8 cups

Onions, chopped

7 cups

Celery diced (~½” dice)

9 cups

Carrots, diced (~½ “ dice)

2 tsp

salt

4 

Bay leaves

1 ½ T

Smoked paprika (maybe more later, but start easy on this)

3 T

Marjoram

3 T

Turmeric

2 T

Ground Cumin

¾ tsp

Cayenne pepper (maybe more later, but start easy on this)

12 cups

Yellow or green split peas, rinsed

11 quarts

Water (mixed with a jar of Better Than Bouillon)

1 ¼ cup

Lemon juice

1 bunch

Parsley, chopped

Opening Supper Recipe:  Yellow Split Pea Soup      P 2 of 2

Instructions

Saute 

Saute onions over medium heat until softened

Add carrots & celery, saute over medium heat (~30 mins)

Add tumeric, cayenne, marjoram, smoked paprika, salt

Saute ~5 more minutes

Add peas & broth

Mix 2 qts hot water (use tea water) with a jar of Better than Bouillon vegan “chicken” broth

Add peas, bouillon mix and the other 9qts of water 

Bring to boil, reduce to simmer.

Simmer an hour or more until peas are soft & creamy.

Taste and maybe adjust seasonings.

If needed: puree partially with immersion blender (leave some chunks of carrots/celery).

12:00

Leave soup @ ~180 but burner turned off, should stay hot.

5:15 Finish

Stir in lemon juice and parsley and adjust seasonings (esp. probably add more salt).

Adapted from Vegetarian Times December 2013
