Lemon Tahini Dressing

Updated by Chris 7/30/2015 for clarity, quantity adjustment

~1.5 qts

  • Gather all ingredients below and one Vitamix container.
  • If needed, double the recipe using two Vitamix containers. 

Ingredients for ONE Vitamix container (do this twice if making extra)

1 cup lemon juice (Juice from about 4-6 fresh lemons)

¾ Cup parsley 

5 Cloves garlic

2 ½ Cups water

1 ½ Tablespoons tamari

2 teaspoons ground cumin

½ teaspoon white pepper 

1 whole tin of Joyva Tahini (=~2c)

½ cup canola oil

  • Using electric juicer, juice the lemons.  
  • Wash parsley and cut off stems (some of the upper stems are OK).
  • In a medium sized bowl, use a hand whisk to mix lemon juice, tahini and 1 cup of the water so that tahini is not too clumpy (or else it will stall the Vitamix).
  • Then blend in the Vitamix with rest of ingredients.  
  • Fill two small pitchers if serving today, else put in a 1qt (smallest square) container.
