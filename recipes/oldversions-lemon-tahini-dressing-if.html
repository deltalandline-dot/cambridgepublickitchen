Lemon Tahini Dressing-IF

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~1.5 qts (quantities below are for one Vitamix container — double the recipe using two containers if making extra)

Equipment

  • Vitamix container (a second one if doubling)

Ingredients (for ONE Vitamix container — do this twice if making extra)

Preparation

  1. Gather all ingredients and one Vitamix container (double the recipe using two Vitamix containers if needed).
  2. Using an electric juicer, juice the lemons.
  3. Wash the parsley and cut off the stems (some of the upper stems are fine to leave on).
  4. In a medium sized bowl, use a hand whisk to mix the lemon juice, tahini, and 1 cup of the water so the tahini isn't too clumpy (otherwise it will stall the Vitamix).
  5. Then blend in the Vitamix with the rest of the ingredients.
  6. Fill two small pitchers if serving today, otherwise put it in a 1 qt (smallest square) container.
