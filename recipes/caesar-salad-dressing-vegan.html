Caesar Salad Dressing (Vegan)

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings per batch

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • Juicer
  • 2 pitchers

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Soak cashews covered with water (ideally KM would do this early).
  2. Juice lemons with juicer.
  3. Add all except salt & pepper in vitamix container and mix on low.
  4. Add salt & pepper to taste.
  5. Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
