Caesar Salad Dressing (Vegan)

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Recipe: Vegan Caesar Salad Dressing                                       

Equipment  Vitamix, juicer, 2 pitchers

Ingredients

For 1 batch 

(~48 servs)

3 ½ cups

Raw cashews, soaked (could be overnight, at least 2 hrs)

1 ¾  cup

water

¾ cup + 2T

EV Olive oil

7 T

Lemon juice

3 ½ T

Dijon mustard

3 ½ tsp

Garlic powder

6

Small cloves of garlic

3 ½ T 

Vegan Worcestershire sauce (Wizard’s...might be some in top of fridge)

4 Tbls + 2 tsp

Capers

~1 tsp each

Salt & pepper (adjust to taste)

Instructions

Soak cashews covered with water (ideally KM would do this early).

Juice lemons with juicer.

Add all except salt & pepper  in vitamix container and mix on low.

Add salt & pepper to taste

Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
