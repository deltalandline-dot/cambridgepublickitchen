Herbal Dressing

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: recipe as written makes one container's worth; make it twice (once in the Vitamix, once in a second container) for a full doubled batch — about 1.5 qts per container.

Equipment

  • Vitamix container
  • A second 2-quart (smallest square) container with lid

Ingredients (for ONE container — you'll put this much in each of the Vitamix and the side container)

Preparation

  1. Gather all ingredients, the Vitamix container, and a second 2-quart (smallest square) container with a lid.
  2. This recipe is doubled: put a full set of the ingredients in each container — it's easiest to add each ingredient to both containers, then move to the next ingredient.
  3. Rinse the herbs (no need to chop; the Vitamix will do it).
  4. Add the ingredient list above to each container.
  5. Blend the contents of the Vitamix container.
  6. Fill 2 small pitchers with it.
  7. Pour the second set of ingredients into the Vitamix container and mix it.
  8. Pour the mixed dressing back into the mixing container, cover, label, and put in the fridge.
