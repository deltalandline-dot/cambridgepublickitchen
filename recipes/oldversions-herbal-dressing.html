Herbal Dressing

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Herbal Vinaigrette (instructions for double recipe)

Updated by Chris 7/30/2015 for a bit milder & less

  • Gather all ingredients below, the Vitamix container and a second 2 quart (smallest square) container with lid.
  • You will double this recipe by putting a set of the below ingredients in each container.  It’s easiest to add each ingredient to both containers, then go to the next ingredient.

Ingredients for ONE Container (you will put this much in each of the vitamix and the side container)

1 ½  cups Rice Vinegar

1 ½  cups water

1 tsp garlic powder (on spice shelf far left) 

¼ cup Honey (tip: coat cup with oil, honey slides right out)

¼ cup Dijon Mustard

½ tsp salt

½ bunch fresh basil leaves (pick off from stems)

(~½ cup packed down)

½  bunch fresh parsley (cut off main trunk of stems, ok to use rest) 

(~½ cup packed down)

3 ½   cups Canola Oil

  • Rinse the herbs (no need to chop, Vitamix will do it).
  • Add the above ingredients list to each container.
  • Blend the contents of the Vitamix container using the Vitamix.
  • Fill 2 small pitchers with it.  
  • Pour second set of ingredients into the Vitamix container and mix it.
  • Pour the mixed dressing back in the mixing container, cover, label and put in the fridge.
