Instructions

Turn on oven to 400 degrees

Mix oil coriander, cumin, curry powder, turmeric and ground ginger.

In the largest bowl, in two batches, add the cauliflower and coat in spice mixture

Lay cauliflower evenly on baking sheets

Cook about 45 minutes starting at 11:15

About 11:45, add the peas and cook another 10-15 minutes or until cauliflower is tender 

Remove from oven, combine trays into large serving bowl and sprinkle with grated lime (or Amchur) and fresh ginger. Squeeze a little lime juice to taste with salt and pepper if you wish.
