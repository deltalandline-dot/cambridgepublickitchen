Cauliflower Recipe

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Note: some KMs prefer to skip this recipe and just steam or roast plain cauliflower — consult the KM. If steaming, start about 11:30; if roasting, about 11:15.

Equipment

  • ~3 large baking sheets (as needed for single-layer)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Turn on oven to 400 degrees.
  2. Mix oil, coriander, cumin, curry powder, turmeric and ground ginger.
  3. In the largest bowl, in two batches, add the cauliflower and coat in the spice mixture.
  4. Lay cauliflower evenly on baking sheets.
  5. Cook about 45 minutes, starting at 11:15.
  6. (~11:45) Add the peas and cook another 10-15 minutes, or until cauliflower is tender.
  7. Remove from oven, combine trays into a large serving bowl, and sprinkle with grated lime (or Amchur) and fresh ginger. Squeeze a little lime juice to taste with salt and pepper, if you wish.
