Kale with Mushrooms

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: 48 servings (lunch only)

Equipment

  • Roasting sheet
  • 2 steamer pan setups

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. First thing in the morning (before you need the oven for the tofu): preheat oven to 350°.
  2. Toss the sliced mushrooms in olive oil and spread on a baking sheet. Roast for ~15-30 minutes at 350° (time depends on how thick they're sliced). Check every 10 minutes, stir, and swap pans on oven shelves as needed. Set aside when done — i.e., when they look appealing enough to eat! (Or: some prefer to sauté them in the largest skillet — check the overhead rack or apron area for large skillets.)
  3. (~11:00) Steam the kale using 2 steamer setups — it will probably take two rounds of 2 steamers each, each about 10 minutes. (Or: some prefer to sauté the kale in the rondo, the big round pan under the rice cooker.) Transfer the cooked kale, layering with the roasted mushrooms, into a 4" deep hotel pan to serve. Cover to keep warm.
