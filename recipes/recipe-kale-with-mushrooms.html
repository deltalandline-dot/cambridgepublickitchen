Kale with Mushrooms

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Day 1                                       Recipe: Kale with Mushrooms                          Page 1 of 1                                

Equipment  roasting sheet, 2 steamer pan setups

Ingredients

#48 (lunch only)

~⅓ cup?

olive oil, enough to lightly coat sliced mushrooms

1 case + 6 heads dino kale

Dino Kale, de-stemmed and torn each leaf-side into 3-4 pieces

4lbs

sliced mushrooms

Instructions

First thing in the morning (ie before you need the oven for the tofu): 

Pre-heat oven to 350

Toss sliced mushrooms in olive oil, spread on baking sheet.  Roast for ~15-30 minutes at 350.   Time depends on how thick they were sliced.  Check every 10 mins, stir, swap pans on oven shelves as needed.

Set aside when done (= appealing to eat in your opinion!)

OR: some prefer to saute them in the largest skillet (check overhead rack or in apron area for large skillets0

~11:00

About 11:00: Steam kale using 2 steamer setups.   It will probably take two rounds of 2 steamers each.   Each one takes about 10 minutes to cook the kale.   

OR: some prefer to saute the kale in the rondo (big round pan under the rice cooker)

Transfer cooked kale layering with roasted mushrooms into a 4” deep hotel pan to serve.  Cover to keep warm.
