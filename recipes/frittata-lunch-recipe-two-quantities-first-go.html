Instructions

Cook Veggies

Preheat oven to 425 degrees. Heat rondo on medium heat and add oil to pan. Once hot add mushrooms allowing to brown on each side. 

Once mushrooms are browned add onion and cook until tender. 

Then add garlic for and cook for a min or two until fragrant and add greens until wilted.  

Remove approximately ⅓ of veggie mixture for bean salad.

Constructing Frittata

While veggies are cooking, mix eggs, half and half, parmesan cheese and scallions.  

Add veggie mixture to egg mixture and place in 2 full hotel pans and sprinkle with gruyere cheese.

Place in oven at 11 am and cook for 45-60 min or until frittata is firm in center. Remove from oven and cut into 24 portions per pan.

Add 6-29? ounce cans cannellini beans to veggies. Taste to ensure flavor.
