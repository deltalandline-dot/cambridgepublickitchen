Frittata Lunch Recipe Two Quantities First go

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: 2 full hotel pans, cut into 24 portions each (48 portions total); the equipment list also mentions a half hotel pan, but it isn't referenced in the steps below.

Equipment

  • Rondo
  • Two full 2" hotel pans
  • One half 2" hotel pan

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Cook Veggies: Preheat oven to 425°F. Heat the rondo on medium heat and add oil to the pan. Once hot, add mushrooms, allowing them to brown on each side.
  2. Once mushrooms are browned, add onion and cook until tender.
  3. Then add garlic and cook for a minute or two until fragrant, and add greens until wilted.
  4. Remove approximately ⅓ of the veggie mixture for bean salad.
  5. Constructing Frittata: While veggies are cooking, mix eggs, half and half, Parmesan cheese, and scallions.
  6. Add veggie mixture to egg mixture and place in 2 full hotel pans; sprinkle with gruyere cheese.
  7. Place in oven at 11 am and cook for 45–60 minutes or until frittata is firm in center. Remove from oven and cut into 24 portions per pan.
  8. For bean salad: Add 6–29? ounce cans cannellini beans to veggies. Taste to ensure flavor.
