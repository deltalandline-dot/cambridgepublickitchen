Equipment  if supper: 3 full pans.  Serve the 2 and save the other for supper.  If no supper, just 2 full size.  Use the 4” (medium depth) so it doesn’t slosh when putting in the oven.

Ingredients:  Note: simplified to be all Vegan no-cheese, serve cheese on the side for the whole meal.

NOTE: If making 3 pans:  We only have 2 4” deep full pans, so use 2 4” half pans or a 6” deep one for the third and save the 3rd for supper leftover.

Instructions (see p2 for stove-top version instructions)

Mix and bake Polenta

Preheat oven to 425.  

Spray pans with spray oil.

Mix polenta and seasonings in each of the pans you are using.

LEAVE PANS UNCOVERED and put them in the oven about 10:15.  Set a timer for an hour.

Stir & Maybe Cover

About 11:15, carefully with hot pad gloves (and maybe with help) remove the pans and stir.

Peek every 20 minutes or so.  If top is getting too brown, put the lid on.  If done, turn off ovens and leave pans inside to stay warm.              

Maybe stir in some earth balance at the end.

Day 3                                                     ALTERNATE:  STOVETOP Polenta                           Page 2 of 2                              

Alternate versions preferred by some cooks (from Lael)

This stovetop version is good and does free up the oven so that you can pre-roast more of the ratatouille veggies, which improves that dish.  But this takes some attention.

  • Bring 10 quarts water to boil (get this going early, ~10:45?).  BE SURE A HEAT DEFLECTOR IS UNDER THE POT!
  • Add ⅓ cup salt
  • About 11:20:  slowly pour  10 cups Polenta into pot whisking constantly to avoid lumps.  Keep whisking while it thickens.
  • When thickened, turn down flame to a bare simmer.
  • Stir every 10 minus for 40 minutes.   This long simmer brings out the sweet corn flavor.
  • Add more boiling (tea machine) water if too stiff.
  • Stir in some EarthBalance (any brand vegan butter) to taste.

Note on possible use of leftovers for the next night supper: if there is quite a bit left (~4 qts?), best to do this right after lunch, during the put-away period):

  • Grease sheet pan with olive oil.
  • Spread the polenta on the sheet pan to ~½-¾” thick.   ¾ of a sheet pan is enough.
  • Sprinkle with basil and vegan parmesan and put the sheet in the fridge (maybe with foil or another sheet pan covering it).
  • At 5:00 on supper day:  put in a 400 oven for the half-hour.   Cut into ~7x8 pieces.

You could probably do this with oven polenta but it’s harder to spread.
