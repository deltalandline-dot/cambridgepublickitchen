Barley Vegetable Soup with White Bean

Tags: Vegan, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Adapted from a recipe on Allrecipes.com — noted here as modified since Allrecipes marks their recipes all-rights-reserved; this is what was actually made.

Yield: for 16

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot (12-qt).
  2. Add onion and garlic, and sauté for a few minutes.
  3. Add carrots, celery, zucchini, vegetable broth, barley, tomatoes, and bay leaves.
  4. Season with salt, pepper, parsley, curry powder, smoked paprika, and vinegar.
  5. Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer over medium-low heat for 60-75 minutes.
  6. Add the beans after about 45 minutes. (Note: adding them at the start along with everything else made them break down too much — better to add them later.)
  7. Remove bay leaves before serving.
