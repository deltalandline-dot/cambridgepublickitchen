Barley Vegetable Soup with White Bean

Barley-Vegetable Soup with White Bean

For 16

It is from Allrecipes.com and says “all rights reserved”, although I modified it – below is what I actually did. Just so you’re aware if using it at IRC…

Ingredients

  • 3 T olive oil
  • 4 quarts vegetable broth 
  • 1 red onion, chopped 
  • 5 small cloves garlic, finely diced
  • 1-3/4 cup uncooked barley 
  • 4 large carrots, chopped 
  • 6 stalks celery, chopped 
  • 2 zucchini, chopped 
  • 1 28-oz can diced tomatoes with juice 
  • 6 bay leaves 
  • 2 teaspoon salt 
  • 1 teaspoon ground black pepper 
  • 2 teaspoon dried parsley 
  • 2 teaspoon curry powder 
  • 2 teaspoon smoked paprika 
  • 2 teaspoon red wine vinegar 
  • 2 (15 ounce) cans white beans (cannellini), drained 

Directions

Heat the olive oil in a large pot (12-qt). Add online and garlic, and saute for a few minutes. Add carrots, celery, zucchini, vegetable broth, barley, tomatoes, and bay leaves. Season with salt, pepper, parsley, curry powder, smoked paprika, and vinegar. Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer over medium-low heat for 60-75 minutes. Add beans after about 45 minutes (*). Remove bay leaves before serving.

(*) I added them with all the other ingredients, but found that they broke down a bit too much. I think adding them later would be better.
