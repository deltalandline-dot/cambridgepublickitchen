Red Lentil Dal

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Equipment

  • Largest stockpot

Note: to ease this day, you may want to prepare the spice mix during the work period or even sometime the day before.

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Start lentils simmering: Rinse the lentils in the strainer under running water. They tend to clump, so separate with your fingers — this may take several batches in the strainer.
  2. Put lentils & water in the stock pot. Bring to a boil and skim off foam.
  3. After foam is skimmed off, lower heat and add the chopped ginger and cubed yams.
  4. Reduce to an active simmer. Check frequently that it is still actively simmering, but not over-boiling, and stir often all the way to the bottom. Always put a heat deflector under this pot. To stir, you may want to stand on a stool and use the big paddle. If it seems to be getting too thick, you can add more water — it should be thick, but still pourable.
  5. While it simmers, prepare the yams, spices & garlic: Check if there are leftover yams, peel and mash if not already done, or get out a can of pumpkin puree (or skip this if none).
  6. Chop the garlic in the mini-cuisinart.
  7. Toast the cumin, coriander, and fenugreek. Use the small skillet, keep the heat low, and wait for it to start to be fragrant and a little brown, but not very (and not burned!).
  8. Grind them (you may grind together) in the spice grinder.
  9. Add turmeric and brown mustard seeds.
  10. Set aside 4 T of the mix to add to the cauliflower.
  11. Stir and check the lentils frequently — this dish is easy to burn!
  12. Sauté onions & spices: Use the large skillet, coat it lightly (~1/8") with canola oil.
  13. Sauté the onions over med/low heat until soft.
  14. Add the garlic and sauté another minute.
  15. Add the spice mix and cayenne and continue to sauté over low heat for 1-2 minutes.
  16. Set aside if it's not yet time to add to the dal.
  17. Stir and check the lentils frequently — this dish is easy to burn!
  18. Blend the lentils, add the remaining ingredients: When the lentils are soft (maybe ~11:15), add the yams or pumpkin.
  19. Using the immersion blender, blend the dal to a smooth consistency, being careful not to submerge the blender beyond the stem, and using a spoon to stir up the dal from the bottom.
  20. Add the onion/spice mixture to the dal, stirring well. Cook for about 5 minutes and then add the lemon juice & maybe zest to taste, stirring well.
  21. Salt to taste.
  22. When it's ready, you can put a lid on it and leave it turned off — it will stay warm.
