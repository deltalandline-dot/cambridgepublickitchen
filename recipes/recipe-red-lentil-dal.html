Red Lentil Dal

Recipe: Red Lentil Dal

Instructions

Start Lentils Simmering

Rinse the lentils in the strainer under running water.  Tends to clump, separate with your fingers.  This may take several batches in the strainer.

Put lentils & water in the stock pot.  Bring to a boil and skim off foam.  

After foam is skimmed off, lower heat, add chopped ginger and cubed yams.

Reduce to an active simmer.  Check frequently that it is still actively simmering, but not over-boiling and stir often all the way to the bottom.  ALWAYS PUT A HEAT-DEFLECTOR UNDER THIS POT. To stir, you may want to stand on a stool and use the big paddle.  If it seems to be getting too thick, you can add more water.  It should be thick, but still pourable.

While it simmers, prepare the yams, spices & garlic

Check if there are leftover yams, peel and mash if not already done, or get out a can of pumpkin puree (or skip this if none).

Chop the garlic in the mini-cuisinart.

Toast the cumin, coriander and fenugreek.  Use the small skillet, keep the heat low and wait for it to start to be fragrant and a little brown, but not very (& not burned!).

Grind them (you may grind together) in the spice grinder.

Add tumeric and brown mustard seeds

Set aside 4 T of the mix to add to the cauliflower.

STIR AND CHECK LENTILS FREQUENTLY, THIS DISH IS EASY TO BURN!

Instructions Continued...

Sauté onions & spices

Use the large skillet, coat it lightly (~ 1/8”) with canola oil.  

Sauté the onions over med/low heat until soft.

Add the garlic and sauté another minute. 

Add the spice mix and cayenne and continue to sauté over low heat for 1-2 minutes.  

Set aside if not time to add to the dal.

STIR AND CHECK LENTILS FREQUENTLY, THIS DISH IS EASY TO BURN!

Blend the lentils, add the remaining ingredients

When the lentils are soft (maybe ~11:15),  add the yams or pumpkin.

Using the immersion blender, blend the dal to a smooth consistency, being careful not to submerge the blender beyond the stem, and using a spoon to stir up the dal from the bottom.

Add the onion/spice mixture to the dal, stirring well.  Cook for about 5 minutes and then add the lemon juice & maybe 

zest to taste, stirring well.

Salt to taste.

When it’s ready you can put a lid on it and leave it turned off, it will stay warm.
