Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Avocado Dressing

Updated by Chris 7/30/2015 to remove yogurt so its vegan

1 ½  qts :

½ cup water

1 ¼  cup lime/lemon juice (~4  limes, rest lemons)

4 lg avocadoes

1/3 c. cilantro

½ tsp garlic powder 

½ tsp cumin

½ tsp white pepper

½ tsp salt

3 ¾ cups canola oil

Add all except oil to the blender.  Blend.

Drizzle in the oil while mixing (or just pour it all in & mix, works fine..)

For one meal: fill 2 small pitchers.
