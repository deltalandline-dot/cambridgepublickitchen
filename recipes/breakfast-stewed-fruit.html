Breakfast Stewed Fruit

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Setup the prunes to soak overnight: get the smallest short round 2-handled pot with lid.
  2. Wash and thinly slice 1 orange, including the peel, into the pot (slice it in half lengthwise, then in quarters lengthwise, then very thinly, so you get thin quarter-moon slices). Oranges are usually in the back room middle fridge.
  3. Wash and cut up a fresh apple and add it to the mix. Apples are usually in the bottom of the kitchen pantry.
  4. It's also a good use for any pieces of apple or orange left on the public cutting board (you could collect these in the evening and put them in the fridge for this purpose).
  5. Add ~7 cups prunes (or one Costco bag).
  6. Add one 3" cinnamon stick from the spice jars.
  7. Fill with water to cover all the fruit plus about 1 inch of water.
  8. Put the lid on, put the pot in the left side of the kitchen fridge wherever you can find room. Leave it to soak overnight.
  9. In the morning, the breakfast cook brings it to a boil and simmers for a few minutes while the oats cook.
  10. Can add to it and reheat for up to 3 days in a row, then start over.
