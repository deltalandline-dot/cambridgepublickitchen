Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Breakfast Stewed Fruit

Setup the prunes to soak overnight:

Get the smallest short round 2-handled pot with lid.

  • Wash and thinly slice 1 orange including peel into the pot (slice it in half lengthwise, then in quarters lengthwise, then very thinly slice so making very thin quarter moon slices)
    • Oranges are usually in the back room middle fridge.
  • Wash and cut up a fresh apple and add to the mix also.
    • Apples are usually in the bottom of the kitchen pantry
  • Its also a good use of any pieces of apple or orange left on the public cutting board (you could collect these in the evening & put in fridge for this purpose).
  • Add ~7 cups prunes  (or one Costco bag)
  • Add one 3” cinnamon stick from the spice jars.
  • Fill with water to cover all the fruit plus about 1” of water.

Put the lid on, put the pot in left side of the kitchen Fridge wherever you can find room.  Leave it to soak overnight.

In the morning, the breakfast cook brings it to a boil and simmers for a few minutes while the oats cook.

We can add to it and reheat for up to 3 days in a row, then start over.
