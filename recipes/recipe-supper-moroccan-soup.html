Supper Moroccan Soup

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Extend leftover Moroccan chickpea stew (and any leftover quinoa pilaf) into a soup. If there's almost nothing left to start with, see the recipe for Moroccan Red Lentil Soup in the Simple Soups file instead.

Yield: ~14–16 quarts

Equipment

  • 2 soup warmers
  • 32 qt stock pot

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Extend the leftovers to make ~14–16 quarts, adding red lentils, water mixed with bouillon paste, and more of the Moroccan Stew spices as needed.
  2. Make the soup on the stove in the large stock pot.
  3. If it's thick and at 175° by lunchtime, it should stay warm all afternoon with the fire off and the lid on. If very watery, it might not — in that case, transfer it to soup warmers (under the toasters).
  4. Set up a large bowl of Greek or regular yogurt for a side.
