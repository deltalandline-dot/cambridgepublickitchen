Supper Moroccan Soup

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

ALT Week 2 Day 7           Supper Recipe: Moroccan Soup

Equipment: 2 soup warmers, & 32 qt stock pot

Ingredients

Left over Morroccan chickpea stew, any quinoa pilaf leftover

Extend to make ~14-16 quarts.  Add as needed:

  • Red lentils (up to ~6 cups??)
  • Water mixed with some bouillon paste
  • Maybe some more of the spices from the Moroccan Stew recipe

Note: If there is almost nothing to start with:  see the recipe for Moroccan Red Lentil Soup under Simple Soups File.

Instructions

Make the soup on the stove in the large stock pot

If it is thick and at 175 degrees by lunch time, should stay warm all afternoon with fire off and lid on.  If very watery, might not, so transfer to soup warmers (under toasters).

Set up a lg bowl of Greek or regular yogurt for a side.
