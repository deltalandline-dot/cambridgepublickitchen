Supper Black Bean Soup with Veg

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~16 quarts

Equipment

  • Largest soup pot (to stay warm)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Add your leftovers to the soup pot. Augment as needed to make ~16 quarts total.
  2. Always put thick soups on a flame disperser and stir often but lightly if the bottom is getting burned — don't scrape it up.
  3. Bring to a full boil (or 180°), turn off the fire, and put the lid on; the big pots will stay warm until 5:00.
  4. Probably best to save any leftover chips for the final snack.
