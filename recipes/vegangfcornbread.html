Vegan GF Cornbread

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Adapted from Nisha Vora, rainbowplantlife.com. This fits nicely in two 2" deep hotel pans, each cut 6x8. This can take a couple of hours of off-and-on attention from someone (KM?), so plan for that — approximate times are noted below. Measurements given in grams need a precision scale (there's one, or more, above the spice shelf that looks like an iPad).

Yield: two 2" deep hotel pans, each cut 6x8

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. (Timing below aims for the cornbread to still be warm when served.) Preheat oven to 400 (with the Vulcan, try 450 and watch it).
  2. (~10:15) Stir vinegar and oat milk and set aside at least 10-20 minutes to curdle.
  3. In a large bowl combine flour, cornmeal, baking powder and salt. Whisk well.
  4. (~10:30) Pour in the melted vegan butter, oil, brown sugar, agave, and curdled oat milk. Careful not to overmix, some lumps ok. Should look like pancake mix.
  5. Fold in the rosemary. Allow batter to rest 10 min to 1 hour.
  6. (~11:10) Heat the empty pans in the oven while the batter is resting, about 10 minutes. Remove from oven, add more lumps of vegan butter to melt and coat the pan. Sprinkle pan bottom with cornmeal. Pour in batter, dividing between the 2 pans.
  7. (~11:20) Bake 25-35 minutes (in the Vulcan, rotate front/back and top/bottom halfway through; might take longer than 25). Test with a toothpick or knife. Cool for 15 minutes before slicing, 5x8 per pan.
