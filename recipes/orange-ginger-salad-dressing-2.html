Orange Ginger Salad Dressing (2)

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: for 1 batch (~48 servings)

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • Microplane zester (or box grater)
  • Juicer
  • 2 pitchers

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Zest limes and oranges with the zester. BE CAREFUL! Very sharp!
  2. Juice limes and oranges with the juicer (next to the rice cooker).
  3. Rough chop the ginger (Vitamix will do the rest).
  4. Add all except oil in the Vitamix container and mix on low.
  5. Drizzle oil into the Vitamix mixing while you do. Gradually increase speed until all emulsified. Divide between 2 small pitchers.
  6. Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
