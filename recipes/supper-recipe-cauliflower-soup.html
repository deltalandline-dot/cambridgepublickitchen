Cauliflower Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

This is usually prepped on opening day but served at first-full-day supper. Best idea is to get it all prepped on opening day, then cook it on opening day when the big 32 qt pot is available — it will keep hot all afternoon. Note: there are 2 flavors of this — original (rather plain) and Thai. We usually serve the original with a spinach/Greek salad bar and the Thai with a slaw (see those recipes), though we rarely do the Thai/slaw version now since we don't usually have the extra ingredients unless pre-arranged with the produce orderer.

Preparation

  1. Sauté: sauté onions until soft, add garlic, and sauté a minute or so with ~2 tsp salt. If doing the Thai version, add the ginger here.
  2. Add the cauliflower, (potatoes), water, and bouillon, and cook until tender. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  3. Finish: blend with an immersion blender, then finish with the rest of the flavors of your choice (original or Thai, above). Set up the cilantro as a side.
