Cauliflower Soup

Day 1                           Supper Recipe:  Cauliflower Soup                     Pg 1 of 2

This is usually prepped on opening day but served at first full day supper.  

Best idea is to get it all prepped on opening day, then cook it on opening day when the big 32 qt pot is available and it will keep hot all afternoon.

Note: there are 2 flavors of this: original (rather plain) and Thai.  We usually serve the original with a spinach/greek salad bar and the Thai with a slaw (see those recipes).  We rarely do this now, especially the slaw, we don’t usually have the extra ingredients unless pre-arranged with produce orderer.

Ingredients

For ~48

1 case minus 2 heads

Cauliflower cut into pieces (save the extra 2 heads for the Dal day later in the week)  Use 8lg or 10 med heads (varies by size how many is in a case).

4lbs

4 lbs yellow potatoes, cubed (optional if don’t have)

18 cloves

Garlic (chopped in  mini cuis)

4lbs

Onions chopped

8 quarts

Water

1 Jar

Veggie bouillon 

(more ingredients next page..)

3 bu

Cilantro rough chopped including stems (ON THE SIDE)

Instructions

Saute

Saute onions until soft, add garlic & saute a minute or so with ~2tsp salt.

IF DOING THAI VERSION: add the ginger here.

Add main ingredients 

Add cauliflower, (potatoes), water, and bouillon and cook until tender. Salt and Pepper to taste

Finish

Blend with immersion blender and finish with rest of flavors of your choice.  

Setup Cilantro for a side.
