Alt Portuguese Kale White Bean Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Adapted from Moosewood Restaurant Favorites.

Yield: ~48 servings (14–15 qts) — this gets eaten, no useful leftovers expected

Equipment

  • 32qt (largest) stock pot with pot-saver under it
  • Zester
  • Juicer
  • Spice grinder
  • Mini Cuisinart
  • Separate 12 qt pot (to give beans a head-start)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Prep ahead: Soak dry cannellini beans overnight — if you forget, try the quick-soak method (see side notice area), or use ~7 x 28oz canned beans instead.
  2. Cook Beans: Note: KM will have pre-soaked the beans. Rinse soaked beans. In a separate 12 qt pot, cover with fresh water, bring to a boil, and immediately turn to a low simmer, skimming if needed; toss in a stalk of fresh rosemary. Add to the main soup pot mid-afternoon — they can continue to cook there.
  3. Locate & Prepare Ingredients: Strain off the oil from the oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes, saving the oil to sauté the onions in the step below. Set the tomatoes aside.
  4. When ready to transfer the beans, use bean broth and/or water as needed to make bouillon with Better Than Bouillon.
  5. Dice onions, carrots, fresh fennel, and potatoes.
  6. Kale: remove thick stem from center, tear into large bite-sized pieces.
  7. Wash lemons.
  8. Zest 1–2 lemons (=scrape off the thin outermost yellow layer of skin) using either the microplane (sharp!) or box grater (suggest wearing the cut-resistant glove for either). Set aside in case needed at the end.
  9. Juice lemons using juicer (near rice cooker).
  10. Lightly toast the fennel seeds dry in a small skillet. Grind using spice grinder.
  11. Chop garlic in Mini Cuisinart on chop setting (best if thawed).
  12. Sauté: Be sure heat-saver disk (on stove shelf) is placed under the stock pot.
  13. Using the reserved sun-dried tomato oil if you have it, or else olive oil, sauté the onions until they are getting soft. Add in chopped garlic and 2 tsp salt. Sauté another minute.
  14. Add the ground fennel and diced fennel and cook for ~10 minutes. Stir in the carrots, potatoes, thyme, sun-dried tomatoes and black pepper and cook for ~20 minutes.
  15. Combine & Cook Soup: About 2:30-3:00, add tomatoes, bouillon and beans. Stir well, bring to a boil and reduce to a simmer. Be sure there is a pot-saver/heat disperser under the pot. Simmer until potatoes are just tender.
  16. Add Greens: Just before going to the orientation meeting, stir in the kale.
  17. Final Seasoning: After the orientation meeting, just before serving, stir in (some) lemon juice & zest to taste and salt to taste.
