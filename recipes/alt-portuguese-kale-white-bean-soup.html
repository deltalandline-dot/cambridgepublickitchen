Alt Portuguese Kale White Bean Soup

Opening                   Alt Supper Recipe: Portuguese Kale White Bean Soup    

                                   (adapted from Moosewood Restaurant Favorites)              (p. 1 of 3)                           

Equipment  32qt (largest) stock pot with pot-saver under it, zester, juicer, spice grinder, Mini Cuisinart (& separate 12 qt pot to give beans a head-start)

Prep ahead:  soak dry cannellini beans overnight...if you forget this, try quick soak method (see side notice area), or use ~ 7 x 28oz canned

Ingredients

~48  (14-15qts)

~4-6 bulbs

fresh fennel bulbs (quanity depends on size), ½ “ dice

4lbs

potatoes (ideally yukon gold), ½ “ dice

14 cloves

garlic, chopped in mini-cuisinart

4 ½ Tbls

2 qts dry, cooked

cannellini (white) beans (about 2 qts dry??)(or ~6-7 28oz cans)

 5 bunches

kale, torn into pieces (very roughly 2”x2” sized)

~1 cup (=~8)

lemons, juiced  ...save some zest in case needed //add to taste

             Recipe: Portuguese Kale White Bean Soup         (p.  2 of 3)

Instructions

Cook Beans

Note: KM will have pre-soaked the beans.    Rinse soaked beans.  In a separate 12 qt pot: cover with fresh water, bring to boil and right away turn to a low simmer, skim if needed, toss in a stalk of fresh rosemary.  Add to main soup pot mid afternoon, they can continue to cook in the soup pot.

Locate & Prepare 

Ingredients

Strain off the oil from the oil packed sun-dried tomatoes SAVING THE OIL, and use it to saute the onions in step below.  Set aside tomatoes.

When ready to transfer beans, use bean broth and/or water as needed to make bouillon with Better Than Bouillon.

Dice onions, carrots, fresh fennel, potatoes..

Kale: remove thick stem from center, tear into large bite sized pieced

Wash lemons

Zest 1-2 lemons (=scrape off the thin outermost yellow layer of skin) using either the microplane (sharp!) or box grater (suggest wearing the cut-resistant glove for either).  Set aside in case needed at the end.

Juice lemons using juicer (near rice cooker).

Lightly toast the fennel seeds dry in small skillet.  Grind using spice grinder.

Chop garlic in Mini Cuisinart on chop setting (best if thawed)

 Recipe: Portuguese Kale White Bean Soup        (p. 3 of 3)

Sauté

Be sure heat-saver disk (on stove shelf) is placed under stock pot 

Using the reserved sun-dried tomato oil if you have it, or else olive oil, sauté the onions until they are getting soft.  Add in chopped garlic and 2 tsp salt.  Saute another minute

Add the ground fennel and diced fennel and cook for ~10 minutes. Stir in the carrots, potatoes, thyme, sun-dried tomatoes and black pepper and cook for ~20 minutes.

Combine &

Cook

Soup

About 2:30-3:00: Add tomatoes, bouillon and beans.  Stir well, bring to a boil and reduce to simmer.  Be sure there is a pot-saver/heat disperser under the pot. 

Simmer until potatoes are just tender.  

Add Greens 

Just before going to orientation meeting:, stir in the kale.

Final

Seasoning

AFTER orientation meeting just before serving: Stir in (some) lemon juice & zest TO TASTE and salt TO TASTE.
