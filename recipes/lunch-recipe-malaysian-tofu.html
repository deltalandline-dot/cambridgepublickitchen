Malaysian Tofu

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Malaysian Influenced Tofu, from Deborah Madison.

Yield: ~48 servings (quantities below are the larger figure for possible leftovers; the figure in parentheses is for the "no supper leftovers" version)

Equipment

  • 2 large baking sheets
  • Rondo (or large stock pot)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Bake Tofu: Preheat oven to 350.
  2. Cube tofu into ¾" cubes, layout on baking sheets on parchment and spray with oil. (This may not be cubed until ~10:15.)
  3. Bake for ~30-40 minutes, stirring occasionally until lightly browned.
  4. While they cook, proceed with next steps.
  5. If needed, make cashew butter (if not using bought): 4 cups cashews, roast lightly (@350 for ~10 mins, watching carefully!), puree until very fine in a robo-coupe, add ~2 tsp oil and puree more until no longer grainy, almost dough- or butter-like (doesn't have to be really all the way to commercial butter).
  6. (~10:30) Start Sauce: Check allergies — maybe make a no-cashew side version? Could try with some tahini. Mix all sauce ingredients except mushrooms, tomatoes and scallions in the rondo (or large stock pot if no rondo) on a flame disperser.
  7. Heat on the stove, whisking to blend. Reduce to simmer for 10 minutes.
  8. Once the sauce is smooth and heated, add the mushrooms.
  9. (~11:00) Add Tofu and Garnish: Add the tofu and tomatoes to the sauce and stir. Watch for low simmer and non-sticking. Garnish with chopped scallions just before serving.
