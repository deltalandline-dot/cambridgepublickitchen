Instructions

Bake Tofu

Preheat oven to 350.

Cube tofu into ¾” cubes, layout on baking sheets on parchment and spray with oil. (This may not be cubed until ~10:15)

Bake for ~30-40 minutes, stirring occasionally until lightly browned.

While they cook, proceed with next steps.

If needed

Make Cashew Butter if not using bought:

4 cups cashews, roast lightly (@350 for ~10 mins, watching carefully!), puree until very fine, in robo-coupe, add ~2tsp oil & puree more until no longer grainy, almost dough or butter like (doesn’t have to be really all the way to commercial butter).
