Chai Apple Crumb Cake

Tags: Vegetarian, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Scale recipe: or ×

A chai-spiced apple crumb cake with optional glaze.

Yield: 1 (9") cake, ~12 slices (prep ~40 minutes, cook ~50 minutes, total ~1.5 hours)

Ingredients

Chai spice mix

Chai apples

Streusel

Butter cake

Optional glaze

Preparation

  1. Prep pan. Heat oven to 350°F / 175°C. Generously butter a 9" pan. Press in parchment (crumple/uncrumple helps it fit).
  2. Make chai apples. Toss sliced apples with brown sugar + chai spice. Set aside and save the juices for glaze.
  3. Make streusel. Mix melted butter + brown sugar + flour + chai spice with a fork until clumpy.
  4. Make batter. Whisk flour + baking powder + salt. Beat butter + sugar 3–4 min until fluffy. Beat in eggs + vanilla (~1 min). Mix in half the dry ingredients, then sour cream + milk, then remaining dry — fold gently.
  5. Assemble. Spread half the batter in the pan. Dollop/spread apple butter (optional). Add remaining batter. Top with apples (leave juices behind). Sprinkle streusel on top.
  6. Bake. Bake 40–50 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean / with a few moist crumbs.
  7. Cool. Cool until manageable, then lift out using the parchment.
  8. Optional glaze. Whisk powdered sugar with reserved apple juices (or a splash of milk) to desired consistency; drizzle over cake.
