Chai Apple Crumb Cake

A chai-spiced apple crumb cake with optional glaze.

Prep time: ~40 minutes

Cook time: ~50 minutes

Total: ~1.5 hours

Yield: 1 (9") cake, ~12 slices

Ingredients

Chai spice mix

  • 2 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp each: allspice, cardamom, cloves, ginger

Chai apples

  • 3 small apples, peeled & thinly sliced
  • 40 g light brown sugar (about 3 Tbsp)
  • 1 Tbsp chai spice mix

Streusel

  • 60 g unsalted butter (1/4 cup), melted
  • 90 g light brown sugar (1/3 cup + 1 Tbsp)
  • 120 g all-purpose flour (1 cup)
  • 1 1/2 tsp chai spice mix

Butter cake

  • 210 g all-purpose flour (1 3/4 cups)
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 125 g unsalted butter (1/2 cup + 1 Tbsp), room temp
  • 175 g granulated sugar (3/4 cup + 2 Tbsp)
  • 2 eggs, room temp
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 135 g whole milk (1/2 cup + 2 Tbsp), room temp
  • 75 g sour cream (5 Tbsp), room temp
  • 180 g apple butter (2/3 cup), optional

Optional glaze

  • 90 g powdered sugar
  • 1–2 Tbsp reserved chai-apple juices (or milk)

Preparation

  1. Prep pan. Heat oven to 350°F / 175°C. Generously butter a 9" pan. Press in parchment (crumple/uncrumple helps it fit).
  2. Make chai apples. Toss sliced apples with brown sugar + chai spice. Set aside and save the juices for glaze.
  3. Make streusel. Mix melted butter + brown sugar + flour + chai spice with a fork until clumpy.
  4. Make batter. Whisk flour + baking powder + salt. Beat butter + sugar 3–4 min until fluffy. Beat in eggs + vanilla (~1 min). Mix in half the dry ingredients, then sour cream + milk, then remaining dry — fold gently.
  5. Assemble. Spread half batter in pan. Dollop/spread apple butter (optional). Add remaining batter. Top with apples (leave juices behind). Sprinkle streusel on top.
  6. Bake. Bake 40–50 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean / with a few moist crumbs.
  7. Cool. Cool until manageable, then lift out using parchment.
  8. Optional glaze. Whisk powdered sugar with reserved apple juices (or a splash of milk) to desired consistency; drizzle over cake.
