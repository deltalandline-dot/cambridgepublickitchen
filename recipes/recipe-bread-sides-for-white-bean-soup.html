Bread sides for White Bean Soup

Tags: Vegetarian, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Either Garlic Bread or plain artisan bread with "Basil Butter" (either only if not having Butternut Squash — check with KM what's planned and what ingredients we have).

Equipment

  • Either large trays or a 4" hotel pan
  • Foil for the bread
  • Bowls for the butters

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Garlic bread: mix the oil, garlic, and herbs (if using).
  2. Put the mix on the needed number of regular and gluten-free slices (do the GF first, to avoid contamination of oil and utensils).
  3. Slice the bread in half lengthwise (separating the top half and bottom half) and spread both halves with the garlic oil mix.
  4. Put uncovered on large trays.
  5. About 11:30: bake at 350 for ~½ hour, or until lightly browning (GF may take less time).
  6. Slice each baked half into ~1 ½" thick slices and serve in a basket with a decorative cloth (bottom of the cloth rack).
  7. Basil butter: put the regular butter out early to soften a bit (not so important if using the RoboCoupe).
  8. Wash the basil.
  9. Proportion the basil between the regular butter and the Earth Balance. Works well to mix the regular butter in the Robo Coupe (clear bowl setup) and the non-dairy in the little mini-cuisinart.
  10. Put the sliced bread into a hotel pan, cover with foil or a domed lid. About 11:30: heat at 350.
  11. Serve in a basket with the butters on the side.
