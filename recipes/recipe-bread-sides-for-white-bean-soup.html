Bread sides for White Bean Soup

Tags: Vegetarian, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Week 2 Day 4         Recipe: Bread Side Options for White Bean Soup                                  

Equipment  Either: large trays OR 4” hotel pan, foil for bread, Bowls for butters

EITHER Garlic Bread OR plain artisan bread with “Basil Butter” (either only if not having Butternut Squash–check with KM what’s planned and what ingredients we have)

For Garlic Bread:

4-5 loaves of Sourdough or other “white” artisan bread

Olive oil (?1 cup to start?)

~20 cloves of garlic minced in the mini cuisinart

Optional: dried basil and marjoram (~1T each)

Mix oil and garlic & herbs if using.

Put mix on the needed number of regular Gluten Free slices.

  (do the GF first to avoid contamination of oil & utensils)

Slice bread in half lengthwise (separating top half and bottom half)

Spread both with garlic oil mix.

Put uncovered on large trays.

About 11:30: Bake at 350 for ~½ hour or until lightly browning. (GF may take less..)

Slice each baked half into ~1 ½” thick slices.

Serve in a basket with a decorative cloth (bottom of cloth rack)

FOR “Basil Butter” and plain heated artisan bread

4-5 loaves  Mixed Artisan breads, sliced  thick (or use 2-3 of TJ’s big Triangles of whole wheat bread, cut triangle in thirds or fourths, then slice)

Gluten free bread slices as needed (~1 ⅕ per GF person)

1 ¼ lb regular butter 

½ tub of Earth Bal  //check quantity est. with no-dairy head-count

1 bunch basil

Put regular butter out early to soften a bit (not so important if using RoboCoupe).

Wash basil

Proportion the basil between the regular and Earth Balance.  Works well to mix the regular in the Robo Coupe (clear bowl setup) and the non-dairy in the little mini-Cuisinart. 

Put sliced bread into a hotel pan, cover with foil or domed lid. ~ 11:30: Heat at 350.  

Serve in a basket with butters on the side.
