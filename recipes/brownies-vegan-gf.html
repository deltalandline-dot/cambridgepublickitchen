Brownies Vegan GF

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Based on the minimalistbaker.com version.

Yield: one 2" hotel pan serves ~52, cutting 8x6 and halving the large corner pieces (or use a second half-pan for more). This batch is scaled to 1 ½ pans; a single 1-pan batch is noted alongside each ingredient.

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F and spray hotel pan.
  2. Prepare vegan eggs in a small bowl, whisk well, and let rest for 5 minutes.
  3. Melt Earth Balance on the stove in a saucepan.
  4. Mix melted Earth Balance, sugar, vanilla, vegan eggs, baking powder, salt, and cocoa powder in a large bowl. Whisk to combine. Lastly, add the flour in stages and combine. Fold in nuts if using.
  5. Fill pan or muffin tins (¾ full).
  6. Bake for 22-26 minutes (or more like 35 — check often). Shake the hotel pan; it's done when the center is stable and the sides are pulling away. Remove and let rest and cool before serving.
  7. Serve or store in an airtight container for a couple of days, or freeze.
  8. Yummy with or without ice cream.
