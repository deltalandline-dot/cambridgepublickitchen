Brownies (Vegan & GF)

--based on minimalistbaker.com version

Note: one 2” hotel pan can serve 52 by cutting 8x6 & cut large corners in half, or might want a second half-pan for more.    

1 ½ pans 1 pan

4.5 c=3 ¾ tubs 3 cups vegan butter eg. Earth Balance (=2 ½ tubs)

6 ¾ cups 4.5 cups cane sugar

18=1c+1T ve,,2 1/4c water 12 Vegan Eggs  (eg Bob’s ¾ cup powder in 1 ½ cups water)

3T 2T vanilla

3T 2T baking powder

2 ¼ tsp 1 ½ t salt

4.5 cups 3 cups cocoa powder

6 ¾ cups 4.5 cups Gluten Free Flour (eg Bob’s 1-1)

Optional: 3 cups 2 cups walnuts or pecans

  • Preheat oven to 350 and spray hotel pan.
  • Prepare vegan eggs in small bowl, whisk well & let rest for 5 minutes.
  • Melt EarthBal on stove in sauce pan.
  • Mix melted EarthBal, sugar, vanilla, vegan-eggs, baking powder, salt and cocoa powder in large bowl.  Whisk to combine.  Lastly add the flour in stages and combine.  Fold in nuts if using.
  • Fill pan or muffin tins (¾ full).
  • Bake for 22-26 minutes (or more like 35--check often).  Shake hotel pan and done when center is stable and sides are pulling away.  Remove and let rest & cool for serving.
  • Serve or store in airtight container for a couple of days or freeze.

Yummy with or without ice cream ;-).
