Agave Dijon Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings (1 batch)

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • 2 pitchers

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Add all ingredients except oil in the Vitamix container and mix on low.
  2. Drizzle oil into the Vitamix while mixing. Gradually increase speed until all emulsified. Divide between 2 small pitchers.
  3. Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
