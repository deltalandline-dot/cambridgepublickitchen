Pinto Beans

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~50 servings either way. Use the first (larger) quantity below unless the retreat is very small (~30) or you don't want leftovers for a supper casserole — pinto beans are a popular leftover for RVs. Second, smaller quantity is for no supper leftovers.

Equipment

  • 20 qt stock pot for the beans
  • Large skillet for the onions

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Prepare and start beans: Rinse the soaked beans.
  2. Put beans, salt and fresh water in the stockpot.
  3. Bring to a boil and skim off any foam.
  4. When done skimming, add bay leaves.
  5. Turn down to a vigorous simmer; check frequently to be sure it is still simmering actively and has adequate liquid.
  6. Prep other ingredients: Chop garlic in mini-Cuisinart.
  7. Toast & grind cumin.
  8. Sauté other ingredients: In the large skillet or small brazier, sauté the onions.
  9. When they are translucent, add the garlic.
  10. Stir tomato paste with 1/2 c. water and add to the onion mix; simmer 10 minutes.
  11. Add all the other seasonings (cumin, oregano, chili powder, brown sugar, salt). Simmer another 10 minutes, stirring frequently.
  12. Combine and finish: Scrape the onion & spice mix into the bean pot. Put a heat deflector under the bean pot!
  13. Let it continue to simmer, checking and adding water as needed to make a good burrito filling consistency (not too soupy, not too thick to burn).
  14. About 11:45, add the green chilies, check taste, and add salt as needed.
  15. Serve with slotted spoons if still watery.
