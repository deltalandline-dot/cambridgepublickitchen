Instructions

Prepare and Start Beans

Rinse the soaked beans.

Put beans, salt and fresh water in stockpot.

Bring to a boil and skim off any foam.

When done skimming, add bay leaves.

Turn down to a vigorous simmer..check frequently to be sure it is still simmering actively and has adequate liquid.

Prep other ingredients

Chop garlic in mini-Cuis

Toast & grind cumin

Saute other ingredients

In the large skillet or small brazier, sauté the onions.                                                       

When they are translucent, add the garlic.

Stir tomato paste with 1/2c. water and add to the onion mix, simmer 10 minutes.

Add all the other seasonings (cumin, oregano, chili powder, brown sugar,salt).  Simmer another 10 minutes stirring frequently.

Combine and finish

Scrape the onion & spice mix into the bean pot. PUT A HEAT DEFLECTOR UNDER THE BEAN POT!!

Let it continue to simmer, checking and adding water as needed to make a good burrito filling consistency (not too soupy, not too thick to burn).

About 11:45: add the green chilies, check taste & add salt as needed

Serve with slotted spoons if still watery.
