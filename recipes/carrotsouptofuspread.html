Carrot Soup Tofu Spread

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~44 servings (aiming to make about 8 qts, not counting excess water)

Equipment

  • 24 qt stock pot
  • Medium plastic bowl

Ingredients

Carrot Soup

Tofu Spread

Preparation

  1. Carrot Soup: Purée the cashews in the Robo Coupe.
  2. Chop the carrots in the Robo Coupe.
  3. Sauté the onions in the stock pot until translucent.
  4. Add carrots, ginger, (yams), cashews, 5-spice & water to the stock pot.
  5. Bring to a boil, reduce to active simmer, stir frequently.
  6. Whisk or use immersion blender to blend smooth after all ingredients are cooked very soft.
  7. Taste for seasonings & salt.
  8. Tofu Salad: Crumble the tofu to the texture of crumbled eggs.
  9. Wash & chop the herbs.
  10. Add the other ingredients and mix.
  11. Taste for salt, pepper, seasoning.
  12. Refrigerate until serving time.
  13. Serve with corn cakes.
