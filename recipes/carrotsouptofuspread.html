Carrot Soup Tofu Spread

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free

Carrot Soup, Tofu Spread

Prep Equipment:  24qt stock pot, med. Plastic bowl

Ingredients

For 44 (aiming to make 8qts beans not counting excess water)

Soup:

Carrots, chopped 13lbs

Onions, diced 4lbs

Ginger, chopped 2lbs

??garlic??

(yams, cubed or pulp) 10lbs or whatever is leftover

Water to make 19 qts, aiming for 18qts when done

cashews 3lbs

five spice powder 2T

??check with Anna some of the below for the dip?

Tofu Spread

Tofu 6lbs

Vegenaise 2c

Celery, fine dice 4c

Turmeric 2T

Dijon Mustard 6T

Nut. Yeast 6T

Parsley 1 bu

Dill or Tarragon 1 bu

Scallions, chopped 3 bu

Corn cakes 8 packs

Carrot Soup, Tofu Spread

Instructions:

Carrot Soup

Puree the cashews in the Robo Coupe

Chop the carrots in the Robo Coupe ??

Saute the onions in the stock pot until translucent.

Add carrots, ginger, (yams), cashews, 5-spice & water to stock pot. 

Bring to a boil, reduce to active simmer, stir frequently.

Whisk or use emersion blender to blend smooth after all ingredients are cook very soft.

Taste for seasonings & salt.

Tofu Salad

Crumble the tofu to the texture of crumbled eggs...  

Wash & chop the herbs.

Add the other ingredients and mix.

Taste for salt, pepper, seasoning.

Refrigerate until serving time.

Serve with corn cakes.

Lunch:  Carrot Soup, Tofu salad (Hearty items for salad bar..)
