Instructions

Rinse the rice well using the large double-mesh strainer (may take several batches).

Between 10:30-11:00

Add rice and water  per above quantities to the rice cooker.  Leave it unplugged until ready to start cooking.

About 10:45: Plug in and turn on the cooker.  It will take about 50-60mins to cook, then it switches to warm.  Before serving, check that is has switched to warm.  If so, carefully open the lid and fluff it with a wood or plastic tool.   Unplug the machine to turn off the “warming”.

Serve with wooden spoons so as not to scratch the container.
