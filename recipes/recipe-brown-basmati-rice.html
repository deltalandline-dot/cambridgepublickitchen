Brown Basmati Rice

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: 16 cups dry rice if doing a supper leftovers batch, or 13 cups if not

Equipment

  • Large strainer
  • Rice cooker

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Rinse the rice well using the large double-mesh strainer (may take several batches).
  2. (~10:30–11:00) Add the rice and water, per the quantities above, to the rice cooker. Leave it unplugged until ready to start cooking.
  3. About 10:45: plug in and turn on the cooker. It will take about 50-60 minutes to cook, then it switches to warm. Before serving, check that it has switched to warm. If so, carefully open the lid and fluff it with a wood or plastic tool. Unplug the machine to turn off the "warming".
  4. Serve with wooden spoons so as not to scratch the container.
