Instructions

Prepare 

Ingredients

Locate all ingredients

Make bouquet garni

Slice/dice all the onions, leeks, shallots & carrots if not done.

Mix bouillon in 2 qts hot tea-urn water.

Saute

Be sure heat-saver disk (on stove shelf) is placed under stock pot 

Coat the bottom of the pot with light olive oil to about ⅛” deep

Sauté (=fry in a little oil uncovered, stirring occasionally) the onions, leeks, shallots and carrots until the onions are getting soft and translucent (not brown).

Add the diced squash, garlic, 3T salt, and 1 ½ T pepper and cook gently for a few minutes, reducing the heat as necessary to keep the garlic and squash from coloring.  May need to add a little of the stock to make it stirrable.

Stir in the agave and cook, stirring, for a few more minutes, stirring often.

Add stock & simmer

Add the stock and bouquet garni bags, bring to a simmer and cook until the squash is tender.

Puree

Puree the soup until smooth and creamy using the immersion blender.

Taste and salt as needed.

*Squash Ordering Note:  I used to try to pre-order 1 week in advance 25lbs of pre-cubed squash from Coast, but they have a terrible record of forgetting it, messing it up, bringing the wrong thing..).  Way too hard for yogis to peel and cube that much hard squash along with everything else needed for the meal.

New discoveries:  order or buy ~30 lbs = ~10 each of whole butternuts and FREEZE them whole, then thaw them overnight.  Now really soft skin and easy enough for yogis to peel and cube.  Too watery to roast but ok for soup.  OR BETTER: poke several holes in them with a fork, roast them the day before whole in the oven for ~1hr at 400, let that cool.  Then easy to peel.  Or can microwave, but pre-roasting might be easier for 10.
