Butternut Squash Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

See squash ordering note at the end of the ingredients and instructions.

Yield: ~50 servings (~24 qts), with supper leftovers

Equipment

  • 32 qt (largest) stock pot, with pot-saver under it

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Prepare ingredients: locate all ingredients.
  2. Make the bouquet garni.
  3. Slice/dice all the onions, leeks, shallots, and carrots, if not already done.
  4. Mix the bouillon in 2 qts hot tea-urn water.
  5. Sauté: be sure the heat-saver disk (on the stove shelf) is placed under the stock pot.
  6. Coat the bottom of the pot with light olive oil to about ⅛" deep.
  7. Sauté (fry in a little oil, uncovered, stirring occasionally) the onions, leeks, shallots, and carrots until the onions are getting soft and translucent (not brown).
  8. Add the diced squash, garlic, 3 T salt, and 1 ½ T pepper, and cook gently for a few minutes, reducing the heat as necessary to keep the garlic and squash from coloring. May need to add a little of the stock to make it stirrable. Stir in the agave and cook, stirring, for a few more minutes.
  9. Add stock and simmer: add the stock and bouquet garni bags, bring to a simmer, and cook until the squash is tender.
  10. Puree: puree the soup until smooth and creamy using the immersion blender.
  11. Taste and salt as needed.

Squash ordering note: pre-ordering 25 lbs of pre-cubed squash a week in advance from Coast used to be the plan, but they have a terrible record of forgetting it, messing it up, or bringing the wrong thing — and it's way too hard for yogis to peel and cube that much hard squash along with everything else needed for the meal.

New discoveries: order or buy ~30 lbs (~10 each) of whole butternuts and freeze them whole, then thaw overnight — the skin gets really soft and is easy enough for yogis to peel and cube. Too watery to roast this way, but fine for soup. Or, better: poke several holes in them with a fork and roast whole the day before in the oven for ~1 hr at 400, then let cool — easy to peel afterward. Could also microwave, but pre-roasting might be easier for a crew of 10.
