Leftover Lentil Salad

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings (about 16 cups of leftovers)

Equipment

  • Large bowl

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Serve the leftovers (Lentil Grape Salad, roasted yams/beets/carrots, quinoa) combined or separately, however you like.
  2. Set up elements of the Greek Salad Bar from Week 1 Day 1: spinach, Kalamata olives, pumpkin seeds, tomatoes, feta cheese, and maybe cucumbers. Probably skip the chickpeas if you have lentil salad left.
  3. Serve with whatever dressing is left — Greek Lemon or Balsamic are good.
