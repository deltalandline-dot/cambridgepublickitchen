Vegan Cheesy Broccoli Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Scale recipe: or ×

ALT Day 6                           Supper Recipe:  Broccoli Soup        page 1 of 2

Equipment: largest 32 quart stock pot, Vitamix, immersion blender

Note: ideally put cashews to soak at breakfast, ready to use by 9:30, soup is ok but just grittier if not pre-soaked

Ingredients

For ~48

5 cups

Cashews, soaked in water to cover by about 2”

1 case

Broccoli, rough chop heads into florets/chunks, optional: could trim some stems and chop (trim VERY DEEPLY, be sure to get ALL the tough stringy stuff off)

4lbs

Yukon type potatoes, 1” cubed (or a bit larger ok)

4lbs

Onions, medium diced

¾ cup

Minced garlic (use mini cuisinart)

2 cups

Nutritional Yeast

1 lemon

Juice and zest

1 jar

Better Than Bouillon

~6 quarts 

Water–use ~3 quarts from hot tea urn into a metal pitcher to dissolve the bouillon, then add the other 3 qts cold

To taste

Salt & pepper (maybe white pepper?)

Optional:

Could serve with cheddar and vegan cheddar on side

Day 6                           Supper Recipe:  Broccoli Soup        page 2 of 2

Instructions

Prep

Blend cashews in their water in the Vitamix until smooth

Mix bouillon with 3 quarts hot tea urn water.

Chop garlic in mini-Cuisinart

PUT a heat-deflector under the pot.  Saute onions in olive oil, add garlic when onions are soft, watch so it doesn’t burn.

Add liquids

Add broth, yeast & cashew blend to onions.  Mix.

Add rest

Add potatoes, (most of the) broccoli & rest of water.   You may need to cook down the broccoli a bit to fit in the rest.  (It all reduces down a lot when done & blended).  Use the big wooden paddle to turn the ingredients so all the broccoli gets cooked.  Cook until broc and potatoes are soft enough to eat and temp is >= 180.

Blend

Using the large immersion blender, blend the soup.  Maybe put the pot on the floor or on a step stool, easier to hold the big blender.

Season

Add lemon juice and zest, salt and pepper to taste.

Get back to ~180, turn off burner, put on lid & leave on stove for supper.
