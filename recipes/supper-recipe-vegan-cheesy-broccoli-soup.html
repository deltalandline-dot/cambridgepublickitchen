Vegan Cheesy Broccoli Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Ideally put the cashews to soak at breakfast so they're ready to use by 9:30 — the soup is fine but just grittier if not pre-soaked.

Yield: ~48 servings

Equipment

  • Largest 32 quart stock pot
  • Vitamix
  • Immersion blender

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Prep: Blend cashews in their water in the Vitamix until smooth. Mix bouillon with 3 quarts hot tea urn water. Chop garlic in the mini-Cuisinart.
  2. Put a heat-deflector under the pot. Sauté onions in olive oil, add garlic when onions are soft, watch so it doesn't burn.
  3. Add liquids: Add broth, yeast & cashew blend to the onions. Mix.
  4. Add rest: Add potatoes, (most of the) broccoli, & the rest of the water. You may need to cook down the broccoli a bit to fit it all in (it all reduces down a lot when done & blended). Use the big wooden paddle to turn the ingredients so all the broccoli gets cooked. Cook until the broccoli and potatoes are soft enough to eat and the temp is ≥ 180°.
  5. Blend: Using the large immersion blender, blend the soup. Maybe put the pot on the floor or on a step stool — easier to hold the big blender.
  6. Season: Add lemon juice and zest, salt and pepper to taste.
  7. Get back to ~180°, turn off the burner, put on the lid, and leave on the stove for supper.
