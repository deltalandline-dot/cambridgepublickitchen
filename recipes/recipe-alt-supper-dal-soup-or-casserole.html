ALT Supper Dal Soup or Casserole

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

This recipe has two forms, made from leftovers: a soup, or a casserole.

Yield: Soup makes ~15-16 quarts; Casserole serves ~48 (fills two hotel pans at least half full)

Equipment

  • Largest (32 qt) stock pot, for the soup
  • Two 2" deep hotel pans, for the casserole

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Soup: mix it all up, bring to a boil, taste, turn off, cover, and it should keep hot for the afternoon. Serve with sides of leftover raita or yogurt, and leftover chutney or raisins.
  2. Casserole: layer the food into the 2 pans: rice, veggies, dal. Serve with sides of leftover raita or yogurt, and leftover chutney or raisins.
