Roasted Butternut Squash

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Equipment

  • Large baking trays

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Optional: rub large pieces of squash with olive oil and sprinkle tops with mixed herbs (avoid garlic for diets — good to keep this plain and make maple butter instead, as below).
  2. Place pieces on large baking trays.
  3. Bake at 375° for 1 ½ to 2 hours total (allow for even more). Done when quite soft and slightly brown. Okay to turn off the oven and leave them in there if done early.
  4. Serve with regular and non-dairy butter.
  5. Or, for an extra treat, make maple butter: in the Robo-Coupe or mini-cuisinart, mix butter, maple syrup, and maybe nutmeg to taste; make a version with vegan butter too.
