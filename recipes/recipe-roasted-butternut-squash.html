Roasted Butternut Squash

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Week 2 Day 4         Recipe: Roasted Butternut Squash                                  

Equipment  large baking trays

Ingredients:

  • butternut squash peel left on, chunked ~6-8 pcs per squash, 
  • optional olive oil
  • mixed herbs (optional, good to keep this plain for diets and maybe make herbal or maple butters)

Optional:  rub large pieces of squash with olive oil, sprinkle tops with mixed herbs (avoid garlic for diets, good to keep this plain and make maple butter as below)

Place pieces on large baking trays.

Bake at 375 for 1 ½ to 2 hours total (allow for even more).  Done when quite soft and slightly brown.  Ok to turn off oven and leave them in there if done early.

Serve with regular and non-dairy butter.

Or for an extra treat:  make Maple Butter:  in Robo or min-cuis mix butter, maple syrup and maybe nutmeg to taste, make a version with vegan butter.
