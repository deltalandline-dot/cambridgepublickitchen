Galette (Sweet or Savory)

A basic galette dough (sweet or savory) plus filling ideas.

Yield: 1 large galette (about 14-inch round)

Ingredients (dough)

  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 1 1/2 tsp sugar (sweet only; omit for savory)
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 8 Tbsp cold unsalted butter, cubed (1/2" pieces)
  • 1/3 cup ice water
  • 2 Tbsp melted butter (for brushing)
  • Turbinado/raw sugar (sweet only; omit for savory)

Preparation

  1. Make dough. In a food processor, pulse flour + sugar (if using) + salt. Add butter; pulse 8–10 times to coarse crumbs.
  2. Add water. Run processor and add ice water 1 Tbsp at a time until dough comes together.
  3. Chill. Form into a 4-inch disk, wrap tightly, and chill at least 30 minutes (or overnight).
  4. Preheat. Heat oven to 375°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment.
  5. Roll + fill. Roll dough into a ~14-inch round. Add filling, leaving a 1–2 inch border.
  6. Fold + finish. Fold edges up, pleating as you go. Brush edges with melted butter; sprinkle sugar for sweet galettes.
  7. Bake. Bake 35–45 minutes until golden. Cool ~15 minutes.

Filling ideas

Savory

  • Roasted red onion
  • Rosemary
  • Chorizo
  • Balsamic reduction

Sweet

  • Apple
  • Clementine zest
  • Cardamom
  • Cinnamon
  • Ginger
  • Lemon juice

Cocktail (ideas)

  • Gin (small amount)
  • Rosemary simple syrup
  • Lemons
  • Seltzer
