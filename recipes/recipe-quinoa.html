Quinoa

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Scale recipe: or ×

Day 1                                                      Recipe: Quinoa                                       Page 1 of 1                              

Equipment  large strainer, rice cooker  CLEAN RICE COOKER IF NEEDED

Ingredients

#48 (lunch only)

10 cups

quinoa (note we sometimes have red, sometimes white quinoa, use what you can find and prefer)

12 ½ cups

water  (ratio of Q:W we use is 1:1 ¼)

Instructions

~ 10:30: Rinse the quinoa very thoroughly  or it has a bitter taste, using the large double-mesh strainer and running your fingers through them (may take several batches).  Put the rinsed grains into the rice cooker (not turned on).  They can sit there rinsed until it’s time to add the water and cook.

Just before 11:00:  Add water (as above) in the ratio of 1 ¼   cups of water for each cup of quinoa.

11:00: Plug in and turn on the cooker.  It will take about 45mins to cook, then it switches to warm.  Set a timer to check when it should be done.  Let it sit for a few minutes, then carefully open the lid and fluff it with a wood or plastic tool.  

If there is time, cover the pot with 2-3 clean white towels and gently set the lid back down.  This will absorb excess moisture and help the quinoa be fluffier. 

You must unplug the machine to turn off the “warming”.

Serve with wooden spoons so as not to scratch the container
