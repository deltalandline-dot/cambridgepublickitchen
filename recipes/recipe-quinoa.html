Quinoa

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings (lunch only)

Equipment

  • Large strainer
  • Rice cooker (clean rice cooker if needed)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. (~10:30) Rinse the quinoa very thoroughly, or it has a bitter taste, using the large double-mesh strainer and running your fingers through it (may take several batches). Put the rinsed grains into the rice cooker (not turned on). They can sit there rinsed until it's time to add the water and cook.
  2. (Just before 11:00) Add water (as above) in the ratio of 1 ¼ cups of water for each cup of quinoa.
  3. (11:00) Plug in and turn on the cooker. It will take about 45 minutes to cook, then it switches to warm. Set a timer to check when it should be done. Let it sit for a few minutes, then carefully open the lid and fluff it with a wood or plastic tool.
  4. If there is time, cover the pot with 2-3 clean white towels and gently set the lid back down. This will absorb excess moisture and help the quinoa be fluffier. You must unplug the machine to turn off the "warming."
  5. Serve with wooden spoons so as not to scratch the container.
