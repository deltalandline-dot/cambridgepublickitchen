Instructions

Note: you may not be able to fit all the zucchini on sheets, if not, steam the extras and save them for the supper casserole.

Preheat both ovens to 375.

Line the pans used for the carrots with parchment paper.  Don’t line the pans for the zucchini.

If veggie choppers already put on trays, spray with spray olive oil, or else hand mix with oil in bowls and then spread on trays.

The carrots benefit from extra long cooking, the corn gets added last, so try this timing (with help from  a timer):

  • 10:40 put the carrots in
  • 11:00 put the zucchini in and stir the carrots
  • 11:30 stir both & pour the corn over top of all pans, return to oven.
  • (11:40 you will be putting tortillas in the oven about here…you may have to combine  veggies into fewer pans to make room–lower oven temp for tortillas when veggies done)
  • 11:45 check for doneness. The zucchini and carrots should be softened (not mushy but easy to fork through) and beginning to brown slightly

When done, empty them all in a 4” hotel pan and mix.
