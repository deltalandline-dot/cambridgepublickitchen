Roasted Zucchini Carrots and Corn

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~50 servings with supper leftovers (or ~35–50 servings using the smaller quantities, without leftovers)

Equipment

  • 3 full-sized baking sheets, or a mix of large and small to fit

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Note: you may not be able to fit all the zucchini on sheets — if not, steam the extras and save them for the supper casserole.
  2. Preheat both ovens to 375°.
  3. Line the pans used for the carrots with parchment paper. Don't line the pans for the zucchini.
  4. If the veggie choppers have already put the vegetables on trays, spray with olive oil spray; otherwise hand-mix with oil in bowls and then spread on trays.
  5. The carrots benefit from extra long cooking, and the corn gets added last, so try this timing (with help from a timer):
  6. (10:40) Put the carrots in.
  7. (11:00) Put the zucchini in and stir the carrots.
  8. (11:30) Stir both and pour the corn over the top of all the pans, return to oven.
  9. (~11:40) You'll be putting tortillas in the oven about here — you may have to combine veggies into fewer pans to make room, and lower the oven temp for the tortillas once the veggies are done.
  10. (11:45) Check for doneness. The zucchini and carrots should be softened (not mushy but easy to fork through) and beginning to brown slightly.
  11. When done, empty them all into a 4” hotel pan and mix.
