Mu Shu Wraps

Tags: Vegetarian, Dairy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: about 1½ wraps per person; quantities below are given for batches of 2, 14, 21, or 30 people (3, 21, 30, or 45 tortillas respectively).

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Note: figure out a strategy for special dietary needs (e.g. some diners get scrambled eggs instead of tofu, alternate sauces, etc.).
  2. Night before: marinate the tofu (see 5-spice tofu steps below).
  3. First thing: bake the marinated tofu slices at 350°F until dry and firm-looking (keep an eye on it, turning halfway through).
  4. Soak all the dried ingredients (tree ears, shiitakes, lily buds) in boiling water for about 30 minutes. Cut the lily buds in half after soaking.
  5. Stir-fry: Stir-fry the onion, minced ginger, and garlic. Add the tree ears, shiitakes, and lily buds (take off the heat once the steamed parts are nearly ready). Add the sauce to the stir-fry.
  6. Steam: Steam the cabbage, ginger slivers, and carrots in a steam tray (allow about 30 minutes).
  7. Mix together the steamed vegetables, the stir-fry, and the tofu.
  8. Maybe heat the tortillas (the thin ones don't need it).
  9. Serve with sliced scallions, a ½ hoisin / ½ reserved mushroom-soaking-liquid mix, and the tortillas, with instructions to spread some hoisin and scallions on a tortilla, add some mu shu filling, then wrap.
  10. Good served with butternut squash or yams, a kale salad with orange and roasted cashews, and miso dressing, for example.
  11. 5-Spice Tofu (try to make the day before): Press the tofu, then cut the loaf into thin-ish slices horizontally. Marinate in 1 Tbsp soy, 1 Tbsp water, ¼ tsp 5-spice powder, and 2 drops sesame oil (large baggies work well for marinating). Refrigerate overnight.
  12. Next day: bake the tofu slices on a baking sheet at 350°F for about an hour, turning halfway; check often for burning and add a little water as needed — you want it to dry out and get firmer. Cut the slices into matchsticks for the mu shu.
  13. Homemade Hoisin Sauce: Blend all the hoisin sauce ingredients together (listed above). You could cook it a little on the stove afterward, or it's fine just blended.
