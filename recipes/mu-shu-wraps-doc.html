Night before: marinate tofu (see below)

First thing: bake marinated tofu slices at 350 until dry & firm looking (keep an eye, turn over half way..)

FIGURE OUT STRATEGY FOR SPECIAL NEEDS (like no-tofu = some with scrambled eggs, alt sauces etc.)

Soak all dried stuff in boiling water about 30 mins

Cut lily buds in half after soaking

Stir-fry onion, minced ginger, garlic

Add tree ears, shitakes, lily buds

(set off fire until steamed parts are nearly ready)

Add sauce to stir-fry

Steam in steam tray (allow 30 min): cabbage, ginger slivers, carrots

Mix together the steamed stuff, stir-fry and tofy.

Maybe heat tortillas (the thin ones don’t need it)

Serve with sliced scallions, ½ hoisin/ ½ mush lqd mix, tortillas

& instructions to spread some hoisin and scallions on tortilla and then add some mushu, then wrap. 

Good served with butternut squash or yams, kale salad with orange and roasted cashews with miso dressing for example.

*5-spice Tofu:

Try to make day before:

press tofu

cut loaf into thin-ish slices horizontally

marinate in 1 T soy, 1 1 T water, ¼ t 5-spice powder, 2 drops sesame oil (I like to use large baggies to marinate it)

refridgerate over night

Next day: bake on baking sheet at 350 for an hour or so, turn halfway, check often for burning and add a little water.  You want it to dry out and get more firm.

Cut slices into matchsticks for the Mushu.
