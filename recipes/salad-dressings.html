Balsamic Salad Dressing

 (1.5 qts has been enough for a full house of 44 for one meal)

~1.5qts  ~3qts (for 2 meals)

  • 1 ¾  c 3 ½   cups  balsamic vinegar
  • 6 12 cloves peeled garlic
  • ½ c 1  cups water
  • 1 T 2 T Dijon mustard
  • 1 T 2 T sherry vinegar
  • ¾  T 1 ½   T black pepper
  • 1 ½  t 2 ½ t  salt (add gradually and taste)
  • 1 ½ t 2 ½ t sugar (add gradually and taste)
  • 3 ½  cups 7 cups olive oil  

Poach the whole peeled garlic cloves in one cup of the oil (one cup out of the amt. called for) in the small saucepan or skillet on medium heat for ~5 minutes to make them more mild.

Add all except the rest of the oil to the blender (poached garlic cloves and their oil can be added unchopped).  Blend.

If room in the Vitamix, drizzle in the oil as you keep blending. If no room, transfer to a larger container and whisk in the oil.

Taste (drizzle a little into a separate spoon) and adjust salt, pepper or other seasonings

For one meal: fill 2 small pitchers.  Serve with smallest ladles.

Herb Vinaigrette (also used for “Tarragon” in menus)

1 ½ qts 3qts (2 meals)

  • 2 c 4c Rice vinegar
  • ¼ c ½ cup honey
  • ¼  c ½ cup Dijon mustard
  • 6 cl 12 cloves garlic
  • 1 c 2 cups fresh herbs (2 version we use: ½ basil ½  parsley, OR all tarragon )
        • Note: for a quick basic vinaigrette: instead of fresh herbs, gradually add ~1-2 T of any mix of dried herbs: eg parsley, basil, tarragon, thyme, dried onion.. to taste
  • 4 c 8 c Canola oil
  • Salt  to taste

Rinse the herbs & pick the leaves off the stems.

Poach the whole peeled garlic cloves in one cup of the oil (one cup out of the amt. called for) in the small saucepan or skillet on medium heat for ~5 minutes to make them more mild.

Add all except the rest of the oil to the blender (poached garlic cloves and their oil can be added unchopped).  Blend.

If room in the Vitamix, drizzle in the oil as you keep blending. If no room, transfer to a larger container and whisk in the oil.

Taste (drizzle a little into a separate spoon) and adjust salt, pepper or other seasonings.

For one meal: fill 2 small pitchers.  Serve with smallest ladles.

Orange Ginger Salad Dressing

1.5qts  

  • ~1T zest of 2 limes
  • ~2T zest of 2 oranges
  • ~1-2T juice of the 2 limes
  • 3 c juice about 4 oranges and top off with apple cider vinegar
  • 2T chopped ginger (about a 2” piece…coarse chop it and let the Vitamix chop it fine along     with the rest)
  • 1 ½ T ground cumin (pre-ground is fine)
  • 1 c olive oil
  • 2 ½  c canola oil

Using the microplane zester or box grater, scrape off just the colored layer of rind from the limes and oranges, try not to get much of the white (it is more bitter).  Be careful with your fingers and knuckes, this tool is very sharp!

Using the electric juicer, juice the limes and oranges.

Add all except oil to the blender.  Blend.

If room in the Vitamix, drizzle in the oil as you keep blending. If no room, transfer to a larger container and whisk in the oil

Taste (drizzle a little into a separate spoon) and adjust salt, pepper or other seasonings

For one meal: fill 2 small pitchers.  Serve with smallest ladles.

Greek Lemon Salad Dressing

  • 1 ½ c Rice vinegar
  • ½ T lemon zest

  • ¼  cup honey
  • 2T  cup Dijon mustard

  • 1T oregano

Using the electric juicer, juice the lemons.

Add all except oil to the blender.  Blend.

If room in the Vitamix, drizzle in the oil as you keep blending. If no room, transfer to a larger container and whisk in the oil

Taste (drizzle a little into a separate spoon) and adjust salt, pepper or other seasonings

For one meal: fill 2 small pitchers.  Serve with smallest ladles.

Lemon Tahini Salad Dressing

For 1 ½ qts: 

  • 2 ½ c tahini
  • 1 c lemon (juice about 4 fresh lemons)
  • 2 ½ c water
  • 5 cloves garlic
  • ¾   c parsley
  • 1 ½ T tamari (omit if no-soy yogis and probably use more salt..)
  • 2t ground cumin
  • 1t white pepper (start with half and add to taste..)
  • Maybe more salt or tamari to taste..

Using the electric juicer, juice at least 4 fresh lemons .

Wash the parsley and cut off the stems (ok to have some of the upper stems).

Add all ingredients to the blender and blend.

Taste (drizzle a little into a separate spoon) and adjust salt, pepper or other seasonings

For one meal: fill 2 small pitchers.  Serve with smallest ladles.
