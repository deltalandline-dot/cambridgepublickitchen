Broccoli-Ricotta Lasagna

Tags: Vegetarian, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Scale recipe: or ×

A simple broccoli-and-ricotta lasagna with béchamel and smoked scamorza.

Yield: ~6 portions (as written)

Ingredients

  • Fresh lasagna sheets — 250 g (or no-boil sheets)
  • Broccoli, cleaned — 500 g
  • Ricotta — 200 g (sheep's milk if possible)
  • Béchamel — 500 ml
  • Parmesan, grated — 100 g
  • Smoked scamorza, cubed — 100 g
  • Salt, to taste

Béchamel

  • Milk — 500 ml
  • Butter — 50 g
  • White flour or cornstarch — 50 g
  • Salt — a pinch

Preparation

  1. Make béchamel. In a pan, whisk milk + butter + salt + flour/cornstarch before heating to dissolve lumps. Cook, stirring constantly, until thickened (~10 minutes).
  2. Prep broccoli + ricotta. Steam broccoli, then mash with a fork. Reserve ~3 Tbsp broccoli for topping. Mix ricotta with mashed broccoli.
  3. Prep cheese. Cube smoked scamorza.
  4. Assemble. Spread a layer of béchamel in the pan. Add a layer of pasta, then béchamel, parmesan, scamorza, and broccoli-ricotta. Repeat until used up; finish with béchamel + reserved broccoli + parmesan.
  5. Bake. Bake at 240°C covered with foil until bubbling, then reduce to 200°C and bake 30–40 minutes total. Remove foil for the last ~5 minutes to brown lightly.
  6. Serve. Cool briefly, slice, and serve. For reheating leftovers, add a splash of milk and warm in a pan.
