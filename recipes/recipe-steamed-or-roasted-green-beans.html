Steamed or Roasted Green Beans

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

ALT Day 6                                     Recipe: Roasted or Steamed Green Beans               Page 1 of 1

SHOULD BE 13 lbs of green beans…if there are a lot more than that, they are intended for another upcoming meal (eg chicken tofu)

Roasted (actually steamed a bit first..):

Equipment: 2 large baking trays, foil

Toss beans in olive oil, sprinkle with salt and maybe pepper.   Heat oven to 350

These will need to be done early to have time for the Tempeh to use the oven.   They can be served room temp.   We found it best to “steam” them for 20-30 minutes first by covering the trays of beans with foil, when almost soft enough to eat,  remove the foil and roast for another ~10 mins until slightly browning.

OR Simple Steamed:

Equipment  steamer pan setup (either 1 re-used for 2 batches or 2 setups)

You may need to wash the steamer pans used for the tempeh to reuse for the beans.   These can be done anytime and served either warm or room temp.

Steam the beans for ??20 minutes until they are as soft as you like them.   Be sure to check that the water doesn’t all boil off.
