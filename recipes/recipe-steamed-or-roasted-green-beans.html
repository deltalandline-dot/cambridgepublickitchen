Steamed or Roasted Green Beans

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~13 lbs green beans (if there's a lot more on hand, the extra is meant for another upcoming meal, e.g. chicken tofu)

Equipment

  • Roasted method: 2 large baking trays, foil
  • Steamed method: steamer pan setup (either 1 pan re-used for 2 batches, or 2 setups)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Roasted (actually steamed a bit first): Toss the beans in olive oil, sprinkle with salt and maybe pepper. Heat oven to 350°.
  2. These will need to be done early to have time for the tempeh to use the oven. They can be served room temp. We found it best to "steam" them for 20–30 minutes first by covering the trays of beans with foil; when almost soft enough to eat, remove the foil and roast for another ~10 minutes until slightly browning.
  3. OR Simple Steamed: You may need to wash the steamer pans used for the tempeh to reuse for the beans. These can be done anytime and served either warm or room temp.
  4. Steam the beans for ~20 minutes until they are as soft as you like them. Be sure to check that the water doesn't all boil off.
