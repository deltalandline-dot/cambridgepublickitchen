Alt Moroccan Red Lentil Soup

Instructions

Prepare 

Ingredients

Locate all ingredients

Measure out lentils and set aside

Dice onions, carrots & celerly

Kale: remove thick stem from center, tear into large bite sized pieced

Wash lemons

Zest 1-2 lemons (=scrape off the thin outermost yellow layer of skin) using either the microplane (sharp!) or box grater (suggest wearing the cut-resistant glove for either).  Set aside in case needed at the end.

Juice lemons using electric juicer or wooden reamer

Wash parsley, discard main trunk of stems, finely chop rest

Chop garlic in Mini Cuisinart on chop setting (best if thawed)

If prefer:  Toast and grind cumin & coriander using small skillet, watching carefully just till it is fragrant.  Grind in black spice grinder, wipe out grinder before and after.  Or just used pre-ground.

Sauté

Be sure heat-saver disk (on stove shelf) is placed under stock pot 

Coat the bottom of the pot with olive oil to about ⅛” deep

Sauté (=fry in a little oil uncovered, stirring occasionally) the onions until they are getting soft.

Add in the carrots & celery and 2t salt.  Sauté for 20 minutes or so to develop the flavor.

Add the garlic, coriander, cumin, turmeric, paprika, cinnamon, salt and pepper and continue cooking for another 2-3 minutes stirring the whole time.

Cook

Lentils

(By about 2:30): Add broth, tomatoes and lentils.  Stir well and heat to a boil.  Be sure there is a pot-saver/heat disperser under the pot.  

Let it simmer at least until the lentils are soft (1 hr or more), fine to leave it on 2hrs or more or until we leave for the meeting

 Stir frequently, add more water if it is getting too thick.

Add Greens

(~4:20) Just before going to the orientation meeting, stir in the kale.  Turn the fire off and put a lid on the pot.

Final

Seasoning

(~5:15 leave meeting early): stir in lemon juice, cayenne, parsley.  Re-heat ~10 minutes.  Taste and adjust seasoning (maybe more salt or lemon zest).
