Alt Moroccan Red Lentil Soup

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings (14–15 qts)

Equipment

  • 32qt (largest) stock pot with pot-saver under it
  • Zester
  • Juicer
  • Mini Cuisinart

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Prepare Ingredients: Locate all ingredients.
  2. Measure out lentils and set aside.
  3. Dice onions, carrots & celery.
  4. Kale: remove thick stem from center, tear into large bite-sized pieces.
  5. Wash lemons.
  6. Zest 1–2 lemons (=scrape off the thin outermost yellow layer of skin) using either the microplane (sharp!) or box grater (suggest wearing the cut-resistant glove for either). Set aside in case needed at the end.
  7. Juice lemons using electric juicer or wooden reamer.
  8. Wash parsley, discard main trunk of stems, finely chop the rest.
  9. Chop garlic in Mini Cuisinart on chop setting (best if thawed).
  10. If preferred: toast and grind cumin & coriander using a small skillet, watching carefully just till fragrant. Grind in the black spice grinder, wiping it out before and after. Or just use pre-ground.
  11. Sauté: Be sure heat-saver disk (on stove shelf) is placed under the stock pot.
  12. Coat the bottom of the pot with olive oil to about ⅛” deep.
  13. Sauté (=fry in a little oil uncovered, stirring occasionally) the onions until they are getting soft.
  14. Add in the carrots & celery and 2 tsp salt. Sauté for 20 minutes or so to develop the flavor.
  15. Add the garlic, coriander, cumin, turmeric, paprika, cinnamon, salt and pepper and continue cooking for another 2–3 minutes, stirring the whole time.
  16. Cook Lentils: (~2:30) Add broth, tomatoes and lentils. Stir well and heat to a boil. Be sure there is a pot-saver/heat disperser under the pot.
  17. Let it simmer at least until the lentils are soft (1 hr or more) — fine to leave it on 2 hrs or more, or until it's time to leave for the meeting. Stir frequently, add more water if it's getting too thick.
  18. Add Greens: (~4:20) Just before going to the orientation meeting, stir in the kale. Turn the fire off and put a lid on the pot.
  19. Final Seasoning: (~5:15, leave meeting early) Stir in lemon juice, cayenne, and parsley. Re-heat ~10 minutes. Taste and adjust seasoning (maybe more salt or lemon zest).
