Banana Bread with Almond Filling Swirl

Tags: Vegetarian, Soy-Free

Scale recipe: or ×

Banana bread with an almond filling swirl.

Yield: 1 loaf (9×5-inch), keeps ~4 days wrapped (freezes well)

Ingredients

Almond filling

Banana bread

Preparation

  1. Make almond filling. Mix egg, almond flour, sugar, almond extract, and melted butter. Refrigerate.
  2. Prep oven/pan. Heat oven to 350°F. Grease and flour a 9×5-inch loaf pan.
  3. Brown butter. Melt butter in a saucepan; cook until nutty-brown. Transfer to a bowl to cool.
  4. Mix dry. Whisk flour, sugar, baking soda, salt, cinnamon (and nutmeg if you like).
  5. Mix wet. Whisk eggs, vanilla, and yogurt; whisk in mashed banana; whisk in cooled browned butter.
  6. Combine. Fold wet into dry just until combined (don't overmix).
  7. Layer. Add half batter to pan, spread half almond filling, add remaining batter, then remaining filling on top. Sprinkle slivered almonds.
  8. Bake. Bake 50–60 minutes until a skewer comes out clean.
  9. Cool. Rest 15 minutes in pan, then remove and cool completely. Dust with powdered sugar if desired.
