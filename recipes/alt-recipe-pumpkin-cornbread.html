Alt Recipe Pumpkin Cornbread

Tags: Vegetarian, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Note: see the simpler "Vegan GF Cornbread" recipe — all versions of this one have eggs. Decide with KM how many GF/DF or regular pans to make; typical for a full house would be 3 half-pans regular, 1 half-pan GF/DF. Or, if too much trouble, you can make it all GF/DF. If you have no-egg people, consider making a small sauce pot of polenta on the stove (1 part polenta : 4 parts water) instead.

Yield: scales for 1–4 half-size (2”) hotel pans; ingredient amounts below are given per pan count (4 pans / 3 pans / 2 pans / 1 pan)

Equipment

  • 1-4 2” half-sized hotel pans (cooks more reliably in half-pans than whole pans)
  • 2 large bowls
  • Maybe 2 smaller bowls for GF/DF version

Ingredients — wet, dairy version

Ingredients — wet, no-dairy version

Ingredients — dry (vary flour type to get regular or GF version)

Preparation

  1. Preheat oven to 350.
  2. Mix the wet ingredients together in one bowl and the dry in a separate bowl (also keeping the different diet versions separate, so perhaps 4 bowls needed).
  3. Spray all the pans with spray oil.
  4. Mix the wet and dry together (for each diet version). Just lightly until mixed, don't over-stir.
  5. Fill the pans and bake at 350 for about 45 minutes (range could be 30-60). Done when lightly brown, lightly patting the top feels solid, toothpick comes out clean.
  6. Let it cool a bit and pre-slice with a bread knife into 4x3=12 or 4x4=16 pieces per pan.
  7. Serve with butter and Earth Balance.
