Alt Recipe Pumpkin Cornbread

                     ALT Recipe: Pumpkin Cornbread (reg, gf, df versions)                     Pg 1 of 2

Equipment  1-4 2” half-sized hotel pans (cooks more reliably in half-pans than whole pans), 2 large bowls, maybe 2 smaller bowls for GF/DF version.

Note: see the simpler “Vegan GF Cornbread” recipe.  All versions of this have eggs.

Decide with KM: 

How many GF/DF or regular to make.  Typical for a full house would be 3 half pans regular, 1 half pan GF/DF.  

Or if too much trouble, you can make it all GF/DF.  If you have no-egg people, consider making a small sauce pot of polenta on the stove (1 part polenta: 4 parts water).

Instructions

Preheat oven to 350

Mix the wet ingredients together in one bowl and the dry in a separate bowl  (also keeping the different diet versions separate, so perhaps 4 bowls needed).

Spray all the pans with spray oil.

Mix the wet and dry together (for each diet version).  Just lightly until mixed, don’t over-stir.

Fill the pans and bake at 350 for about 45 minutes (range could be 30-60).  Done when lightly brown, lightly patting the top feels solid, toothpick comes out clean.

Let it cool a bit and pre-slice with bread knife into 4x3=12 or 4x4=16 pieces per pan.

Serve with butter and Earth Balance.
