Chai

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

This page combines two chai recipes found together in the source: a bulk institutional recipe (Surya's) and a small-batch "Chai Masala" recipe.

Yield: Surya's bulk batch (below, with milk added at serving) serves ~18. No yield was given for the Chai Masala recipe.

Ingredients

Surya's Chai

Chai Masala (Indian Spiced Tea)

Preparation

  1. Surya's Chai: Simmer for an hour.
  2. Sit overnight to steep.
  3. Bring back to a boil.
  4. Add the loose black tea.
  5. Add the sugar.
  6. Cook 5-10 minutes.
  7. Strain & cool = chai base.
  8. Serve 3:1 with whole milk, reheating (for a full recipe: all of the above plus 2/3 gal milk, serves 18).
  9. Sourcing tip: a cheap source of cardamom is United Natural Foods / Frontier Herb, about $15 (per source note) — you need to find a non-profit that bulk-orders from them to piggyback onto.
  10. Chai Masala (Indian Spiced Tea): Pour six cups water, tea, ginger, and cardamom into a saucepan, bring to a boil and cook 3 minutes.
  11. Add milk and sugar to taste, and boil 2 minutes more.
  12. Strain and serve.
