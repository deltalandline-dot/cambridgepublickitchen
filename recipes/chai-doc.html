Chai Masala (Indian Spiced Tea)

Published: December 7, 2010

4 teaspoons tea leaves 

1 teaspoon coarsely chopped ginger (no need to peel) 

2 green cardamom pods, cracked 

1/2 cup milk 

Sugar, to taste. 

Pour six cups water, tea, ginger, and cardamom into a saucepan, bring to a boil and cook 3 minutes. Add milk, and sugar to taste, and boil 2 minutes more. Strain and serve.
