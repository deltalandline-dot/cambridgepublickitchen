Tarragon Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Note: you may be asked to make 2 batches — read the instructions below before starting. The quantities here are for ONE batch.

Yield: ~48 servings per batch

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • 2 pitchers
  • Extra 2 qt square container with green lid, for a second batch

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Rinse herbs — no need to chop, the Vitamix will do it.
  2. Add all ingredients except the oils to the Vitamix container and mix on low. If making 2 batches, measure a second batch (except oils) into the square container.
  3. Drizzle the oils into the Vitamix while mixing. Gradually increase speed until all emulsified. Divide between 2 small pitchers.
  4. For a second batch: pour the measured container ingredients into the Vitamix and blend. Add the second batch of oils as above. Pour back into the container, label, date, and store in the fridge for later.
