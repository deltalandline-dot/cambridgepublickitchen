Broccoli Cashew Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~50 servings

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Cover raw cashews with water by 2 inches and let sit for 1-2 hrs for the cashews to absorb water and soften. Blend cashews in the water until creamy.
  2. Sauté onions in a large stock pot until soft. Add garlic and sauté for 1-2 minutes, stirring frequently.
  3. Add remaining ingredients. Bring to a boil and lower to a simmer. Once the broccoli is cooked, about 15-20 minutes, blend with an emulsifying (immersion) blender. Add lemon to taste.
