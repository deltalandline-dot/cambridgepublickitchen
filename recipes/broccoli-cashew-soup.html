Broccoli Cashew Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Vegan Cheezy Broccoli

Servings: 50 | Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients:

5 cups cashews

water

4 lbs onions diced

3/4 cup minced garlic

8 quarts water 

8 lbs broccoli

4 lbs potatoes

1 container better than bouillon vegetarian stock

2 cups nutritional yeast

juice of lemons to taste (optional)

Directions:

1. Cover raw cashews with water by 2 inches and let sit for 1-2 hrs for cashews to absorb water and soften. Blend cashews in water until creamy.

2. Sautee onions in large stock pot until soft.  Add garlic and sauté for 1-2 minutes stirring frequently. 

3. Add remaining ingredients. Bring to a boil and lower to a simmer.  Once broccoli is cooked, about 15-20 minutes blend with an emulsifying blender. Add lemon to taste.
