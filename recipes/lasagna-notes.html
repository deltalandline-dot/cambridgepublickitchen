Lasagna notes

Tags: Vegetarian, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: 2 regular hotel-pan lasagnas + ¼ special-needs (SN) pan

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Scaling notes (for reference): need 12 cups cheese/spinach mix (24 cups = 8 qts when doubled); 11 cups sauce ×2.5 = 28 cups; 23 sheets of TJ's noodles (~21 per box) means ordering 2 boxes → 3 boxes total; 4 cups mozzarella doubles to 8 cups; 2 cups parmesan doubles to 4 cups; 2 cups bread crumbs ×2 = 4 cups.
  2. Cheese mix (need 12 cups total; ratio is 1 cup ricotta : ½ cup spinach): about 9 lbs raw spinach or 9 (10 oz) frozen bags (4 cups prepped/cooked ×2.25); about 8 lbs ricotta (7 cups = 4 lbs, doubled to 7.5 → about 5x32oz tubs, or 8 15oz tubs); about 4 cups goat cheese (1 cup = 2 Trader Joe's tubs or 1 small log, doubled); 8 eggs (4 eggs = ~1 cup, doubled); 1 cup bread crumbs (buy); 1 T nutmeg; salt & pepper.
  3. Sauce: bottled sauce plus sautéed 4 lbs mushrooms and 4 lbs onions.
  4. Layer (per hotel pan): 2c sauce; 7½ sheets TJ's lasagna noodles; 6c cheese/spinach mix; 3c sauce; 3c mozzarella; 7½ sheets noodles; 6c cheese/spinach mix; a layer of julienned basil (optional); 3c sauce; 7½ sheets noodles; 3c sauce; top with 2c parmesan + 1c bread crumbs + 1c mozzarella.
  5. Sauté the mushrooms and onion, maybe with some extra dry basil and oregano.
  6. Mix mushrooms and onion into the sauce.
  7. Steam the spinach and press dry (set aside some spinach for the special-needs version(s)).
  8. Beat the eggs and mix the cheeses with the spinach and nutmeg (for the alt version, make tofu ricotta and add spinach).
  9. Layer the pans as above.
  10. (~11:00) Bake for 1 hour, then let sit 15 minutes.
