Frittata Lunch Recipe Two Quantities

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: quantities below are for the batch "with supper leftovers"; a smaller "no supper leftovers" batch size was in the template but its quantities were never filled in.

Equipment

  • Rondo
  • Two full 2" hotel pans

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Cook Veggies: Preheat oven to 350°F. Heat the rondo on medium heat and add oil to the pan. Once hot, add onions.
  2. Once onions begin to get tender, add garlic and cook for a minute or two until fragrant.
  3. Add chopped veggies of KM's choice.
  4. Constructing Frittata: While veggies are cooking, mix eggs, milk, Parmesan cheese, and scallions.
  5. Once veggies are cooked, reserve 2 cups of veggie mixture for alternative-diet folks if relevant this meal. Then add veggies to two hotel pans evenly.
  6. Top veggies with cheese and pour egg mixture over, distributing between two pans evenly.
  7. Place in oven at 11:00 am and cook until frittata is firm in center, 45–60 minutes. Remove from oven and cut into 24 portions per pan.
  8. For bean salad: Add 2–29 oz cans white beans to reserved veggies. Taste to ensure flavor; salt and pepper as needed.
