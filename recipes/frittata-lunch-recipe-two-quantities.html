Frittata Lunch Recipe Two Quantities

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Recipe: Frittata                                   

Equipment  Rondo, two full 2 in hotel pans

Ingredients

with supper

leftovers

no supper

leftovers

7-10 lbs

Veggies of choice (red pepper, zucchini, kale, spinach, mushroom etc…)

6 cups

30 cloves

4 cups

Parmesan Cheese

10 dozen

Eggs

5 cups 

 Milk or half and half if you have on-hand

24 

Scallions chopped

4-6 cups

Cheese shredded or crumbled

2-29 oz cans

White beans

Recipe: Frittata

Instructions

Cook Veggies

Preheat oven to 350 degrees. Heat rondo on medium heat and add oil to pan. Once hot add onions.

Once onions begin to get tender add garlic and cook for a min or two until fragrant.

Add chopped veggies of KM’s choice

Constructing Frittata

While veggies are cooking, mix eggs, milk, parmesan cheese and scallions.  

Once veggies are cooked reserve 2 cups of veggie mixture for alternative diet folks if relevant this meal.  Then proceed to add veggies to two hotel pans evenly. 

Top veggies with cheese and pour egg mixture over, distributing between two pans evenly. 

Place in oven at 11:00 am and cook until frittata is firm in center 45-60 min. Remove from oven and cut into 24 portions per pan.

For bean salad

Add 2- 29 oz cans white beans to reserved veggies. Taste to ensure flavor, salt and pepper as needed.
