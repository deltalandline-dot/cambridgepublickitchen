Mashed Potatoes

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: 48 servings with a supper-leftovers reserve (or the smaller amounts in parentheses below for 48 servings with no supper leftover, ~36 if also serving supper)

Equipment

  • 2 6" deep hotel pans
  • Large metal bowl
  • Potato masher (hand tool)
  • Maybe mixer

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Put into 2 deep hotel pans and cover with water and 1 tsp salt per pan.
  2. Cover and bring to a boil.
  3. Cook until tender (estimated 40-45 minutes).
  4. Drain each pan into a large colander and put back in the pan.
  5. Working in batches, mash with a hand-held pastry cutter (or try the electric mixer or immersion blender if you like it super smooth, but rough is fine).
  6. Add in Earth Balance as needed for good mashed-potato texture.
  7. Salt to taste (lightly — they can always add more).
