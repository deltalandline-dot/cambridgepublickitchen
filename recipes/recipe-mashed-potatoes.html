Recipe: Mashed Potatoes                                                    Page 1 of 1                          

Equipment 2 6” deep hotel pans, large metal bowl, potato masher (hand tool), maybe mixer

Ingredients

No supper

20lbs 

15 lbs

Yellow, White or red potatoes cut into quarters

Yukon gold are the best if avail..

~ 1 ½ cups

1 cup

Earth Balance (no-soy if needed)

1 Tbls+to taste

2 tsp+ to taste

salt

Instructions:

Put into 2 deep hotel pans and cover with water & 1tsp salt per pan

Cover and bring to a boil

Cook until tender (est 40-45 mins)

Drain each pan  into large collander and put back in the pan

Working in batches, mash with hand-held pastry cutter (or you could try the electric mixer or immersion blender if you like super smooth, but rough is fine)

Add in Earth Balance as needed for good mash-potato texture.

Salt to taste (lightly, they can always add).
