Carrot Cashew Soup

Carrot Cashew Soup  (THIS NEEDS WORK, PLACEHOLDER, GOOD ENOUGH FOR NOW)

Equipment: heaviest 32qt stock pot, immersion blender

Ingredients

  • 14 lbs Carrots  //could pre-slice the day before in the robo coupe
  • 3lbs onions, diced
  • 1 lb ginger
  • (optional: <= 5lbs leftover yams)
  • 3lbs cashews
  • 2T 5 spice powder

Instructions:

Cover cashews with water and soak for 1-2 hours (ie do it at start of veggie chop, leave it till 9:30)

Dice onions

Locate ~14lbs of carrots & maybe yams: Excess already shredded, leftover roasted yams.

Chop the ginger in batches in the mini-chopper (grossly cut up first, no need to peel if organic).

Sauté the onions in the large rondo pan until getting soft (20-30 minutes?..not till browning).

Add carrots (and yams) and continue to sauté stirring often over low heat until some light browning, not too brown.

While these sauté:  puree the cashews in the vitamix (multiple batches if needed)

Add ginger and 5 spice powder & sauté another minute or two

Add cashew milk and water to bring it up to ~14 qts

Bring to a boil, reduce to simmer stirring frequently.

After all ingredients are soft, use emersion blender to blend smooth.

Taste for seasonings and salt or add more 5 spice as needed, or salt, pepper, lemon juice?

Aim to have it about 170 degrees by lunch time.  Then turn off the gas and leave the pot covered on the stove.  It should stay above 145 for the afternoon.  Reheat at 5:00 to bring it back to 165.

Optional: serve with Yogurt if still leaves 2x32oz left for final breakfast.
