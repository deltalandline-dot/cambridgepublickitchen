Carrot Cashew Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Yield: ~14 quarts

Equipment

  • Heaviest 32qt stock pot
  • Immersion blender

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Cover cashews with water and soak for 1–2 hours (i.e. do it at the start of veggie chop, leave it till 9:30).
  2. Dice onions.
  3. Locate ~14 lbs of carrots & maybe yams: excess already shredded, leftover roasted yams.
  4. Chop the ginger in batches in the mini-chopper (roughly cut up first, no need to peel if organic).
  5. Sauté the onions in the large rondo pan until getting soft (20-30 minutes, not till browning).
  6. Add carrots (and yams) and continue to sauté, stirring often over low heat until some light browning, not too brown.
  7. While these sauté: puree the cashews in the Vitamix (multiple batches if needed).
  8. Add ginger and 5-spice powder & sauté another minute or two.
  9. Add cashew milk and water to bring it up to ~14 qts.
  10. Bring to a boil, reduce to simmer stirring frequently.
  11. After all ingredients are soft, use immersion blender to blend smooth.
  12. Taste for seasonings and salt, or add more 5-spice as needed, or salt, pepper, lemon juice.
  13. Aim to have it about 170 degrees by lunch time. Then turn off the gas and leave the pot covered on the stove — it should stay above 145 for the afternoon. Reheat at 5:00 to bring it back to 165.
  14. Optional: serve with yogurt if there are still 2x32oz left for final breakfast.
