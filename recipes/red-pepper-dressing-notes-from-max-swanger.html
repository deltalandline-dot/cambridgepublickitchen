Red Pepper Dressing Notes from Max Swanger

the red pepper dressing is using the jars of roasted peppers i got.

put them in vitamix (drain and split them in two batches)

add red wine vinegar-blend-drissle in olive oil and season to taste with salt and pepper.

usually about 1:3 in terms of vinegar to oil… like maybe try 3/4 C vinegar to 2 1/4 cup oil per batch-should work.

you can probably put it all in there at once and it will prob. still emulsify…start with 1tsp each salt/pepper

that’s what I was going to do.
