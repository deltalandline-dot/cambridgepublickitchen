Preparation

  1. Put the roasted peppers in the Vitamix (drained, split into two batches).
  2. Add red wine vinegar and drizzle in the olive oil — usually about 1:3 vinegar to oil, e.g. try ~¾ cup vinegar to 2¼ cups oil per batch. Should work.
  3. Season to taste with salt and pepper, starting with about 1 tsp each.
  4. Blend. You can probably put it all in at once and it will likely still emulsify.
