Lemon Cookies (2)

Tags: Vegan, Dairy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: 18 cookies

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Preheat oven to 350°.
  2. Using a fork or electric mixer, beat sugar and oil together.
  3. Add mylk, zest and vanilla; beat until it resembles applesauce.
  4. Add half the flour and the remaining cornstarch, baking powder and salt.
  5. Add remaining flour.
  6. Spoon about 2 T per cookie, flatten with hands.
  7. Bake 10-12 min until bottoms are golden brown.
  8. Lemon Glaze: sift powdered sugar and add remaining glaze ingredients, whisk well, then drizzle on completely cooled cookies and place in fridge to harden the glaze.
