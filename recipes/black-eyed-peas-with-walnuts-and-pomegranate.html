Black Eyed Peas with Walnuts and Pomegranate

From Cooks Mediterranean

4-5 qts leftover, could be about right for full house

This filled Lael’s large bowl heaped up (or a med-large metal bowl)

Off-season suggestions: cranberries & celery?? (but cran & pommol???)


X7 for 38

1 ¼ c 3 T evoo

1 ¼ c 3 T dukkah //see recipe below

14 T = ¾ cup+2T 2 T lemon juice &  zest of 2-3 lemons

14 T = ¾ cup+2T 2 T pomegranate molasses

S&p to taste 

14@15oz or 7 28 oz 2 15 oz cans black eyed peas, rinsed Note: 1 can = 1 ¾ cups 

Note: 3lbs dry=scan 5qts cooled, added 4 cans = 6qts/24 cups beans

3 ½ c ½ c walnuts toasted and chopped

3 ½ c //used 3 lg poms ½ c pom seeds

3 ½ c //used ~2c ½ cup minced parsley

28 4 scallions sliced thin

Whisk evoo, ¾ cup of the dukkah, lemon, pom.molasses, 1 ½  t salt,  1t pepper until smooth

Add black-eyed peas, walnuts, pom seeds, parsley & scallions & toss.

Season as needed with s & p //I added some Aleppo pepper, meh

Sprinkle with rest of Dukkah as garnish

*Fancy homemade Dukkah (or use TJs)

2 cups:

1 15oz can chickpeas

1 t evoo

½ cup pistachios toasted

1/3 cup black sesame seeds, toasted

2 ½ T coriander seeds, toasted

1 T cumin seeds, toasted

2 t fennel seeds, toasted

1 ½ t pepper

1 ¼ t salt

Preheat oven to 400

Rinse & drain & Pat chickpeas dry

Toss with oil, roast until brown and crisp 40-45 mins stirring every 5-10 mins

Process in food processor until coarse ground

Pulse pistacious & sesame to coarse ground

Pulse cor, cum & fen to fine ground

Add pepper & salt & whisk
