Black Eyed Peas with Walnuts and Pomegranate

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

From Cook's Mediterranean. Makes about 4-5 quarts with leftovers — enough for a full house — and fills a large bowl heaped up (or a medium-large metal bowl). Off-season, could try swapping in cranberries and celery in place of the pomegranate.

Yield: ~4-5 quarts / about 38 servings (this batch is scaled 7× from the single-batch recipe below)

Ingredients

Black-eyed pea salad

Dukkah (makes ~2 cups; homemade, or use store-bought, e.g. Trader Joe's)

Preparation

  1. Dukkah: Preheat oven to 400°F. Rinse, drain, and pat the chickpeas dry.
  2. Toss chickpeas with oil and roast until brown and crisp, 40-45 minutes, stirring every 5-10 minutes.
  3. Process the roasted chickpeas in a food processor until coarsely ground.
  4. Pulse the pistachios and sesame seeds to a coarse grind.
  5. Pulse the coriander, cumin, and fennel seeds to a fine grind.
  6. Combine, add pepper and salt, and whisk together.
  7. Black-eyed pea salad: Whisk the olive oil, ¾ cup of the dukkah, lemon juice and zest, pomegranate molasses, 1 ½ tsp salt, and 1 tsp pepper until smooth.
  8. Add the black-eyed peas, walnuts, pomegranate seeds, parsley, and scallions, and toss.
  9. Season as needed with salt and pepper (a bit of Aleppo pepper also works, though it was only so-so here).
  10. Sprinkle with the rest of the dukkah as garnish.
