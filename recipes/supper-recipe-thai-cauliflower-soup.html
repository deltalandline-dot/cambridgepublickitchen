Thai Cauliflower Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

First Day   Supper Recipe:  Thai Cauliflower Soup

This is usually prepped on opening day but served at first full day supper.  Best idea is to get it all made except for the “final touches” on opening day or even day before (as soon as you have the produce), then reheat it on first full day when the big 32 qt pot is available and it will keep hot all afternoon.

Ingredients

For ~48

10 hds (1 case -2)

Cauliflower: 2 heads cut into edible size flowerets, 8 heads rough chopped for later blending. [set aside the other 2 from the case of 12 for another recipe later]

5lbs

Yellow onions, diced medium

1 ½ cups

Ginger, minced in small cuisinart

30 cloves 

Garlic, minced in small cuisinart

2-3

Fresh jalapeno peppers, seeded, chopped fine (optional)

~¾  cup

Fresh lime juice, maybe ~10 limes? And zest of ~5 of them

⅔  cup

Yellow Thai Curry paste

⅔ of a lg can

Coconut milk

Simple Stock:

1 jar bouillon

Mixed with ~3 qts hot tea machine water to dissolve, add with more water or lemongrass stock (see below) to a total of ~16 quarts of soup (of SOUP, not just of stock, maybe ~6 qts stock total??)

1 bu

Optional: Scallions, chopped white & light green parts

2 bags

Frozen peas

salt

To taste at end

OPTIONAL: 

STOCK FOR AMBITIOUS COOKS WHO HAVE EXTRA TIME: make stock with 

~20

Lemon grass stalks, ?galangal, kaffir lime leaves

Instructions

Saute

Saute onions until, add ginger, garlic, jalapenas & saute a minute or so. 

Add ingredients and cook

Add rough chopped cauliflower and stock (either diluted bouillon or fancy) to not quite cover the cauliflower…and let cook until cauliflower is soft.

Blend with immersion blender until smooth

Add curry paste & blend that in.

Add florets.  

This should now be about noon:  insure it’s over 170.  Put a lid on it, turn off the gas and it will stay hot all afternoon.

[or cool and save to reheat when needed]

Final Touches

At 5:00pm

Test temp, put it on a flame disperser and turn the gas back on to low/med.  

If time: head the coconut milk in a sauce pan enough to melt fat, but not to boil. Add in the coconut milk.  Make sure it is ~165 but try not to let it come back to a boil (separates the coconut milk).

Add lime zest and juice to taste.

Add salt to taste if needed

~5mins before serving: add the peas (probably thawing in the fridge).

Serve with cilantro (listed under the salad prep, they both call for it) and optionally the chopped scallions.
