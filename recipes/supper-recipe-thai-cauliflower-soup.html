Thai Cauliflower Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

This is usually prepped on opening day but served at first full day's supper. Best idea is to get it all made except for the "final touches" on opening day or even the day before (as soon as you have the produce), then reheat it on the first full day when the big 32 qt pot is available — it will keep hot all afternoon.

Yield: ~48 servings

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Sauté: Sauté onions until, add ginger, garlic, jalapenos & sauté a minute or so.
  2. Add ingredients and cook: Add the rough-chopped cauliflower and stock (either diluted bouillon or fancy) to not quite cover the cauliflower, and let cook until the cauliflower is soft.
  3. Blend with an immersion blender until smooth. Add curry paste & blend that in.
  4. Add the florets.
  5. This should now be about noon: insure it's over 170°, put a lid on it, turn off the gas, and it will stay hot all afternoon (or cool and save to reheat when needed).
  6. Final Touches (at 5:00pm): Test temp, put it on a flame disperser and turn the gas back on to low/med. If time: heat the coconut milk in a sauce pan enough to melt the fat, but not to boil, then add it in. Make sure it is ~165° but try not to let it come back to a boil (it separates the coconut milk). Add lime zest and juice to taste. Add salt to taste if needed. ~5 mins before serving: add the peas (probably thawing in the fridge).
  7. Serve with cilantro (listed under the salad prep — they both call for it) and optionally the chopped scallions.
