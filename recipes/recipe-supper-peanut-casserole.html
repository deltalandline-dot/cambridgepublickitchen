Supper Peanut Casserole

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Scale recipe: or ×

Supper Recipe:  African Peanut Casserole

Equipment: 2 2” hotel pans with domed lids (on top of pan rack)

The idea:

Improvise casseroles out of leftover African Peanut Stew, Roast Cabbage if any, rice.

Ingredients (in this case, potential ingredients, use what we have & be creative)

For ~48

Min ~3-4 qts

Leftover rice, make some on stove top if not enough

Min ~6-8 qts

Leftover African Peanut Stew--add some canned chickpeas if not enough

Optional

Any leftover roasted cabbage

Instructions

You may want to layer the casseroles with grain on the bottom, then the stew and cabbage, or mix it all up.

Fill the 2 pans evenly with the mix and cover them with domed lids, label them and put them in the fridge.  Check that serving signs list the ingredients you wind up using.
