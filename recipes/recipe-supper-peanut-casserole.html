Supper Peanut Casserole

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

The idea: improvise a casserole out of leftover African Peanut Stew, roasted cabbage (if any), and rice — use what we have and be creative with the ingredients below.

Yield: for ~48 servings, fills 2 2” hotel pans

Equipment

  • 2 2” hotel pans with domed lids (on top of pan rack)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. You may want to layer the casseroles with grain on the bottom, then the stew and cabbage, or mix it all up.
  2. Fill the 2 pans evenly with the mix and cover them with domed lids, label them, and put them in the fridge. Check that the serving signs list the ingredients you wind up using.
