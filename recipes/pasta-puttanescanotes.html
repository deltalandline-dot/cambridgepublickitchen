Pasta Puttanesca Notes

Tags: Neither, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Notes from watching Dan make it.

Yield: ~50 servings, plus a supper leftover batch

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Start a 32 qt pot of water boiling at 9:30 — it takes a long time to boil, 1 ½ hrs even (our pasta insert pan isn't really big enough).
  2. Prep all ingredients.
  3. About 11:00: heat a large skillet (rondo?) over medium heat and add oil, garlic, anchovies, and chopped black olives. Sauté the mixture until the anchovies melt into the oil and completely dissolve and the garlic is tender, about 3 minutes — your kitchen never smelled so good! Add the olives and capers, sauté some more, then let it sit a while on very low or off to blend the flavors.
  4. If using: pan-fry chunks of extra-firm tofu (frozen/thawed worked best for consistency) — he had it in big chunks, though smaller pieces (or none) might be preferable.
  5. Add the tomatoes, oregano, Calabrian peppers, caper water, black pepper, and parsley. (Take out a no-tofu portion first if needed, then add the tofu to the rest.) Bring the sauce to a bubble, reduce heat, and simmer 8 to 10 minutes.
  6. Save some sauce out for the GF portion (and/or the no-soy portion, if using tofu).
  7. While it simmers, make 2 kinds of pasta (wheat for most, GF for however many need it). You can make the GF pasta by just pouring some of the clean boiling water over the GF pasta (or use tea water) and letting it sit about a minute.
  8. When the pasta is a couple of minutes from done (less done than al dente), don't just drain it — lift it out with a big ladle, including some water, and add it to the sauce. Add more pasta water to the sauce and try to keep it mixed (it's hard to really mix in the full rondo and it won't come to a full boil, so don't burn it) — the idea is this binds the starches in the pasta and pasta water with the sauce and makes it creamy.
  9. Garnish with parsley.
  10. Serve with Parmesan and vegan parm.
