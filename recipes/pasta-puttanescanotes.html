Pasta Puttanesca Notes

Tags: Neither, Nut-Free

Pasta Puttanesca a la Dan Landry

Notes from watching Dan make it

Ingredients: 

For ~50 + supper LO  

  • Lots of EVOO (coat rondo to 1/8 “ or more) 
  • ~40 cloves garlic, chopped in mini-cuis
     
  • ~1 ½  cups black olives cured in oil, chopped
  • Roland Foods Oil Cured Black Olives Pitted Whole Olives Specialty Imported Food 5-Pound Bag
    • (FAR LESS THAN THIS NEEDED…1lb or less plenty—actually would be fine with our kalamatas, maybe better even.
  • Calabrian peppers SEEDED and chopped 
  • Roland Foods Calabrian Chili Peppers in Vinegar, Specialty Imported Food, 17.5-Ounce Package
  • Oregano, fresh if we have:  3-4 long stems leaves chopped, (or probl 3-4 T dry)
  • ~2 ½  c capers SAVE CAPER LIQUID
  • Mix (lg) jar of caper liquid with equal parts? water
  • 12x14 oz cans crushed tomatoes
  • 3-4 c flat leaf parsley, chopped
    • Save some for garnish
  • 8lbs  spiral pasta, plus 1-2 lbs of GF as needed
  • Optional: pan fried chunks of tofu mixed in
  • Service with Parmesan cheese

Directions: 

🡪Start 32 qt pot of water boiling at 9:30 --takes long time to boil 1 ½ hrs even.  (Our pasta insert pan isn’t really big enough).

Prep all ingredients.

About 11:00:  Heat a large skillet (rondo??) over medium heat and add oil, garlic, anchovies, and chopped black olives??. Saute mixture until anchovies melt into oil and completely dissolve and garlic is tender, about 3 minutes: your kitchen never smelled so good! Add olives, capers. Saute some more, let it sit awhile on very low or off to blend flavors.

If using: pan fry chunks of x-firm tofu (we used frozen/thawed which was the best consistency)—he had it in big chunks, I might have liked smaller or none.

Add tomatoes, oregano, Calabrian peppers, caper water, black pepper, and parsley. [Take out a no-tofu portion if needed.  Add tofu to rest.]  Bring sauce to a bubble, reduce heat, and simmer 8 to 10 minutes. 

Save some sauce out for the GF portion (and/or maybe no-soy portion if using tofu)l

2.  While it simmers, make  2 kinds of pasta (wheat for most, gf for however many): 

You could make the GF pasta by just pouring some of the clean boiling water over the GF (or get tea water)….let is sit about a minute.

When the pasta is a couple of minutes from done (less done than al dente),  don’t just drain it, lift pasta out with big ladel including some water & ,add it to the sauce, Maybe add more pasta water to the sauce and [bring the whole thing back to boil—see next sentence],  Hard to really mix and get that effect in the full rondo, but do add the pasta water and try to keep it mixed it…it won’t really come to a boil,  don’t burn it, but the idea is this binds the starches in the pasta  & pasta water with the sauce & make it creamy.

Garnish with parsley.

Serve with Parmesan & vegan parm.
