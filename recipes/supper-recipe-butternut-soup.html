Butternut Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings (or ~36 for the smaller batch)

Equipment

  • ~20 qt soup pot (largest is ideal, but it's probably being used for lunch)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Sauté: sauté onions until transparent, then add garlic and cook briefly.
  2. Simmer: add the butternut squash, potatoes, thyme, and stock; bring to a boil and reduce to a simmer.
  3. When partly done, add the parsley.
  4. Finish: add lemon, salt, and pepper to taste.
  5. About noon: cover and turn off the fire. Test that it is 165°F — it should hold at ~145-165°F until supper. Check it again at 5:00 and reheat if needed, or transfer to the soup warmers and keep those on low all afternoon.
