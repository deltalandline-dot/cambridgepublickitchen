Sherry Shallot Vinaigrette FOG

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Sherry Shallot Vinaigrette

1 ½ qt   

 

1 ½ cup sherry vinegar

12 shallots 

1 t Dijon mustard

2  ¼ cup olive oil

2 ¼ cup canola oil

1 t salt

½ t white pepper

Thinly slice the shallots

Sauté the shallots in some of the olive oil.

Put vinegar, mustard, seasonings in the blender and blend.  Add the shallots with their oil and just pulse it a couple of times (we don’t want the shallots totally liquified..)

Transfer to a larger container and whisk in the rest of the oils.

Taste (drizzle a little into a separate spoon) and adjust salt, pepper or mustard.

For one meal: fill 2 small pitchers.  Serve with smallest ladles.
