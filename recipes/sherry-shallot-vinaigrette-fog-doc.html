Sherry Shallot Vinaigrette FOG

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~1.5 qts

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Thinly slice the shallots.
  2. Sauté the shallots in some of the olive oil.
  3. Put the vinegar, mustard, and seasonings in the blender and blend. Add the shallots with their oil and just pulse it a couple of times (we don't want the shallots totally liquified).
  4. Transfer to a larger container and whisk in the rest of the oils.
  5. Taste (drizzle a little into a separate spoon) and adjust salt, pepper, or mustard.
  6. For one meal: fill 2 small pitchers. Serve with the smallest ladles.
