Supper Dal Soup or Casserole

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: Soup ~15–16 quarts; Casserole fills two 2” deep hotel pans at least half full (for ~48)

Equipment

  • Soup: largest 32 qt stock pot
  • Casserole: two 2” deep hotel pans

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Soup: Mix it all up, bring to a boil, taste, turn off, and cover — it should keep hot for the afternoon. Serve with sides of leftover raita or yogurt, and leftover chutney or raisins.
  2. Casserole: Layer the food into the 2 pans: rice, veggies, dal. Serve with sides of leftover raita or yogurt, and leftover chutney or raisins.
