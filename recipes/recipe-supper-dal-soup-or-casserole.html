Supper Dal Soup or Casserole

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Day 5                      Supper Recipe:  Dal Soup or Casserole

SOUP:  use largest 32 qt stock pot, making ~15-16 qts

SOUP ingredients

What you have

Leftover curried cauliflower (if you made plain steamed, look at the curry recipe and add some of those spices and some frozen peas)

~4QTS

Leftover Dal

~3 med cans

Tomatoes with  juice

1 lg can

Coconut milk

broth

Water + Bouillon paste as needed to make total ~15-16 qts of soup

1-2 qts

Leftover rice

Instructions:  mix it all up, bring to a boil, taste, turn off, cover and should keep hot for the afternoon.  Serve with sides of leftover raita or yogurt, and leftover chutney or raisins.

CASSEROLE: fill two 2” deep hotel pans at least half full

CASSEROLE Ingredients

For ~48

Leftover dal (~ 4-5 quarts..if less than this, add a can of white or garbanzo beans.

Leftover cauliflower and peas (add more frozen peas if short). 

Mix veggies with 2 small cans of coconut milk.

Leftover rice (~ 4-5 quarts, if short, make some more on the stove)

If you added a lot of new unseasoned stuff, stir in 1-2 tsp curry powder.

Instructions: layer food into the 2 pans: rice, veggies, dal.  Serve with sides of leftover raita or yogurt, and leftover chutney or raisins.
