Sherry Shallot Vinaigrette Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Scale recipe: or ×

Recipe: Sherry Shallot Vinaigrette Salad Dressing                                       

Equipment  Vitamix, ~4qt plastic container & whisk, 2 pitchers

Ingredients

For 1 batch 

(~48 servs)

1 ½ cup

sherry vinegar

2 very large

shallots (like small onions..check onion area if we have any)

2 T

dijon mustard

2 ¼ cups

olive oil

2 ¼ cups

Light olive oil

1 tsp

salt

½ tsp

white pepper

Instructions

Remove outer papery skin & thinly slice the shallots

Sauté the shallots in some of the olive oil.

Put vinegar, mustard, seasonings in the blender and blend.  

Drizzle rest of oil into the running Vitamix and blend well.

Add the shallots with their oil and just pulse it a couple of times (we don’t want the shallots totally liquified..)

Taste and adjust salt & pepper if needed.

Divide between 2 small pitchers.

Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
