Sherry Shallot Vinaigrette Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: 1 batch (~48 servings)

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • ~4 qt plastic container & whisk
  • 2 pitchers

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Remove the outer papery skin & thinly slice the shallots.
  2. Sauté the shallots in some of the olive oil.
  3. Put the vinegar, mustard, and seasonings in the blender and blend.
  4. Drizzle the rest of the oil into the running Vitamix and blend well.
  5. Add the shallots with their oil and just pulse it a couple of times (we don't want the shallots totally liquified).
  6. Taste and adjust salt & pepper if needed.
  7. Divide between 2 small pitchers. Cover & store in the fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
