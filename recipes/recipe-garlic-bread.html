Garlic Bread

Tags: Vegan, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Equipment

  • Large trays

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Mix the oil, garlic, and herbs if using (these quantities are a guess — make more if needed).
  2. Put the mix on the needed number of regular gluten-free slices (do the GF slices first, to avoid contaminating the oil or utensils).
  3. Slice the bread in half lengthwise (separating the top half and bottom half).
  4. Spread both halves with the garlic oil mix.
  5. Put uncovered on large trays.
  6. (~11:30) Bake at 350° for about ½ hour, or until lightly browning (GF may take less time).
  7. Slice each baked half into about 1½" thick slices.
  8. Serve in a basket with a decorative cloth (bottom of the cloth rack).
