Garlic Bread

Tags: Vegan, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

ALT Week 2 Day 7       Recipe: Garlic Bread               

Equipment  large trays 

For Garlic Bread:

4-5 loaves of Sourdough or other “white” artisan bread

Olive oil (?1 cup to start?)  

~20 cloves of garlic minced in the mini cuisinart

Optional: dried basil and marjoram (~1T each)

Mix oil and garlic & herbs if using.  //these quantities are a guess, make more if needed

Put mix on the needed number of regular Gluten Free slices.

   (do the GF first to avoid contamination of oil or utensils)

Slice bread in half lengthwise (separating top half and bottom half)

Spread both with garlic oil mix.

Put uncovered on large trays.

About 11:30: Bake at 350 for ~½ hour or until lightly browning. (GF may take less..)

Slice each baked half into ~1 ½” thick slices.

Serve in a basket with a decorative cloth (bottom of cloth rack)
