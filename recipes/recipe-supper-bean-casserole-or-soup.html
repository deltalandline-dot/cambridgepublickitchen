Supper Bean Casserole Or Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Improvise casseroles or soup out of leftover tortilla bar ingredients — the ingredients below are potential ingredients, use what we have and be creative.

Yield: casserole fills 2 2” full-sized hotel pans; soup aims for ~14–18 quarts, depending on the size, age, and appetite of the group

Equipment

  • Large mixing bowl
  • 2 2” full-sized hotel pans with lids, or a 20 qt soup pot

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Casserole: Layer the casseroles with a light layer of rice and tortillas on the bottom, then mix together the beans, veggies, and rest. A layer of torn-up tortillas works well as a topping (we don't put cheese on top anymore, only serve it on the side).
  2. Soup: Add all the beans and veg to the soup pot, thin with bouillon (1 tsp Better than Bouillon to 1 cup water), and thicken with a little rice or torn-up tortillas if wanted.
  3. Serve either version with cheese and vegan cheese, and cilantro if we have enough.
