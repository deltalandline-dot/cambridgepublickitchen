Supper Bean Casserole Or Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Day 3                                     Supper Recipe:  Bean Casserole or Soup                           Pg. 1 of 1

Equipment: lg mixing bowl, 2 2” full-sized hotel pans with lids, or 20qt soup pot

The idea:

Improvise casseroles or soup out of leftover tortilla bar ingredients. 

Ingredients (potential ingredients, use what we have & be creative)

For Casserole:

Leftover rice (&maybe quinoa and/or corn tortillas torn up.  Need only a thin layer of grain (maybe 3 qts total). 

Leftover pinto beans (if less than 4 qts, open some cans of black beans to have this much)

Leftover veggies of any kind (add more frozen corn or frozen green beans to make at least 3qts veggies)

Leftover salsa, or open another jar if needed to make it moist and tasty

Could spice up if needed with salt, pepper, ground cumin, garlic powder etc. (usually only needed if you used a lot of new plain ingredients).

For Soup: 

Any/all of the above, but very light on the rice or quinoa & tortillas.

Add bouillon to bring to a soup consistency.

Could add more canned tomatoes, but probably not needed.

Aim for 14-18 quarts, depending on the size, age & appetite of the group.

Instructions

Casserole Suggestion:  layer the casseroles with a light layer of rice and tortillas on the bottom, then mix together then beans, vegs & rest.  A layer of torn up tortillas works well as a topping (we don’t put cheese on top anymore, only serve on the side).

Soup: add all beans & veg to soup pot, thin with bouillon (1 tsp BtB to 1 cup water), thicken with a little rice or torn up tortillas if wanted.

Serve either version with cheese & vegan cheese, cilantro if we have enough.
