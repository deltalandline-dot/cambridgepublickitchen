Cashew Green Chili Cream(1)

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Goes well with the "Blended Black Bean Soup" recipe. For a simpler alternative: add some lemon juice and salt (both to taste) to a carton of vegan cashew yogurt, and add green chilies if you like and there are no chili allergies.

Yield: ~48-50 servings (smaller amounts in parentheses below are for a vegan-only batch)

Equipment

  • Vitamix

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Soak the cashews as long as you can (longer is smoother, but grainy is fine).
  2. Drain them.
  3. Add all ingredients to the Vitamix blender (works better than the Robocoupe for this). Blend until smooth.
  4. Taste and adjust seasonings until you like it.
