Cashew Green Chili Cream

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

                              Recipe: Cashew Green Chili Cream                            Page 1 of 1                               

Equipment Vitamix

Ingredients

Goes well with “Blended Black Bean Soup” recipe.

~48-50  

4 ½ cups

Cashews, soaked in water as long as overnight, or as short as needed.

2 cups

Water (fresh, for blending, not the soaking water)

5-6 sm cans

*Diced green chilis, drained (or start with a bit less water)

¼ cup 

Lime juice

6

Garlic cloves (blending will chop)

1 tsp 

salt

½ tsp

Ground cumin

Pinch (> ⅛ tsp)

Cayenne (if using mild chilis)

Instructions:

Soak cashews as long as you can (longer is smoother, but grainy is fine).

Drain them.

Add all ingredients to Vitamix blender (works better than Robocoupe for this.  Blend until smooth.

Taste and fiddle with seasonings until you like it!

*Confession:  just guessing on the quantity of green chilis, I used about ¾ cups of a tastier, hotter, fancier bottled brand from New Mexico...
