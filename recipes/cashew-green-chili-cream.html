Cashew Green Chili Cream

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Goes well with the "Blended Black Bean Soup" recipe.

Yield: ~48-50 servings

Equipment

  • Vitamix

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Soak cashews as long as you can (longer is smoother, but grainy is fine).
  2. Drain them.
  3. Add all ingredients to Vitamix blender (works better than Robocoupe for this). Blend until smooth.
  4. Taste and fiddle with seasonings until you like it!
  5. Note on the green chilis: the quantity is a guess — the original cook used about ¾ cup of a tastier, hotter, fancier bottled brand from New Mexico.
