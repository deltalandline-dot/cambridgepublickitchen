Instructions

Set-up and prep

Preheat both ovens to 350.

Slice each package in half across the center, then each half piece through the thickness dimension to make two thin patties.  That is, if the half-piece is ~½ “ thick rectangle, the resulting patties will be ¼” thick rectangles.  Tip: maybe use 2 white scrapers to hold the piece safely on its side (the KM will demonstrate, probably the morning yogi will do it)

Mix marinade & start on prepping sides

Mix the marinade:  lite olive oil, sesame oil, rice vin. & tamari & water.  Handy to use a large plastic pitcher.

Layout the slices in the flat on the baking sheets, not overlapping.  Pour the marinade over the slices.

Put the pans in the ovens about 10:45.  Continue working on sides & the veg.

After about half an hour (~11:15), turn over all the tempehs.  Add water or more marinade if it is getting dry.

We are now serving the cheese on the side, see “fixings” page.
