Tempeh Ruebens and Fixings

Tags: Vegetarian, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Equipment

  • 2 large baking sheets for tempeh
  • Pitcher or bowl to mix sauce

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Note (10/2025): the steaming step was removed — experiments showed it's not needed.
  2. Set-up and prep: Preheat both ovens to 350°.
  3. Slice each package in half across the center, then each half piece through the thickness dimension to make two thin patties. That is, if the half-piece is ~½" thick, the resulting patties will be ¼" thick rectangles. Tip: maybe use 2 white scrapers to hold the piece safely on its side (the KM will demonstrate, probably the morning yogi will do it).
  4. Mix marinade & start on prepping sides: Mix the marinade — lite olive oil, sesame oil, rice vinegar, tamari, and water. Handy to use a large plastic pitcher.
  5. Layout the slices flat on the baking sheets, not overlapping. Pour the marinade over the slices.
  6. (~10:45) Put the pans in the ovens. Continue working on sides & the veg.
  7. (~11:15) After about half an hour, turn over all the tempehs. Add water or more marinade if it is getting dry.
  8. We are now serving the cheese on the side, see "fixings" below.
  9. Set-up sides: Make the Russian dressing (just mix the Veganaise, ketchup, and horseradish — see ingredients list).
  10. Set up sauerkraut (drain), mustard (serve in the jar), chips, and cheese.
  11. Layout the rye bread on the large baking sheets, overlapping the pieces so it fits on maybe 1 full and 1 half-sized baking sheet. Don't cover it so that it more toasts than steams. Put it in about 11:30 and check it frequently.
