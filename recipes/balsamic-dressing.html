Balsamic Dressing

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Instructions for a double recipe (makes two batches at once).

Yield: doubled recipe — 2 batches, each filling ~2 small pitchers

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • Second 4 quart container with lid
  • Small skillet (for poaching garlic, if making that version)
  • 2 small pitchers

Ingredients (for ONE container — you will put this much in each one)

Preparation

  1. Gather all ingredients, the Vitamix container, and a second 4-quart container with lid.
  2. You will double this recipe by putting a set of the ingredients in each container. It's easiest to add all ingredients except the oil to both containers, then move to the next ingredient.
  3. If time allows: put 12 cloves of fresh garlic in a small skillet, cover with about ½ cup of olive oil, and heat over medium heat, watching closely until they sizzle and just begin to turn a bit brown around the edges. Remove the skillet from the stove and set aside. (If there's no time, you can use powdered garlic instead — fresh unpoached garlic is too strong for many people.)
  4. Add the above ingredients except the oil to each container.
  5. Blend the contents of the Vitamix container, slowly drizzling in 4 ¼ cups of olive oil (4 ½ cups if using powdered garlic) through the lid.
  6. Fill 2 small pitchers with it.
  7. Pour the second set of ingredients into the Vitamix container and mix it, again slowly drizzling in the other batch of olive oil.
  8. Pour the mixed dressing back into the mixing container, cover, label, and put in the fridge.
