Balsamic Dressing

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Scale recipe: or ×

Balsamic Vinaigrette (instructions for double recipe)

  • Gather all ingredients below, the Vitamix container and a second 4 quart container with lid.
  • You will double this recipe by putting a set of the below ingredients in each container.  It’s easiest to add all ingredients except the oil to both containers, then go to the next ingredient.
  • If time: put 12 cloves of fresh garlic in the small skillet, cover with about ½ cup of olive oil and heat over medium heat, watching closely until they sizzle and just begin to turn a bit brown around the ediges.  Remove skillet from stove and set aside. [If not time, we can use powdered garlic.  Fresh unpoached is too strong for many people.]

Ingredients for ONE Container (you will put this much in each one)

1 ½  Cups Balsamic vinegar

1T sherry vinegar

1 T Dijon mustard

1 tsp black pepper

½  tsp honey

½  tsp salt

6 poached garlic cloves with half their cooking oil (see above) (or ¾ tsp garlic powder)

Drizzle this into the Vitamix container slowly as you mix each batch:

4 ¼    Cups Olive Oil (make it 4 ½ if you use powdered garlic)

  • Add the above ingredients except the oil to each container.
  • Blend the contents of the Vitamix container using the Vitamix, slowly drizzling in the 4 ½ cups of olive oil through the lid.
  • Fill 2 small pitchers with it.  
  • Pour second set of ingredients into the Vitamix container and mix it, again slowly drizzling in the other 4 ½ cups of olive oil.
  • Pour the mixed dressing back in the mixing container, cover, label and put in the fridge.
