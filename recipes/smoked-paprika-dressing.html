Smoked Paprika Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings (1 batch)

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • Juicer
  • 2 pitchers

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Juice limes in the juicer next to the rice cooker.
  2. Add all ingredients except the oil to the Vitamix container and mix on low.
  3. Drizzle the oil into the Vitamix while mixing. Gradually increase speed until fully emulsified. Divide between 2 small pitchers.
  4. Cover and store in the fridge (if not for today, label and date it too).
