Smoked Paprika Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Recipe: Smoked Paprika Vinaigrette                         

Equipment  Vitamix, juicer, 2 pitchers

Ingredients

For 1 batch 

(~48 servs)

2 ½ cups

Red wine vinegar

1 cup 

agave

¼ cup

Dijon Mustard

¼ cup

Lime Juice

2 T

Smoked Paprika

2 T

Oregano 

2 cloves

Garlic

½ cup

Chopped Shallot

3 cup

olive oil

1 T

Pepper

1 T

salt

Instructions

Juice limes in juicer next to rice cooker

Add all ingredients  except oil in vitamix container and mix on low.

Drizzle oil into Vitamix mixing while you do.  Gradually increase speed until all emulsified.    Divide between 2 small pitchers.

Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
