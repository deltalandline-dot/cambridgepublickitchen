Hollyhock Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings (1 batch)

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • 2 pitchers

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Add all vinegar, water, tamari, garlic and the first 3 cups of canola oil to the vitamix container and mix on low.
  2. Add nutritional yeast and blend in thoroughly.
  3. Drizzle remaining oil into Vitamix, mixing while you do. Gradually increase speed until all emulsified. Divide between 2 small pitchers.
  4. Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
