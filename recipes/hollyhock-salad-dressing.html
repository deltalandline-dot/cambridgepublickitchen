Hollyhock Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Recipe: Hollyhock Salad Dressing                                       

Equipment  Vitamix, 2 pitchers

Ingredients

For 1 batch 

(~48 servs)

¾  cup

apple cider vinegar

¾ cup

water

¾ cup

tamari

9 cloves (or 2 tsp)

garlic cloves (or powder)

1  cup

nutritional yeast (yellow stuff in pantry near oatmeal)

3 cups + 3 ½  cups

light olive oil (added in 2 batches)

Instructions

Add all vinegar, water, tamari, garlic and first 3 cups of canola oil to vitamix container and mix on low.

Add nutritional yeast and blend in thoroughly.

Drizzle remaining oil into Vitamix mixing while you do.  Gradually increase speed until all emulsified.    Divide between 2 small pitchers.

Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
