Blue Cheese Dressing

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Recipe: Blue Cheese Dressing                                       

Equipment  Vitamix, 2 pitchers

Ingredients //Note:  you will need to put some non-dairy leftover type out for no-dairy

For 1 batch 

(~48 servs)

4 x 5oz tubs

Or 3x8oz (TJs)

Crumbled blue cheese (quality matters..)

3 cups

milk

½ cup

Lemon juice

½  tsp

Salt (depends on brand of cheese...add slowly & taste)

1 tsp

White pepper

Instructions

Add all in vitamix container and mix on low.

Add all to Vitamix. Gradually increase speed until all emulsified.    Divide between 2 small pitchers.

Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).

-->Good to make early because it gets thicker as it sits.
