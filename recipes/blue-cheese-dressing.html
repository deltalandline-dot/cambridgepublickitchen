Blue Cheese Dressing

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Note: put out some non-dairy leftovers or an alternative for anyone avoiding dairy, since this dressing itself isn't dairy-free.

Yield: ~48 servings (1 batch)

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • 2 pitchers

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Add everything to the Vitamix container and mix on low.
  2. Gradually increase speed until fully emulsified. Divide between 2 small pitchers.
  3. Cover and store in the fridge (if not using today, label and date it too).
  4. Good to make early, since it gets thicker as it sits.
