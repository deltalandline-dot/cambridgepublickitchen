Thai Yellow Curry W2

Tags: Neither, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings (or ~36 if not doing a supper leftover batch)

Equipment

  • Rondo
  • 10-12 qt stockpot

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. ***Note: this full recipe is enough for a full rondo plus ~8-10 qts in one of the smaller stockpots. It works to get down to the *** step below in the rondo, then separate out the 2 quantities and proportionally divide up any additional vegetables. Then you can set aside the other pot to use for the leftover supper.
  2. Pre-Roast (Eggplant & maybe others): If using eggplant, pre-roast it to lose bitterness & help it absorb sauce better. If there's room in the oven, it's tasty to pre-roast some other hard veggies like cauliflower, carrots, zucchini, yams (not broccoli).
  3. Roast Tofu: See separate recipe — we usually serve this separately, both for diets and for table interest.
  4. Sauté spices: Add oil to the rondo, heat to medium/high, and add onions; sauté until translucent. Add garlic, ginger, curry paste, and turmeric, and cook for a minute or two until fragrant. Taste the sauce & adjust spices if needed.
  5. Add veggies: Once onions and spice mix are ready, add coconut milk. Try not to let it fully boil once you add the coconut — it will separate (still edible if it does, but not ideal). Add veggies hardest first, tenderest last, something like: (~10:30) raw carrots, yams, squash, potatoes, peppers, zucchini, roasted eggplant (*** see above); (~11:40) raw cauliflower and/or other pre-roasted veggies; (~11:50) raw broccoli if having any.
  6. Note from Thina (for a possibly better version): use only 1 big coconut milk and maybe 2 small cans, plus 1 jar of Better Than Bouillon. Use 1 small can of coconut milk as the oil to melt the curry paste (don't worry about oil separating). Then add all the hard veggies, including onions, and add the rest of the coconut milk and Better Than Bouillon, then the rest of the softer veggies. She only roasted the eggplants, and you can use whatever veggies you wish. When the veggies are cooked, it's good to go!
