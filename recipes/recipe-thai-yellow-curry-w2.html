Instructions (if you wish: see note at the bottom from Thina for a better? version)

***Note:  This full recipe is enough for a full rondo plus ~8-10 qts in one of the smaller stock-pots.  It works to get down to the *** step below in the rondo, then separate out the 2 quantities and proportionally divide up any additional vegetables. Then you can set aside the other pot to use for the leftover supper.

Pre-Roast

Eggplant & maybe others

If using eggplant, pre-roast it to lose bitterness & help it absorb sauce better.  

If room in the oven, it’s tasty to pre-roast some other hard veggies

like cauliflower, carrots, zucchini, yams (not broccoli).

Roast

Tofu

See separate recipe, we usually serve this separately, both for diets and for table interest.

Saute spices

Add oil to rondo, heat to medium/high and add onions and saute until translucent.  Add garlic, ginger, curry paste and turmeric and cook for a minute or two until fragrant.   Taste sauce & adjust spices if needed.

Add

Veggies

Once onions and spice mix is ready, add coconut milk.

Try not to let it fully boil once you add the coconut, it will separate

(still edible if it does, but not ideal).

Add veggies hardest first, tenderest last, something like:

~10:30 Raw carrots, yams, squash, potatoes, peppers, zucchini, roasted eggplant (*** see above)

~11:40 Raw cauliflower and/or other pre-roasted veggies

~11:50 Raw broccoli if having any

Thina recommends: use only 1 big coco milk and maybe 2 small cans. and 1 jar better than bouillon. Use 1 small can coco as oil to melt the curry paste (don’t worry about oil separating). Then you can add all hard veggies, including onions, and add rest of coco milk and better than bouillon and then rest of softer veggies. I only roasted the eggplants. And you can have whatever veggies you wish. When veggies are cooked, it’s good to go!
