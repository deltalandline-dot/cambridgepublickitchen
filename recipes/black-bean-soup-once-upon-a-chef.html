Black Bean Soup - Once Upon a Chef

Tags: Neither, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: 4 servings (about 30 minutes total)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Heat the olive oil over medium heat in a large soup pan. Add the onions, garlic cloves, and carrots and cook, stirring occasionally, until onions are soft and translucent, about 8 minutes. Do not brown.
  2. Add the black beans, chicken broth, oregano, coriander, cumin, cayenne pepper, and salt and bring to a boil. Reduce the heat, cover, and simmer gently for about 15 minutes.
  3. Purée the soup using a hand-held immersion blender until very smooth and creamy. Stir in the lime juice and season with salt and pepper to taste.
  4. Ladle the soup into bowls and top each bowl with a dollop of sour cream and freshly chopped cilantro.
  5. Note: if using a standard blender instead of an immersion blender, purée the soup in batches, being careful not to fill the jar more than halfway. Leave the lid's steam vent open and loosely cover with a dish towel to let the heat escape. Pour the blended soup into a clean pot.
