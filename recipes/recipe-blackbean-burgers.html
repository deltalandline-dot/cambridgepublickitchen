Week 2 Day 7                              Recipe: Blackbean Burgers            Page 2 of 2

Instructions

EARLY:

Maybe at 6:40 …set the shitakes to soak in hot (tea machine) water

[If you don’t have canned or pre-made beans, start the black beans and plan to stay with them until done or turn off for sit.]

~9:30 Prep

Ingredients

Mix the vegan egg per package instructions

Strain canned beans saving liquid in case needed (probably not)

Lightly pulse the oats in Robocoup (using the clear plastic setup) (5-6 quick pulses)

Squeeze shitakes dry, trim hard stems, chop in mini-cuisinart, or Robocoup or by hand until finely chopped, not all the way to paste.

Mince onions & garlic (by hand or in mini-cuis)

Chop parsley 

Mix

Roughly mix all ingredients in a large bowl

In batches as it fits, pulse mixture in Robocoupe–not all the way to a puree, leaving some rough texture.  If needed, add some bean liquid or more oats to get a good texture for forming into patties.

Shape

Scoop out 1/3 cup portions and shape by hand (wearing gloves) into burger patties. Make them on the thin side (~1/4 “), they are dense.

Let patties sit about 5 minutes before baking.

Bake

At 350 for 15 minutes, flip over and bake 15 mins on other side.

?Cheese

Maybe add cheese on the diet-appropriate quantity for last few mins.  A popular option with the dairy eaters. Or serve on side.

We do now have sliced vegan cheese to put out, but leave several plain.
