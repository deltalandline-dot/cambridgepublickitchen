Blackbean Burgers

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Using ⅓ cup portions, the larger recipe made 66 burgers — quite a few people ate 2. The smaller batch (~⅔ recipe) made 46, which was way too many for 36 light eaters! You might want to cut these down further for light eaters or fewer people.

Yield: ~50 servings = 66 burgers (or ~36 servings using the smaller quantities below = 46 burgers)

Equipment

  • Big bowl
  • Robocoupe
  • Mini-cuisinart
  • Sheet pans

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Early: maybe at 6:40, set the shiitakes to soak in hot (tea machine) water. If you don't have canned or pre-made beans, start the black beans and plan to stay with them until done, or turn off for a sit.
  2. ~9:30 prep ingredients: mix the vegan egg per package instructions.
  3. Strain canned beans, saving the liquid in case needed (probably not).
  4. Lightly pulse the oats in the Robocoupe (using the clear plastic setup), 5-6 quick pulses.
  5. Squeeze the shiitakes dry, trim the hard stems, and chop in mini-cuisinart, Robocoupe, or by hand until finely chopped, not all the way to a paste.
  6. Mince the onions and garlic (by hand or in mini-cuis).
  7. Chop the parsley.
  8. Mix: roughly mix all ingredients in a large bowl.
  9. In batches as it fits, pulse the mixture in the Robocoupe — not all the way to a puree, leaving some rough texture. If needed, add some bean liquid or more oats to get a good texture for forming into patties.
  10. Shape: scoop out 1/3 cup portions and shape by hand (wearing gloves) into burger patties. Make them on the thin side (~¼"), they are dense. Let patties sit about 5 minutes before baking.
  11. Bake: at 350 for 15 minutes, flip over, and bake 15 minutes on the other side.
  12. Cheese (optional): maybe add cheese, in the diet-appropriate quantity, for the last few minutes. A popular option with the dairy eaters, or serve on the side. We do now have sliced vegan cheese to put out, but leave several plain.
