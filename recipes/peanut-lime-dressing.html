Peanut Lime Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free

                       Peanut Lime Dressing                    page 1 of 1

NOTE: This is intended to be served on the side with the Thai Salad for first day supper.

Equipment  Vitamix, bowl

Ingredients

For ~48

2 ¼ c

Peanut butter

2c

water

1 ¾ c 

Lime juice (10-12 limes), keep zest of ~4 (ok to top off with lemons if limes are short or not juicy)

¾ c

Rice vinegar

3 tsp

Sesame oil

½ cup

agave

½ cup

tamari

6 cloves

Garlic (vitamix will chop)

1 T

Ginger (rough chop, vitamix will fine chop)                                                                                    

1 ½ cups

Light olive oil

Instructions:

Mix peanut butter and water in a bowl with a whisk.

Add that and all else except light oil to the Vitamix, mix.

Drizzle in oil while mixing to emulsify.

Taste & adjust balance of agave, tamari, vinegar, water..
