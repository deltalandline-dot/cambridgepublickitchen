Peanut Lime Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

This is intended to be served on the side with the Thai Salad for first-day supper.

Yield: ~48 servings

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • Bowl

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Mix peanut butter and water in a bowl with a whisk.
  2. Add that and everything else, except the light oil, to the Vitamix and mix.
  3. Drizzle in the oil while mixing to emulsify.
  4. Taste and adjust the balance of agave, tamari, vinegar, and water.
