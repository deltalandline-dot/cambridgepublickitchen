Thin Mint Cookies

Tags: Vegan, Dairy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: makes 65 cookies

Ingredients

  • Chocolate Coating (coats about half the cookies, ~32):

Preparation

  1. Cookies: In a food processor, pulse flour, sugar, cocoa powder, salt, and baking soda until combined. Add margarine, milk, vanilla, and mint extract. Pulse a few more times until the mixture comes together.
  2. Transfer the mixture to a large bowl and knead it with your hands until it comes together and all the flour is incorporated. Chill the dough in the refrigerator for 1 hour.
  3. Preheat the oven to 350°F and line two large baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone baking mats.
  4. Remove the dough from the refrigerator. Roll a heaping teaspoon of cookie dough into a ball and place onto the prepared baking sheets, leaving about 3 inches between each ball.
  5. Evenly flatten the dough with your fingertips so that it is about ¼ inch thick and bake for 12-14 minutes. Let cool completely.
  6. Chocolate Coating: Melt the chocolate chips and margarine in a double boiler or microwave. Stir in the mint extract and mix until smooth. Dip the completely cooled cookies into the chocolate.
