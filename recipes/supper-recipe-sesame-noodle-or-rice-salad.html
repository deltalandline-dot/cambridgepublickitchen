Sesame Noodle or Rice Salad

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

KM has 2 choices here: rice or noodles, peanut or sesame/ginger dressing. (Consider nut allergies.)

Yield: ~48 servings

Equipment

  • Rice Cooker or 20qt w/ pasta insert
  • Large sauce pan
  • Large skillet
  • Vitamix
  • Huge salad bowl to mix
  • 2nd-largest bowl to serve

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Make Rice or Noodles: Easiest to do the day before if not using a rice cooker. If using noodles, do it on the day in the 20qt pasta cooker insert — add some sesame oil to the boiling water, then drain & rinse with cold water.
  2. Make Dressing: Also easy to do the day before. Choose either Sesame Peanut Dressing or Sesame Ginger Dressing. Consider peanut allergies — maybe save some undressed salad & toss with simple oil, rice vinegar & ginger.
  3. Prep Veggies: Cut up veggies as specified in the ingredients list. Steam just a little, still slightly crunchy: carrots, broccoli stems, asparagus. Leave raw: snap or snow peas, frozen peas, cucumber.
  4. Prep Seeds: Using a large skillet, spread out a total of 1 cup white sesame seeds. Put on a low flame and watch carefully. Remove when just lightly brown. Some go in the dressing, some in the salad.
  5. Toss: Toss all veggies, grain, and (setting aside a nut-allergy portion?) dressing in one of the super-huge salad bowls. Transfer to the next-largest bowl to serve. Cover, refrigerate & re-toss before serving. Ideal to take out of the fridge at 3:50 & let it come nearer to room temp.
