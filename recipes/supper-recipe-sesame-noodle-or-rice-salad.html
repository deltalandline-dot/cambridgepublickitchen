Sesame Noodle or Rice Salad

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free

Day 6                Supper Recipe:  Sesame Rice (or Noodle) Salad             page 1 of 2

Equipment:  Rice Cooker or 20qt w/ pasta insert, Large sauce pan, lg skillet, Vitamix, huge salad bowl to mix, 2nd largest to serve

KM has 2 choices here: rice or noodles, peanut or sesame/ginger dressing.

(Consider nut allergies.)

Ingredients:

For ~48

EITHER:

6 cups raw

Short grain brown rice (assume cooked the day before)

OR:

5 boxes Annie Chun brown-rice noodles

3lbs 

Asparagus (cut into 1” pieces), blanched

~2lbs 

Snap peas or snow peas (cut in half or thirds), raw

3lbs 

Carrots, Small dice, blanched (or can use 2-3cups shredded)

Broc. stems

saved broccoli stems, small dice, blanched

1 pack peas

Frozen (use raw, or could chop of green beans if no peas).

2

Whole cucumbers, small dice

4 bunches

Scallions sliced small

¾  cup

Toasted white sesame seeds (& more in dressing see below)

Dressing:  choose either Sesame Ginger or Peanut Sesame (filed under salad dressings)  

Optional sides:  

2 little jars

Pickled ginger

Mixed herbs:

Bowl of chopped cilantro & mint mixed with a little oil.

Instructions 

Make Rice or 

Noodles

Easiest to do the day before if not using Rice Cooker then.

If using noodles, do on the day in 20qt pasta cooker insert.  Add some sesame oil to the boiling water. Drain & rinse with cold water. 

Make Dressing

Also easy to do the day before.  Choose either one of:  Sesame Peanut Dressing or Sesame Ginger Dressing.

CONSIDER PEANUT ALLERGIES--maybe save some undressed salad & toss with simple oil, rice vinegar & ginger.

Prep Veggies

Cut up veggies as specified in ingredients list.

Steam just a little, still slightly crunchy: carrots, broccoli stems, asparagus

Leave raw: snap or snow peas, frozen peas, cucumber.

Prep Seeds

Using large skillet, spread out total of 1 cup white sesame seeds. Put on low flame and watch carefully.  Remove when just lightly brown.  Some go in dressing, some in salad.

Toss

Toss all veggies, grain and (setting aside a nut allergy portion?) dressing in one of the super-huge salad bowls.  Transfer to next-largest to serve.  Cover, refrigerate & re-toss before serving.  Ideal to take out of fridge at 3:50 & let it come nearer to room temp.
