Preparation

  1. If there isn't enough left to make something tasty, first check the freezer for suitable leftovers to use, or make a simple soup out of what we have (or run out and get ingredients for).
  2. Spray the pans.
  3. Layer "as you like it." Simple suggestion: crumble the tofu and mix with the veggies and gravy, then top with mashed potatoes.
  4. Option: make a smaller dish with no cheese, sprinkling maybe parmesan on top to give it some browning and more flavor.
