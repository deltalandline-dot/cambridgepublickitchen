Supper Shepards Pie

Tags: Vegetarian, Nut-Free

Supper Recipe: Shepard’s Pie

Equipment: 2 2” deep full sized hotel pans with lids

Ingredients

Left over mashed potatoes, chicken-fried tofu, green beans, gravy

Optional supplements: frozen or other leftover veggies if needed, bouillon if needed, rice, cans of white beans to supplement tofu?

If there isn’t enough left to make something tasty out of, FIRST: check freezer for suitable leftovers to use, or make a simple soup out of what we have or can run out & get ingredients for.

Instructions

Spray pans

Layer “As You Like It”.  Simple Suggestion: crumble tofu & mix with veggies & gravy, top with mashed potatoes

Option: making a smaller dish with no cheese and sprinkling maybe parmesan on top to give it some browning & more flavor.
