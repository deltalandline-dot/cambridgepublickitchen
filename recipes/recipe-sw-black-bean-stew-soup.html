Instructions

Saute 

Heat olive oil in stock pot and add onions.  Saute until translucent.  

Add Ingredients

Add celery, chili powder, oregano, and cumin, paprika to saute mixture and cook for a few minutes.

Add tomatoes, bring to a boil and lower to a simmer. 

Simmer for at least half an hour.

Add peppers, zucchini, beans and water as needed to make the quantity desired.  Bring to boil, simmer until time for last step.

**Consider separating out some into a smaller stock pot as a guarantee of leftovers, and because this gets very full..

Finish

Add corn & green chilies about 20 mins before serving.       

Add salt as needed.                                              

Serve with cheddar, cilantro & sour cream.
