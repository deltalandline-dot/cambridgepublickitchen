SW Black Bean Stew Soup

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~50 servings, enough for 2 meals (or use the smaller quantities, ¼ less, for ~36 servings)

Equipment

  • 32 qt (largest) stock pot, maybe a 2nd stock pot for excess
  • Mini Cuisinart

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Sauté: Heat olive oil in the stock pot and add the onions. Sauté until translucent.
  2. Add ingredients: Add the celery, chili powder, oregano, cumin, and paprika to the sauté mixture and cook for a few minutes.
  3. Add the tomatoes, bring to a boil, and lower to a simmer. Simmer for at least half an hour.
  4. Add the peppers, zucchini, beans, and water as needed to make the quantity desired. Bring to a boil, and simmer until time for the last step. Consider separating out some into a smaller stock pot as a guarantee of leftovers, and because this gets very full.
  5. Finish: Add the corn and green chilies about 20 minutes before serving. Add salt as needed.
  6. Serve with cheddar, cilantro, and sour cream.
