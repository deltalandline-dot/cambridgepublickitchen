Sesame Ginger Dressing

Sesame Ginger Dressing          page 1 of 1

Equipment:  Vitamix, bowl

Ingredients

For a pre-tossed salad for 48

As a pre-tossed salad, either the supper noodle or rice, or a slaw.

Double these quantities for a self-serve dressing.

1 ¼ c

Lime Juice+zest of 2-3 limes, OR Rice Vinegar OR mixture

1 Tbls

Grated ginger (good to use the microplane)

½ cup

ginger, chopped in mini-cuisinart

6 Tbls

Tamari

3 Tbls

agave

3 Tbls

Sesame oil

6 cloves

Garlic, minced in mini-cuis OR 1 ½ tsp garlic powder

2Tbls

Siracha sauce

¼ cup

Toasted white sesame seeds (& more in salad, see above)

½ to 1Tbls

Salt (add ½ T & see...probably need more in final salad)

2 cups

Light olive oil

Instructions:

Blend all but oil in Vitamix.  Drizzle in oil & blend again.
