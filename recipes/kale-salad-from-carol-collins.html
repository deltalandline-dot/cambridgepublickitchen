Kale Salad from Carol Collins

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: 4 servings (as a main dish)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Pull kale leaves off the tough stems and discard stems. Chop the kale and the cabbage into small, bite-sized pieces.
  2. Chop the apple into small, bite-sized pieces and add to a small bowl of cold water with about 1 T of lemon juice.
  3. Transfer kale and cabbage to a large bowl. Sprinkle a small amount of sea salt or kosher salt and massage the leaves with your hands by lightly scrunching big handfuls at a time, until the leaves are darker in color and fragrant.
  4. Add the cranberries and walnuts. Drain the apple and add to the mix.
  5. Crumble the goat cheese over the top.
  6. In a small bowl, whisk the dressing ingredients together vigorously and pour over the salad. Toss until the salad is evenly coated with dressing.
  7. Allow the salad to marinate for an hour or so before serving.
