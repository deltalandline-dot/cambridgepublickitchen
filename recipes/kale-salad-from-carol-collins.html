Kale Salad from Carol Collins

Kale Salad from Carol Collins

 

½ cup lightly roasted walnuts

8 oz. kale (I prefer the smooth Lacinato)

1 cup red cabbage

½ cup dried cranberries

1 medium Granny Smith apple

2 oz. soft goat cheese, chilled

 

Dressing:

3 T olive or canola oil

1½ T apple cider vinegar

2 teas rice vinegar

1 T smooth Dijon mustard

1½ teas honey

 

Pull kale leaves off from the tough stems and discard stems.  Chop the kale and the cabbage into small, bite-sized pieces

 

Chop the apple into small, bite-sized pieces and add to small bowl of cold water with about 1 T of lemon juice. 

 

Transfer to a large bowl.  Sprinkle small amount of sea salt or kosher salt and massage the leaves with your hands by lightly scrunching big handfuls at a time, until the leaves are darker in color and fragrant.

 

Add the cranberries and walnuts.  Drain the apple and add to mix.

Crumble the goat cheese over the top.

 

In a small bowl, whisk the dressing ingredients together vigorously and pour over the salad.   Toss until the salad is evenly coated with dressing.

 

Allow the salad to marinate for an hour or so before serving.

                       

                                                  `   Serves 4 as main dish
