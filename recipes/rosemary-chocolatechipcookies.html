Rosemary-Chocolate Chip Cookies

Tags: Vegan, Dairy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: 15 cookies

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°.
  2. Beat together coconut oil and rosemary, then add sugars and beat well.
  3. Add mylk and flaxseed and beat again for 30 seconds or so.
  4. Add about half of the flour and the remaining dry ingredients, and mix well.
  5. Scoop about 2 T portions of cookie dough and gently flatten on a cookie sheet.
  6. Bake 10-12 minutes until bottoms are golden brown.
