Broccoli Salad

Tags: Neither, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

From Trish, Mom On Timeout.

Yield: 12 servings

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Whisk together mayonnaise, sugar, red wine vinegar, and celery seed in a small bowl. Place in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Combine broccoli, bacon, toasted almonds, green onions, celery, and grapes in a large serving dish.
  3. (To toast almonds, place on a large baking sheet and bake at 350°F for 3-5 minutes, stirring in between. Do not walk away — they burn easily. The almonds are toasted when they turn light brown.)
  4. Toss the dressing with the rest of the salad ingredients and stir.
  5. Dressing can be added at the last minute if desired.
