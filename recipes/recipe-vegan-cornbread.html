Vegan Cornbread

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48-50 servings

Equipment

  • 4 half-sized 2" hotel pans (cooks more evenly than 2 full-size)
  • 2 large mixing bowls
  • 2 small mixing bowls

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 375°. Grease pans. Mix v-egg or ground flax and WARM water separately, and let set for 5-10 minutes to thicken (will need to whisk the v-egg). Mix the alternative milk of choice and vinegar and let set while preparing the other ingredients.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, mix the dry ingredients: cornmeal, GF flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in one bowl.
  3. In a separate mixing bowl, mix together the oil, pumpkin, sweetener, "milk" mixture, and flax mixture.
  4. Leave wets and drys separate until just ready to go in the oven. Mix just until combined, being careful not to overmix.
  5. Place mixture into 4 half-sized 2-inch-deep hotel pans (stored on the island near the pantry door). Bake for 40-50 minutes, or until golden brown and cooked in the center. Test by patting for firmness & checking that a toothpick or knife comes out clean. Start checking after 30 minutes.
