Tarragon Dressing

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Instructions below are for making a double batch.

Equipment

  • Vitamix container
  • A second 2 or 4 quart container with lid

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Gather all ingredients below, the Vitamix container, and a second 2 or 4 quart container with lid.
  2. You will double this recipe by putting a set of the ingredients in each container — it's easiest to add each ingredient to both containers, then move on to the next ingredient.
  3. Rinse 2 boxes of tarragon and pick the leaves off the stems, then divide the pile back in half.
  4. Add the ingredients list (above) to each container.
  5. Blend the contents of the Vitamix container using the Vitamix.
  6. Fill 2 small pitchers with it.
  7. Pour the second set of ingredients into the Vitamix container and mix it.
  8. Pour the mixed dressing back into the container, cover, label, and put in the fridge.
