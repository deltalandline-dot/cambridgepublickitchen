Carmelized Onion Black Bean Dip

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Cook oil and onions for about 10 minutes until golden brown.
  2. Add vinegar and salt and paprika and cook 2 minutes longer.
  3. Process in blender for 20 to 40 seconds or until dip consistency.
  4. Garnish with 1 Tbs chopped parsley.
