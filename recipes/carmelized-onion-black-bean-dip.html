Carmelized Onion Black Bean Dip

 

Ingredients:

 

2 /15 oz cans black beans/drained and rinsed

 

4 medium red onions chopped

2 Tbs Olive Oil

 

1 Tbs Balsamic Vinegar

1/2 tsp salt

1/2 tsp paprika

 

1 Tbs chopped parsley

 

Cook oil and onions for about 10 mins until golden brown

Add vinegar and salt and paprika and cook 2 mins longer

Process in blender for 20 to 40 seconds or until dip consistency

 

Garnish -  with 1 Tbs chopped parsley
