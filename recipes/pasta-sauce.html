Instructions

Sauteing 

Add olive oil to pan on medium-high heat and add onion.   When onions are partly soft, add mushrooms and saute until water is released & mostly cooked off.]  

Cook until onions softened then add garlic.

Cook garlic for 1-2 minutes until fragrant then proceed to add ingredients

Add ingredients.

Add tomato sauce, tomato paste, basil, oregano, thyme, bay leaves and sugar.  If using sun-dried tomatoes they can be added now too.   Bring to boil, then lower to simmer. Simmer for 1-3 hours to let flavors integrate.  Salt and pepper to taste.

Add Spinach

Note: this step can be done at end of making the sauce ahead of time, or BEST: just before using it for lasagna:  

If using frozen: thaw and squeeze dry (thoroughly very important!), then add after the sauce is done.
