Pasta Sauce

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Make-ahead note: for retreat use, this part must be made ahead and frozen (no time on the retreat day of serving!). This makes about 14 quarts, and freezes in 2 8-qt cambros. This batch is not really enough for serving over pasta on its own — it's sized for lasagna, enough for 2 4" deep pans of lasagna (per the recipe in the folder).

Yield: ~50 servings (reduced quantities for a smaller batch are also given below, though the source doesn't specify that batch's serving count)

Equipment

  • Rondo or large sauce pot

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Sauté: add olive oil to the pan on medium-high heat and add the onion. When the onions are partly soft, add the mushrooms and sauté until the water is released and mostly cooked off. Cook until the onions have softened, then add the garlic. Cook the garlic for 1-2 minutes until fragrant, then proceed to add the remaining ingredients.
  2. Add ingredients: add the tomato sauce, tomato paste, basil, oregano, thyme, bay leaves, and sugar. If using sun-dried tomatoes, they can be added now too. Bring to a boil, then lower to a simmer. Simmer for 1-3 hours to let the flavors integrate. Salt and pepper to taste. Add water if the sauce gets too thick (it should be pretty thick, though).
  3. Add spinach: this step can be done at the end of making the sauce ahead of time, or, best, just before using it for lasagna. If using frozen spinach: thaw and squeeze dry (thoroughly — very important!), then add after the sauce is done.
