Preparation

  1. Sauté onions: in a large pot, sauté onions in olive oil until tender.
  2. Add remaining ingredients, bring to a boil, then down to a simmer, and cook until the butternut squash is easily pierced with a fork, 20-30 minutes.
  3. Emulsify: using an immersion blender, blend until smooth. Season with salt and pepper.
