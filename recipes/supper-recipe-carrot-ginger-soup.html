Carrot Ginger Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Supper Recipe:  Carrot Ginger Soup

Equipment  

Ingredients

For ~48

½ cup

Olive oil

4 cups

Onions chopped

15 lbs 

Carrot

1 

Large can coconut milk

6-8

Quarts vegetable broth

4-6 inches

Ginger minced in cuisinart (more if desired)

Instructions

Saute onions

In a large pot saute onions in olive oil until tender.

Add ingredients

Add remaining ingredients, bring to a boil and then down to a simmer and cook until butternut squash is easily pierced with a fork. 20-30 min.

Emulsify

Using an emulsifying blender blend, blend until smooth. Season with salt and pepper.
