Dal

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Note: a few changes from Lael, 11/2023.

Yield: ~50 servings. Quantities below are listed as three numbers — 50 servings with supper leftovers reserved / 50 servings with no supper leftovers / 36 servings if also serving supper.

Equipment

  • 32 oz stockpot (the largest one)
  • Largest skillet

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Start lentils simmering: Sauté the onions in some oil in the bottom of the stock pot.
  2. Rinse the lentils in a strainer under running water — they tend to clump, so separate with your fingers; this may take several batches.
  3. Put the lentils, bay leaves, water, and salt in the stock pot. Bring to a boil and skim off the foam.
  4. After the foam is skimmed off, lower the heat.
  5. Add the ginger and half the garlic.
  6. Reduce to an active simmer. Check frequently that it's still actively simmering, but not over-boiling. Stir often, all the way to the bottom, and always put a heat deflector under this pot — it's easy to burn. Maybe use a big paddle. If it seems to be getting too thick, you can add more water; it should be thick but still pourable.
  7. Prepare the spice mixture once the dal is cooked (add to the dal shortly before serving, about 12:00): Heat the oil in the large skillet until hot. Add the mustard seeds and stir, cooking just until they pop (a few seconds). Once they pop, add the cayenne, turmeric, bay leaves, cumin, coriander, fenugreek, and the rest of the garlic, stirring a few times. Turn heat to low and cook until the garlic just begins to brown. Add the tomatoes and cook a few minutes, stirring often.
  8. Remove the bay leaves from the dal if you can find them, and add the spice/tomato mixture along with the lime juice. Taste for heat and salt. (Probably add the juice of 2 lemons, maybe a little brown sugar too — have all the cooks taste it!)
