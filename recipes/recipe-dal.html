Instructions

Start Lentils Simmering

Saute the onions in some oil in the bottom of the stock pot.

Rinse the lentils in the strainer under running water.  Tends to clump, separate with your fingers.  This may take several batches in the strainer.

Put lentils, bay leaves, water & salt in the stock pot.  Bring to a boil and skim off foam.  

After foam is skimmed off, lower heat

Add ginger and half the garlic.

Reduce to an active simmer.  Check frequently that it is still actively simmering, but not over-boiling.  STIR OFTEN ALL THE WAY TO THE BOTTOM.  ALWAYS PUT A HEAT-DEFLECTOR UNDER THIS POT, EASY TO BURN!.  Maybe use big paddle.  If it seems to be getting too thick, you can add more water.  It should be thick, but still pourable.

Preparing the spice mixture once dal is cooked

Add spices to dal shortly before serving, about 12:00.

Once dal is cooked prepare spices.  Begin by heating the oil in the large skillet until hot.  Add mustard seeds and stir cooking just until they pop, just a few seconds. Once they pop add cayenne, tumeric, bay leaves, cumin, coriander, fenugreek and rest of garlic stirring a few times.  Turn heat to low and allow spice mixture to cook until garlic just begins to brown.  Add tomatoes and cook a few minutes stirring often.

Remove bay leaves from dal if you can find them and add the spice/tomato mixture to dal along with juice of limes.  Taste for heat and salt.

(Probably add juice of 2 lemons, maybe a little brown sugar also...have all the cooks taste it!)
