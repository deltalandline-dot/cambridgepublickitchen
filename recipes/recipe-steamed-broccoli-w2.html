Steamed Broccoli W2

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Equipment

  • 2 6” hotel pans, 2 steamer pans, 2 lids

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. We're doing a whole case, so you may need to do 2 rounds — it can all be finished a bit early, anytime from 11:30 onward or so, and be served room temperature if needed.
  2. Setup 2 steamer assemblies: get the two 6” deep (deepest) solid pans and fill them with 2” of water.
  3. Set the water-filled pans across 2 burners each.
  4. Divide as much broccoli as will fit (with the lid fully closed) into the 2 perforated pans.
  5. Set the perforated pans in the water-filled pans and put a lid on each.
  6. Turn on both burners. Let the broccoli steam for about 5 minutes after the water comes to a boil. Carefully lift the lid and check on it.
  7. When it looks done, remove the perforated pans from the base pans and empty the broccoli into a large metal serving bowl.
  8. Refill one or both pans with any remaining broccoli and steam for just 2–3 minutes (we may use some of these for further baking, so leave it barely done). Leave the pans on the counter (out of the water) so we can serve more if needed.
