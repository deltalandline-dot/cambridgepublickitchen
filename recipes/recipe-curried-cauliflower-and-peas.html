Curried Cauliflower and Peas

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Equipment

  • One 6" (deepest) full-sized hotel pan with lid

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Make sauce, add veg, and cook on stovetop: Open the cans of coconut milk and tomatoes so they're ready to pour.
  2. Mix the curry powder, paprika, and dal spice mix together.
  3. Fry the spice mix for a minute or so in the oil in the bottom of the hotel pan on LOW HEAT, watching and stirring the whole time.
  4. Add the coconut milk and tomatoes to the fried spice mix.
  5. Add the cauliflower and stir it up a bit if possible.
  6. Cover and cook over medium heat (lower the heat once it gets going).
  7. (~11:15) Cook about 30 minutes, checking and stirring occasionally.
  8. (~11:45) Add the peas and stir it all up if possible.
