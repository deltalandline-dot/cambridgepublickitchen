Recipe: Curried Cauliflower and Peas

Instructions

Make sauce, add veg and cook on stove top

Open the cans of coconut milk & tomatoes so they are ready to pour.  

Mix the curry powder, paprika and Dal spice mix together.

Fry the spice mix for a minute or so in the oil in bottom of the hotel pan on LOW HEAT watching and stirring the whole time.  

Add the coconut milk and tomatoes to the fried spices mix.   

Add the cauliflower and stir up a bit if possible.

Cover and cook over medium heat, lower the heat once it gets going?

Cook about 30 mins…start maybe 11:15, check and stir occasionally.

About 11:45, add the peas and stir it all up if possible.
