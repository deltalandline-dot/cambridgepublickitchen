Lemon Tahini Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Recipe: Lemon Tahini Salad Dressing                                       

Equipment  Vitamix, electric juicer, mixing bowl, whisk, 2 small pitchers

Ingredients  NOTE: THIS NEEDS REWORKING, WE CURRENTLY  PREFER THE ORANGE TAHINI

For 1 batch 

(~48 servs)

2 ½ cups

water

1 cup

lemon juice  (4-6 lemons)

¾ cup

parsley

5 cloves or 1 ¼ tsp

fresh garlic or powder

1 ½ Tblsp

tamari

2 tsp

cumin

½ tsp

white pepper

2 cups

tahini (=1 whole tin of Joyva, or measure out bulk

½  cup

Light olive oil

Instructions

Using juicer, juice the lemons.

Wash parsley and cut off stems (some of the upper stems are OK).

In a medium sized bowl, use a hand whisk to mix lemon juice, tahini and 1 cup of the water so that tahini is not too clumpy (or else it will stall the Vitamix)

Then blend in the Vitamix with rest of ingredients. 

Divide between two small pitchers.

Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
