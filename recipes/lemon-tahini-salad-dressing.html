Lemon Tahini Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Note: this recipe needs reworking — we currently prefer the Orange Tahini dressing instead.

Yield: ~48 servings (1 batch)

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • Electric juicer
  • Mixing bowl
  • Whisk
  • 2 small pitchers

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Using juicer, juice the lemons.
  2. Wash parsley and cut off stems (some of the upper stems are OK).
  3. In a medium sized bowl, use a hand whisk to mix lemon juice, tahini and 1 cup of the water so that the tahini is not too clumpy (or else it will stall the Vitamix).
  4. Then blend in the Vitamix with the rest of the ingredients.
  5. Divide between two small pitchers.
  6. Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
