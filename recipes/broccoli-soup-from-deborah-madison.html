BROCCOLI SOUP FROM DEBORAH MADISON

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Vegetable Soups from Deborah Madison's Kitchen (via Lael).

Yield: ~42 servings (this batch is scaled 7× from the original, a ~6-serving recipe)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Separate the broccoli crowns from the stalks and cut into florets of similar size (small or large). Thickly peel the stalks so none of the fibrous outer layer remains, then slice thinly.
  2. Heat the oil in a wide soup pot. When hot, add the onion, garlic, chile, ginger, and cilantro and sauté, stirring rapidly, for 1 minute. Add the potato and broccoli stems, season with a scant teaspoon salt, add ~2 qts of the water, and simmer, covered, until the onion is soft, about 5 minutes. Add the broccoli florets and the rest of the water and simmer just until the broccoli is tender, 5 to 10 minutes once it returns to a boil. Let cool briefly, then purée until smooth, leaving only tiny nubbins of broccoli.
  3. Save some out for no-soy diets, then mix the miso with some of the soup to dilute it, and pour it back into the pot. Taste for salt.
  4. Stir in ~1 Tbsp toasted sesame oil.
