Hollyhock Dressing

Updated by Chris 7/30/2015 from notes by Michelle

Makes 1.5qts

Blend the below well in the Vitamix:

  • ¾ cup water
  • ¾ cup tamari 
  • ¾ cup apple cider vinegar
  • 9 cloves garlic
  • 3 c canola or other non-olive oil 

Add & blend well again:

  • 1 cup nutritional yeast

Add and blend well again:

  • 3.5-4 cups canola oil

Fill 2 small pitchers if for today, or else store in 2quart (smallest square) container with label & date.
