Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~1.5 qts

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Blend the water, tamari, apple cider vinegar, garlic, and 3 cups oil well in the Vitamix.
  2. Add the nutritional yeast and blend well again.
  3. Add the remaining 3.5-4 cups canola oil and blend well again.
  4. Fill 2 small pitchers if for today, or else store in a 2 quart (smallest square) container with label & date.
