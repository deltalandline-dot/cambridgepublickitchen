Steamed or Roasted Green Beans

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Equipment

  • 2 large baking trays, foil (for roasting)
  • Steamer pan setup — either 1 reused for 2 batches, or 2 setups (for steaming)

Preparation

  1. Roasted (and steamed a bit first on the trays): Toss beans in olive oil, sprinkle with salt and maybe pepper. Heat oven to 350°F. These need to be done early to have time for the Tempeh to use the oven. They can be served room temp. We found it best to "steam" them for 20–30 minutes first by covering the trays of beans with foil; when almost soft enough to eat, remove the foil and roast for another ~10 minutes until slightly browning.
  2. OR Simple Steamed: You may need to wash the steamer pans used for the tempeh to reuse for the beans. These can be done anytime and served either warm or room temp. Steam the beans for ~20 minutes until they are as soft as you like them. Be sure to check that the water doesn't all boil off.
