Peanut Casserole

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Equipment

  • 2 2" hotel pans, or largest soup pot (for the ~16 qt soup version, to stay warm)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. For either version: chop the leftover cabbage into bite-sized pieces.
  2. For casseroles: mix or layer whatever rice, cabbage, and peanut stew you have leftover. If there's not enough to make 2 (2") casseroles (or 1-1½ if lots of 8 precepts), a soup is probably easier to pad out. Cover and refrigerate.
  3. For soup: add your leftovers to the soup pot (using no more than ~2 qts rice, so it's not too thick). Augment as needed to reach ~16 qts (or less if lots of 8 precepts) using canned tomatoes, any not-needed zucchini/kale/cabbage (check with KM), maybe frozen green beans, and Better Than Bouillon. Consult on flavor if it's not enough — maybe more peanut butter, salt, or lemon. Bring to a rapid boil, turn off the fire, and put the lid on — the big pots will stay warm until 5:00.
  4. About 3:50: reheat the casseroles during the talk; they're done when they reach 165°F.
