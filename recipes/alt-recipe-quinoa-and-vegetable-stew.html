ALT Recipe Quinoa and Vegetable Stew

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

This is a hearty, veggie- and quinoa-packed Latin stew. It's full of nutritious ingredients and bright, fresh flavors — a healthy soup you'll want all year round! Adapted from cookingclassy.com, itself adapted from the Cook's Country One Pan Wonders Cookbook.

Yield: original recipe serves 6 (prep 15 min, cook 40 min, total ~55 min). Quantities below are given as full-house batch / ~33-serving batch (with 4 qts leftover) / original single recipe.

Preparation

  1. Heat oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat. Add onion and bell pepper and sauté until softened, about 5 minutes.
  2. Stir in garlic, paprika, coriander, and cumin and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Stir in broth and potatoes and bring to a boil over high heat.
  3. Reduce heat to medium-low (covering the pot from this point on works well) and simmer gently for 10 minutes.
  4. Stir in quinoa and simmer for 8 minutes. Stir in corn (can wait to add corn until adding the peas if using frozen corn) and simmer until potatoes and quinoa are just tender, about 5–7 minutes.
  5. Stir in tomatoes and peas and cook until warmed through, about 2 minutes.
  6. Remove from heat, season with salt and pepper to taste. (Stew can be refrigerated for up to 2 days.)
  7. Add additional broth as needed to thin the sauce when reheating. Sprinkle each serving with queso fresco, avocado and cilantro before serving.
  8. Note: do not omit the toppings — they are key to the flavor of this soup.
