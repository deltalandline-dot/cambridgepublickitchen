Vegan Cornbread

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48-50 servings (4 half-pans), or ~36 servings if using 3 half-pans (quantities were recently upped to fill 4 half-pans)

Equipment

  • 4 half-sized 2” hotel pans (cooks more evenly than 2 full-size)
  • 2 large mixing bowls
  • 2 small mixing bowls

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 375 degrees. Grease pans. Mix v-egg or ground flax and warm water separately, let set for 5-10 minutes to thicken (will need to whisk the v-egg). Mix alternative milk of choice and vinegar and let set while preparing other ingredients.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, mix dry ingredients: cornmeal, gf flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt in one bowl.
  3. In a separate mixing bowl, mix together oil, pumpkin, sweetener, "milk" mixture, and flax mixture.
  4. Leave wets and drys separate until just ready to go in the oven. Mix just until combined, being careful not to overmix.
  5. Place mixture into 3 or 4 half-sized 2-inch-deep hotel pans (stored on the island near the pantry door). Bake for 40-50 min, or until golden brown and cooked in the center — test by patting for firmness or checking that a toothpick/knife comes out clean. Start checking after 30 mins.
