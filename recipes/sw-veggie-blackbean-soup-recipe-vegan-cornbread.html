Instructions

Heat oven to 375 degrees. 

Grease pans.

Mix v-egg or ground flax and WARM water separately, let set for 5-10 minutes to thicken (will need to whisk the v-egg).

Mix alternative milk of choice and vinegar and let set while preparing other ingredients.

In a large mixing bowl, mix dry ingredients:  corn meal, gf flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt in one bowl. 

In a separate mixing bowl, mix together oil, pumpkin, sweetener, “milk” mixture, flax mixture.

Leave wets and drys separate until just ready to go in oven.  Mix just until combined, be careful not to overmix.

Place mixture into 3 or 4 half-sized  2 inch-deep hotel pans (stored on the island near pantry door).  Bake for 40-50 min? or until golden brown and cooked in the center.  Test by patting for firmness & toothpick or knife comes out clean.

Start checking after 30 mins.
