Roasted Cubed Tofu

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Note: sauce quantities are approximate — use your judgement.

Yield: ~48 servings (or ~36 servings)

Equipment

  • Huge salad bowl to toss (maybe a smaller one to serve)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Mix oil, vinegar, tamari, and sesame oil.
  2. Toss cubed tofu in the mixture.
  3. Spread on parchment on large baking trays.
  4. Roast at 350° for ~40 minutes. Check every 15 minutes or so, turning when brown on the bottom.
  5. Sprinkle with sesame seeds & serve in a large bowl.
