Brown Rice

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: two batch sizes — a larger batch "with supper leftovers" (headcount not stated in the source) and ~36 servings if not doing a supper leftover batch; both quantities are given below.

Equipment

  • Large strainer
  • Rice cooker

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. (~10:30–11) Rinse the rice well using the large double-mesh strainer (may take several batches). Put the damp rice in the cooker.
  2. (About 11:00) Add water to the rice in the cooker per above.
  3. Plug in and turn on the cooker. It will take about 50–60 minutes to cook, then it switches to warm. Before serving, check that it has switched to warm. If so, carefully open the lid and fluff it with a wood or plastic tool.
  4. Serve with wooden spoons so as not to scratch the container.
