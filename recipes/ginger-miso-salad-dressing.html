Ginger Miso Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: 1 batch (~48 servings)

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • 2 pitchers

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Rough chop the ginger.
  2. Add all except the oil to the Vitamix container and mix on low.
  3. Drizzle the oil into the Vitamix, mixing while you do. Gradually increase speed until all emulsified. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed.
  4. Divide between 2 small pitchers.
  5. Cover and store in the fridge (if not for today, label and date it too).
