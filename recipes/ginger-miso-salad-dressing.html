Ginger Miso Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Scale recipe: or ×

Recipe: Ginger Miso Salad Dressing                                       

Equipment  Vitamix, 2 pitchers

Ingredients

For 1 batch 

(~48 servs)

½ cup

apple cider vinegar

½ cup

tamari

½ cup

Agave

¾ cup

yellow miso (or any kind will do)

¼ cup

chopped ginger (rough chop, let vitamix take it from there)

½ cup

water

1 tsp

toasted sesame oil

4 cups

Light olive oil

Instructions

Rough chop the ginger.

Add all except oil in vitamix container and mix on low.

Drizzle oil into Vitamix mixing while you do.  Gradually increase speed until all emulsified.    Taste & adjust seasoning if needed.

Divide between 2 small pitchers.

Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
