Shepherds Pie

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Equipment

  • 2 2” hotel pans, or ~16 qts soup in the largest soup pot (to stay warm)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. For Either: Chop up the leftover cabbage into bite-sized pieces.
  2. For Casseroles: Mix or layer whatever you have leftover of rice, cabbage and peanut stew. If not enough to make 2 2” casseroles (or 1-1½ if a lot of 8 precepts), a soup is probably easier to pad out. Cover & fridge.
  3. For Soup: Add your leftovers to the soup pot (using no more than ~2 qts rice, i.e. not too thick). Augment as needed to have ~16 qts (or less if lots of 8 precepts) using:
    • Canned tomatoes
    • Any not-needed zucchini or kale or cabbage? (check with KM)
    • Maybe frozen green beans?
    • Better than Bouillon
    • Consult on flavor if not enough (maybe more peanut butter? Salt? Lemon?)
    Bring to a rapid boil, turn off the fire, put the lid on — the big pots will stay warm until 5:00.
  4. At 3:50: reheat casseroles during the talk, done when 165 degrees.
