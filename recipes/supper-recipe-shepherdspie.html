Shepherds Pie

Week 2 Day 4           Supper Recipe:  Shepherd’s Pie

Equipment  2 2” hotel pans or ~16qts soup in largest soup pot (to stay warm)

Ingredients:  leftover Peanut Stew, Roasted Cabbage and Rice

Instructions

For Either: 

Chop up the leftover cabbage into bite sized pieces

For Casseroles:

Mix or layer whatever you have leftover of rice, cabbage and peanut stew.  If not enough to make 2x2” casseroles (or 1-1½ if a lot of 8 precepts), a soup is probably easier to pad out.   Cover & fridge.

For Soup:

Add your leftovers to the soup pot (using no more than ~2qts rice, ie not too thick).   Augment as needed to have ~16qts (or less if lots of 8 precepts) using:

  • Canned tomatoes
  • Any not needed zucchini or kale or cabbage? (check with KM)
  • Maybe frozen green beans?
  • Better than Bouillon
  • Consult on flavor if not enough (maybe more peanut butter? Salt? lemon? )

Bring to a rapid boil, turn off fire, put lid on, the big pots will stay warm until 5:00.

At 3:50: reheat casseroles during talk, done when 165 degrees.
