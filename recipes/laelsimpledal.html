Lael Simple Dal

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

For when we're tired of the usual onion & tomato base.

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Bring lentils, water, turmeric, chopped ginger and salt to a boil and then simmer until done.
  2. When nearly done: fry cumin seeds in oil for 1 min, add coriander, garam masala & cayenne.
  3. Add fried spices to dal.
  4. Add greens to dal if using.
  5. Just before serving, stir in the 4 oz pureed ginger and the lemon juice to taste.
  6. Taste for salt, lemon.
