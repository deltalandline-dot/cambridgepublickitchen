Lael’s Simple Dal

(if we’re tired of onion & tomato)

6 qt red lentils

12 qt water

¼ c + 2T turmeric

¼ c salt

4oz chopped ginger  + 4 oz pureed ginger (added later)

1/2c cumin seeds

1/3 cup ground coriander

¼ cup garam masala

¾ tsp cayenne

~¼ cup oil

½-1 c. lemon juice

Optional: spinach or other greens

Bring lentils, water, turmeric, chopped ginger and salt to a boil and then simmer until done.

When nearly done:

Fry cumin seeds in oil for 1 min, add coriander, garam masala & cayenne.

Add fried spices to dal.

Add greens to dal if using.

Just before serving stir in 4 oz pureed ginger and the lemon juice to taste.

Taste for salt, lemon.
