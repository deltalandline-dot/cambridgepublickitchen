Cheese Polenta

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Cheesy (and not) Oven Polenta: Ingredients & Setup

Equip: two 4” full-sized hotel pans, one half-sized 4” hotel pan (they won’t be full, but easier if they don’t slosh, so use 4”).

Preheat the oven to 425.  Turn down if needed after 30-40 minutes.

  • In each of the two large pans, mix:
    • 5 cups polenta (coarse corn meal)
    • 20 cups water
    • 1 T salt
  • In the half pan, mix:
    • 2 ½ cups polenta
    • 10 cups water
    • ½ T salt
  • Mix together in a bowl and set aside for adding (at the end) to the 2 large pans:
    • 1.5 lb Shredded swiss/gruyere mix, (or other “italian mix” or jack..ask KM).
    • 2 tubs of Crumbled blue or gorgonzola cheese
    • 2 T dried basil
    • 1 ½ T black pepper
  • Set aside for the tops of the large pans (at the end):
    • 3 cups shredded parmesan cheese (1 ½ cups each)
  • For the smaller no-cheese pan, mix and set aside:
    • 1 T Olive oil
    • 1  T dried basil
    • 2 tsp black pepper

Cheesy (and not) Oven Polenta: Baking Instructions

Oven bake the polenta:

  • Put lids on the pans and put them in the oven about 10:15.  Set a timer for an hour.  
  • Mix the cheeses & spices as described above.
  • About 11:15, carefully with hot pad gloves (and maybe with help) stir the pans.  Don’t worry if not thick yet.  Set the timer for another 15-20 mins. if not thick yet.

When the polenta has become thick (after ~60-80 mins) , add the cheese to the cheese-y versions:

  • Again carefully with gloves and help:  stir half the jack/blue/basil/pepper mix into each of the two full-sized pans. Sprinkle half the parmesan cheese over each of the two large pans.
  • Don’t stir in cheese in the half-pan!  Stir in the olive oil, basil, pepper mix.
  • Return all pans to the oven uncovered for about half an hour (or ok to leave for longer).  If the cheese is getting too brown on top, put the lids back on, if it is not getting brown, turn the heat up to 500 for a few minutes.
