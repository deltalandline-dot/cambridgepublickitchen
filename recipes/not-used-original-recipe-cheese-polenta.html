Cheese Polenta

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Equipment

  • Two 4" full-sized hotel pans
  • One half-sized 4" hotel pan (it won't be full, but it's easier if it doesn't slosh, so use a 4" pan)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (turn down if needed after 30-40 minutes).
  2. Put lids on the pans and put them in the oven about 10:15. Set a timer for an hour.
  3. Mix the cheeses & spices as described above (for the two large pans and the no-cheese pan).
  4. (~11:15) Carefully, with hot pad gloves (and maybe with help), stir the pans. Don't worry if not thick yet. Set the timer for another 15-20 minutes if not thick yet.
  5. When the polenta has become thick (after ~60-80 minutes), add the cheese to the cheese-y versions: again carefully with gloves and help, stir half the jack/blue/basil/pepper mix into each of the two full-sized pans. Sprinkle half the parmesan cheese over each of the two large pans.
  6. Don't stir cheese into the half-pan — stir in the olive oil, basil, pepper mix instead.
  7. Return all pans to the oven, uncovered, for about half an hour (or ok to leave for longer). If the cheese is getting too brown on top, put the lids back on; if it is not getting brown, turn the heat up to 500°F for a few minutes.
