Roasted Root Vegetables

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Scale recipe: or ×

Day 6 (or ALT 5)                              Recipe: Roasted Root Vegetables                               Page 1 of 1    

Equipment  large baking sheets, large mixing bowl

Ingredients

serves ~50

10lbs

beets, 1” cubed

10lbs

yams, 1” cubed

2lbs

carrots, 1” chunked  (sometimes more of above and no carrots)

**actually any mix of these vegetables, weights are a suggestion, should total about 22 lbs

to lightly coat

olive oil, either use bowl and toss or use spray

Instructions

Preheat (probably both) ovens to 400.

Toss each kind of vegetables separately with enough olive oil to lightly coat.  Consider chopping up and adding some rosemary from the garden!

Line as many baking sheets as needed with parchment paper (probably already set up by veggie choppers).

Spread the oiled vegetables among the number of sheets needed to keep them only 1 level deep and keep vegetable types separate in case they are done at different times.

Veggies go in ~10:45.  Stir and turn every half hour.  Done when tender and starting to caramelize. As they are done, transfer to a metal bowl and serve.
