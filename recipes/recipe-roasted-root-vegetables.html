Roasted Root Vegetables

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~50 servings

Equipment

  • Large baking sheets
  • Large mixing bowl

Ingredients

Note: any mix of these vegetables works — the weights below are a suggestion; total should be about 22 lbs.

Preparation

  1. Preheat (probably both) ovens to 400°.
  2. Toss each kind of vegetable separately with enough olive oil to lightly coat. Consider chopping up and adding some rosemary from the garden.
  3. Line as many baking sheets as needed with parchment paper (probably already set up by veggie choppers).
  4. Spread the oiled vegetables among the number of sheets needed to keep them only 1 level deep, keeping vegetable types separate in case they finish at different times.
  5. Veggies go in ~10:45. Stir and turn every half hour. Done when tender and starting to caramelize. As they finish, transfer to a metal bowl and serve.
