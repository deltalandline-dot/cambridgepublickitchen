Glazed Tempeh

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Sweet and sticky with just a little kick, these sliced tempeh strips are briefly marinated, baked, then re-glazed to trap in maximum flavor and tender texture.

Yield: serves 6 (or 36 at scale — see quantities below; the tempeh, collards, and corn scale ×6, but sauce/marinade ingredients scale ×12, i.e. double the sauce)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Place tempeh strips in a medium heat-proof bowl. Set aside.
  2. Whisk together maple syrup, orange juice, 2 Tbs. (×12: 1½ cups) oil, ketchup, mustard, sriracha, tamari, pepper, and cinnamon in a medium saucepan. Bring to a simmer over medium heat and cook 5 to 7 minutes, or until slightly thickened. Pour the hot mixture over the tempeh strips, cover, and let marinate 30 minutes, or overnight.
  3. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet with foil, and coat with cooking spray. Arrange the marinated tempeh strips on the prepared baking sheet; reserve the marinade. Cover the baking sheet tightly with foil, and bake 15 minutes. Remove the foil, and flip the tempeh pieces. Bake, uncovered, 5 minutes more, or until the tempeh is slightly browned. Remove from oven, and brush the tempeh with the remaining glaze.
  4. Meanwhile, heat the remaining 2 Tbs. (×6: ¾ cup) oil in a large saucepan over medium-high heat. Add garlic, and cook 30 seconds. Add the collard greens in two batches, wilting the first batch before adding the second. Stir in the corn, and season with salt and pepper, if desired. Cook 5 to 7 minutes, or until the greens are tender, stirring occasionally. Serve topped with the tempeh slices.
