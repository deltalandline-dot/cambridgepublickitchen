Glazed Tempeh

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Glazed Maple-Mustard Tempeh Strips with Collards – Page 1 of 2

Sweet and sticky with just a little kick, these sliced tempeh strips are briefly marinated, baked, then re-glazed to trap in maximum flavor and tender texture.

Ingredients: serves 6 (serving 6 x 6, make twice the amount of sauce)

2 8-oz. pkg. (x6 12 8-oz.pkg.) tempeh, sliced into ½-inch strips

⅓ cup (x12 4 cup) maple syrup

¼ cup (x12 3 cup) orange juice

¼ cup (x12/6 1 ½ & ¾  cup) safflower oil, divided

2 Tbs. (x12 1 1/2 cup)ketchup

2 Tbs. (x12 1 1/2 cup) Dijon mustard

1 Tbs. (x12 ¾ cup) sriracha sauce

1 Tbs. (x12 ¾ cup) low-sodium tamari

1 tsp. (x12 ¼ cup) freshly ground black pepper

¼ tsp. (x12 1 tbl.) ground cinnamon

2 cloves (x12 12 cloves) garlic, minced (2 tsp. (x12 1/2 cup))

2 12-oz. (x6 12 12-oz.) bunches collard greens, stems and ribs removed, leaves thinly sliced

2 cups (x6 12 cups) frozen fire roasted corn, thawed

Glazed Maple-Mustard Tempeh Strips with Collards – Page 2 of 2

Instructions: 

1 | Place tempeh strips in medium heat-proof bowl. Set aside.


2 | Whisk together maple syrup, orange juice, 2 Tbs.(x12 1 ½ cup) oil, ketchup, mustard, sriracha, tamari, pepper, and cinnamon in medium saucepan. Bring to a simmer over medium heat, and cook 5 to 7 minutes, or until slightly thickened. Pour hot mixture over tempeh strips, cover, and let marinate 30 minutes, or overnight.


3 | Preheat oven to 350°F. Line baking sheet with foil, and coat with cooking spray. Arrange marinated tempeh strips on prepared baking sheet; reserve marinade. Cover baking sheet tightly with foil, and bake 15 minutes. Remove foil, and flip tempeh pieces. Bake, uncovered, 5 minutes more, or until tempeh is slightly browned. Remove from oven, and brush tempeh with remaining glaze.


4 | Meanwhile, heat remaining 2 Tbs.(x6 ¾ cup) oil in large saucepan over medium-high heat. Add garlic, and cook 30 seconds. Add collard greens in two batches, wilting first batch before adding second. Stir in corn, and season with salt and pepper, if desired. Cook 5 to 7 minutes or until greens are tender, stirring occasionally. Serve topped with tempeh slices. 

Serves 6 or 36
