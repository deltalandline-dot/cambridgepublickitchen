Avocado Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Scale recipe: or ×

Recipe: Avocado Salad Dressing                                       

Equipment  Vitamix, electric juicer, 2 pitchers

Ingredients

For 1 batch 

(~48 servs)

2 cups

water

1 cup

lime/lemon juice  (use 4 limes, top off with lemons if needed..can use rice vin if short of lemons/limes but it tastes much fresher with citrus juice)

5 large-ish

avocados 

⅓ cup

cilantro

½ tsp

garlic powder

¼ cup

agave

½ tsp

Cumin powder

¾  tsp

white pepper

1 ¼ tsp

Salt 

2  cups

light olive oil

~¼ cup

Water (this is a thick dressing, but if seems too thick, more water..)

Instructions

Juice limes and lemons with juicer.

Add all except oil in vitamix container and mix on low.

Taste and adjust salt, agave, pepper, lime to taste if you wish to.

Drizzle oil into Vitamix mixing while you do.  Gradually increase speed until all emulsified.    Divide between 2 small pitchers.

Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
