Avocado Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings (1 batch)

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • Electric juicer
  • 2 pitchers

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Juice limes and lemons with juicer.
  2. Add all ingredients except oil in the Vitamix container and mix on low.
  3. Taste and adjust salt, agave, pepper, and lime to taste if you wish.
  4. Drizzle oil into the Vitamix while mixing. Gradually increase speed until all emulsified. Divide between 2 small pitchers.
  5. Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
