Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Mix the cabbages, green beans, mint, carrots, and any optional sprouts/peppers with enough baby kale to fill the medium salad bowl. Maybe mix it in the giant bowl and serve in the medium one.
  2. Make 1 batch of Peanut Lime Dressing (below), set up in 2 small pitchers.
  3. Set up and serve as sides: 2 bunches cilantro, 2 lbs peanuts.
  4. For the Peanut Lime Dressing: mix the peanut butter & water in a bowl to thin it.
  5. Add that and everything except the oil to the Vitamix; mix.
  6. Drizzle in the oil while mixing.
