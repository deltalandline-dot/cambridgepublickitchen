Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free

Salad to go with Thai Cauliflower Soup

(option for first full day supper, instead of Greek salad bar)

1 (or ~½ of large one) red cabbage: Shred or manually thinly slice

1 napa cabbage: thinly sliced

2lbs frozen green beans: Steam until thawed

2 cups of chopped mint leaves (either bought or from the garden outside the 

kitchen door)

~2 cups shredded carrots

Optional: see if we bought some mung bean sprouts (the fat white kind)

Optional: see if we bought some extra bell peppers.  (Make sure you leave 15 for the ratatouille meal.)

Mix all the above with enough baby kale to fill the medium salad bowl.  Maybe mix it in the giant one and serve in the medium.

Make 1 batch of Peanut Lime Dressing below, set up in 2 small pitchers.

Set up and serve as sides:  2 bunches cilantro, 2lbs of peanuts

For Peanut Lime Dressing: 

2 ¼  c  Peanut Butter 

2 c water  // more if needed if too thick

1 ¾  c lime juice (10-12 limes) & zest of 4 limes 

//ok to top off with lemons if you can’t get enough lime juice from the limes

¾  c rice vinegar

3 t  sesame oil  

½   c agave  // maybe more, taste it at the end

½  c tamari  //maybe less if not low-sodium?

6 cloves garlic  //the vitamix will mince it

1 T      roughly chopped ginger //the vitamix will mince it

1 ½   c  light olive oil

Mix PB & water in a bowl to thin.

Add that & all but oil to the vitamix, mix. 

Drizzle in oil while mixing.
