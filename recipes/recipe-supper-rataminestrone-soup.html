  • 2nd largest stock pot (the one with the pasta insert?)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Casseroles: First, if Andrea Fella is teaching and there are leftovers for her, maybe make a medium glass bowl of leftover polenta plus her no-bell-pepper ratatouille for her to microwave (don't put it in the oven).
  2. If enough leftovers: Make ~18 quarts of soup with the leftover ratatouille (hopefully ~8 qts left), adding Better Than Bouillon broth and any/all of the ingredients above as needed.
  3. When hot: When it's done, or just before lunch, transfer to soup warmers for the afternoon.
  4. At 5:00pm: If prepared, add the pre-cooked and cooled gluten-free pasta.
  5. Setup cheese: Set up a bowl of parmesan and "white" vegan cheese on the side.
