Supper Rata Minestrone Soup

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Day 4                             Supper Recipe:  Supper Minestrone Soup

Equipment:  2nd largest stock pot (the one with the pasta insert?) & soup warmers?

Ingredients: Leftover ratatouille and extras

Casseroles:

First: If Andrea Fella is teaching & there are leftovers for her, maybe make a med. glass bowl of leftover polenta + her no-bell-pepper ratatouille for her to microwave (don’t put in oven)

If Enough

Leftovers:

 Make ~18 quarts soup with leftover ratatouille (hopefully ~8qts left), adding Better Than Bouillon broth, any/all of the following as needed: 

  • more canned tomatoes (1-2 28oz cans?)
  • sometimes there are “extra” zucchini’s prepped for the rata
  • More garlic
  • leftover white beans &/or ~2 28oz cans white beans or garbanzos
  • ?frozen green beans (1-2 packs)
  • More “italian” herbs from Ratatouille recipe
  • If we have GF pasta:  pre-make it, rinse & cool it.  Add cooked pasta just before serving in the evening.

When hot:

When it’s done, or just before lunch, transfer to soup warmers for the afternoon.

At 5:00pm:

If prepared:  Add pre-cooked & cooled gluten-free pasta. 

Setup cheese:

Setup a bowl of parmesan and “white” vegan cheese on the side.
