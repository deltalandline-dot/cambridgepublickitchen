Instructions

Roast

Eggplant  &

Maybe

Fennel

Set the oven to 375. (You can put eggplant (and fennel) in as soon as ready, no need to wait for pre-heat)

(Eggplant should be set up on large baking sheets spread in a single layer.)  Spray the paper with olive oil spray.  Optionally, do the same with the fresh fennel (or it can just be added in with mushrooms).

Roast the eggplant (& fennel) cubes for about 30 minutes, stirring once.  Set aside.  You may also roast the peppers and even the zucchini if you have time and oven space.

(While it roasts, get the polenta ready for the oven..other recipe).

Prep

Chop the garlic in the mini-cuis (chops better when thawed)

Lightly toast 4 T fennel seeds in small skillet watching closely, grind in spice grinder (wipe or brush out spice grinder before and after..)

Sauté

Coat the rondo with olive oil to about 1/8” and sauté the onion.                                                                    

When the onions gets translucent, add mushrooms (& fresh fennel if using, whether pre-roasted or not).  Saute until they soften.

Add the tomato paste to the onions & sauté a couple of minutes Add the garlic and sauté briefly.

Option: drain the tomatoes, SAVING THE LIQUID to add in as needed or for future use in left-overs casserole.

Add the tomatoes, dried herbs and parsley.

Let the sauce simmer for ~15 minutes

The stew can simmer anywhere from 45-90 minutes, stir often. Maybe cover if not too juicy.

Prep sides, season, garnish

Wash and cut the basil leaves into thin strips.

Set-up a side bowl of parmesan cheese.

About 30 minutes before serving time, check that the vegetables are all soft and taste the seasoning.  Add more herbs if needed, salt and pepper to taste.

Garnish with the slivered fresh basil if using.

Serve in the rondo pot.
