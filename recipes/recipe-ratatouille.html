Ratatouille

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~50 servings with supper leftovers and more (or ~50 servings with no supper leftovers, using the alternate quantities). Note: the larger quantity won't fit in the rondo — good to save some for supper in any case, by moving ~6-8 qts to a small stock pot and setting it aside for a supper soup base.

Equipment

  • Large rondo pan with lid
  • 2-3 large baking sheets
  • Small stock pot

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Roast eggplant & maybe fennel: Set the oven to 375° (you can put the eggplant and fennel in as soon as ready, no need to wait for preheat).
  2. Set the eggplant up on large baking sheets, spread in a single layer. Spray the paper with olive oil spray. Optionally, do the same with the fresh fennel (or it can just be added in with the mushrooms).
  3. Roast the eggplant (& fennel) cubes for about 30 minutes, stirring once. Set aside. You may also roast the peppers and even the zucchini if you have time and oven space.
  4. While it roasts, get the polenta ready for the oven (see other recipe).
  5. Prep: Chop the garlic in the mini-cuisinart (chops better when thawed).
  6. Lightly toast 4 T fennel seeds in a small skillet, watching closely, and grind in the spice grinder (wipe or brush out the spice grinder before and after).
  7. Sauté: Coat the rondo with olive oil to about 1/8" and sauté the onion.
  8. When the onions get translucent, add mushrooms (& fresh fennel if using, whether pre-roasted or not). Sauté until they soften.
  9. Add the tomato paste to the onions & sauté a couple of minutes. Add the garlic and sauté briefly.
  10. Option: drain the tomatoes, saving the liquid to add in as needed or for future use in a leftovers casserole.
  11. Add the tomatoes, dried herbs, and parsley.
  12. Let the sauce simmer for ~15 minutes.
  13. Add vegetables: Add the zucchini and eggplant. (This is where you should shift some off into a side stock pot, both for space and to save for supper.)
  14. If Andrea Fella is teaching, set aside a no-pepper version for her (including enough for leftovers if having): could add some celery or extra fennel, if available, to her version instead of bell peppers.
  15. Add bell peppers to the main version.
  16. Put a heat deflector under the rondo pan! The stew can simmer anywhere from 45-90 minutes; stir often. Maybe cover if not too juicy.
  17. Prep sides, season, garnish: Wash and cut the basil leaves into thin strips.
  18. Set up a side bowl of parmesan cheese.
  19. About 30 minutes before serving time, check that the vegetables are all soft and taste the seasoning. Add more herbs if needed, salt and pepper to taste.
  20. Garnish with the slivered fresh basil, if using.
  21. Serve in the rondo pot.
