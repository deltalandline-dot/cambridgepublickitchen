Mushroom gravy

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Recipe: Mushroom Gravy                                  

Equipment: Rondo

Ingredients

#leftover amount

½ cup

Earth balance vegan butter

3 lbs

onions

5 lbs

Mushrooms

3 qts 

water

1 cup

cornstarch

½ jar

Veggie stock

2 T

Rosemary from garden

2 T

Sage from garden

¾ cup

Nutritional yeast

Salt and pepper

Instructions

Saute

Melt butter on medium heat in rondo and add onions. Saute for 5-10 minutes until they begin to soften.  Add mushrooms and saute until liquid begins to release.

Mixing

Mix water, veggie stock and cornstarch and add to mushroom and onion mixture.

Add ingredients and finish

Add remaining ingredients, rosemary, sage, nutritional yeast and bring to boil and reduce to simmer until thick and delicious. Add salt and pepper to taste
