Mushroom gravy

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: sized to use up a "leftover amount" (no specific serving count given in the original).

Equipment

  • Rondo

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Sauté: Melt the butter on medium heat in the rondo and add the onions. Sauté for 5-10 minutes until they begin to soften. Add the mushrooms and sauté until liquid begins to release.
  2. Mixing: Mix the water, veggie stock, and cornstarch, and add to the mushroom and onion mixture.
  3. Add ingredients and finish: Add the remaining ingredients — rosemary, sage, nutritional yeast — and bring to a boil; reduce to a simmer until thick and delicious. Add salt and pepper to taste.
