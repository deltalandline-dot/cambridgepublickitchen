Lasagna

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Could either make an extra no-dairy, no-egg lasagna with the "Vegan Ricotta" recipe and vegan mozzarella (if shopped for), or have sauce, spinach, beans, and GF rotelli on the side.

Yield: 48 servings (or 36 — makes 2 full pans, or 1½ pans for the smaller batch)

Equipment

  • 2 (2-inch deep, or could use 4" deep for no-slosh but only half-filled) full-sized hotel pans
  • Mixing bowl
  • 12 qt pot for side pasta, or another pan/half-pan if making vegan lasagna

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Prepping ingredients (this assumes the sauce was pre-made earlier — see the separate pasta sauce recipe): Preheat oven to 375°. Start the sauce reheating — see if you need to add the spinach, and if so, drain it very well (press hard with spoons into a large strainer). Mix the ricotta, eggs, half the mozzarella, salt, and pepper.
  2. Maybe reserve: Reserve some sauce (depending on headcount) for the GF/DF alternative (see below). Reserve some mozzarella and/or parmesan for a side condiment for special diets.
  3. Layering: Spray the pans with spray oil! Layer the lasagna per pan: bottom (first) — a thin layer of sauce to coat the pan, noodles, ricotta mix (if doing 2 pans use ¼ of the total here, if doing 1½ pans use ⅓ of the total here), a thicker layer of sauce (5-6 cups for a whole pan), a light sprinkling of mozzarella, noodles, ricotta mix (another ¼ or ⅓ of the total), a thicker layer of sauce (5-6 cups for a whole pan), a light sprinkling of mozzarella, noodles, a thin layer of sauce; top (last) — a generous sprinkling of mozzarella.
  4. Baking: Top the pans with the new dome lids (on top of the pan rack), or make peaked lids of foil. Bake 60 minutes, remove the lids, and bake another 20 minutes. Remove from the oven and let set for 20-30 minutes before serving.
  5. Make GF/dairy-free alternative: If needed, make the GF spiral pasta per package instructions so it's done just prior to serving (typically boils 6-10 minutes). Heat the reserved sauce and white beans in a saucepan. Combine the pasta with the sauce and beans.
