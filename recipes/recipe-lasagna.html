Instructions  (This assumes the sauce was pre-made earlier..see separate recipe)

Prepping Ingredients

Preheat oven to 375.  

Start sauce re-heating.  See if you need to add the spinach and if so, drain it VERY WELL, ie press hard with spoons into large strainer).

Mix Ricotta, eggs, half the mozzarella, salt and pepper. 

Maybe Reserve:

Reserve some sauce (see headcount) for the GF/DF alternative (see below).

Reserve some mozzarella and/or parmesan for a side condiment for diets.

Layering

SPRAY PANS with spray oil!!

Layer the lasagna per pan:

BOTTOM (FIRST) =>  Thin layer of sauce to coat the pan

                                     Noodles

                                    Ricotta mix (if  doing 2 pans, use 1/4th of the total here, if doing 1 ½, 

                                              use ⅓ the total here)

                                    Thicker layer of sauce (5-6 cups for a whole pan)

                                    Light sprinkling of mozzarella

                                    Noodles

                                    Ricotta mix (another  1/4th or ⅓ of the total here)

                                    Thicker layer of sauce (5-6 cups for a whole pan)

                                    Light sprinkling of mozzarella

                                    Noodles

                                    Thin layer of sauce

  TOP (LAST)==>       Generous sprinkling of mozzarella

Baking

Top the pans with the new dome-lids (on top of pan rack), or make peaked lids of foil bake 60 minutes, remove lids and bake another 20 minutes.  Remove from oven and let set for 20-30 minutes before serving.

Heat reserved sauce and white beans in a sauce pan.

Combine pasta with sauce & beans.
