Artichoke Hummus

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~16 cups (for a supper with leftovers), or ~8 cups (typical last-day snack only, blue bowl)

Equipment

  • Large bowl
  • Robo Coupe
  • Large ceramic serving bowl
  • Leftover container if making the double (16-cup) size

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. You will need to make this in 2 batches (for 8 cups) or 4 batches (for 16 cups) to fit in the Robo Coupe, so add some of each ingredient to each batch as you go, then hand-mix the batches together. Assemble the Robo Coupe with the clear bowl and blade, adding roughly proportional parts of each ingredient to each batch.
  2. Blend using pulse at first, then leave it running for a full minute or so to get a smooth consistency. Mix all the batches together. Serve in a large ceramic bowl with garnish.
