Tarragon Dressing

Tarragon Vinaigrette 

  • Gather all ingredients below and TWO Vitamix containers.
  • You will double this recipe:  add the ingredients to the first Vitamix container then repeat with the second container.

Ingredients for ONE Vitamix Container (do this TWICE)

1 Cup water

6 Cloves garlic

3 1/2 Cup Canola Oil + additional oil to cover garlic for poaching

2 Cup Rice Vinegar

¼ Cup Honey

¼ Cup Dijon Mustard

1 box of Tarragon (approx. ½ Cup)

  • Add 1 cup WATER to each Vitamix container. 
  • Add 3 1/2 Cup Canola Oil to each Vitamix container.
  • Poach Garlic: Place 12 whole garlic cloves in the smallest skillet and cover with Canola oil.  Simmer in medium heat for about 5 minutes.  Watch closely.
    • Add 6 cloves and half the oil to each Vitamix container. (Adding it to the cool oil/water prevents garlic from further cooking)  
  • Rinse the Tarragon and pick the leaves off the stems.
  • Add Remaining Ingredients to first Vitamix container.  Repeat with second Vitamix container.
  • Blend all ingredients. 
  • Fill 2 small pitchers with each salad dressing made.
