Tarragon Dressing

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Instructions below are for making a double batch.

Equipment

  • Two Vitamix containers
  • Smallest skillet, for poaching garlic

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Gather all ingredients below and two Vitamix containers.
  2. You will double this recipe: add the ingredients to the first Vitamix container, then repeat with the second container.
  3. Add 1 cup water to each Vitamix container.
  4. Add 3 1/2 cup canola oil to each Vitamix container.
  5. Poach Garlic: Place 12 whole garlic cloves in the smallest skillet and cover with canola oil. Simmer on medium heat for about 5 minutes — watch closely. Add 6 cloves and half the oil to each Vitamix container (adding it to the cool oil/water prevents the garlic from further cooking).
  6. Rinse the tarragon and pick the leaves off the stems.
  7. Add Remaining Ingredients to the first Vitamix container. Repeat with the second Vitamix container.
  8. Blend all ingredients.
  9. Fill 2 small pitchers with each salad dressing made.
