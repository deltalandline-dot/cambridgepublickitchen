Simple Miso Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Maybe offer on the last night with noodle salad. From Rachel.

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Heat 15 quarts of water to a low simmer.
  2. Add 15 sheets of nori cut into large squares.
  3. Simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Add 3.5 cups of white miso (blended with some hot water to avoid clumping).
  5. Add 3.5 cups of extra firm tofu. (I used the silken tofu from the yogi fridge, so there should have been 11 left in the main fridge for the next retreat.)
  6. Simmer for another 5 minutes.
  7. Before serving, add finely chopped scallions. The recipe says 7 cups; I used what we had left from the produce order, which was probably about half that.
