Simple Miso Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Simple Miso Soup   (maybe offer on last night with noodle salad)

  ..from Rachel

15 sheets Nori cut in large squares

3.5 cups miso

3.5 cups cubed tofu

7 cups chopped scallions

Heat 15 quarts of water to a low simmer.
Add 15 sheets of nori cut into large squares.
Simmer for 5 minutes
Add 3.5 cups of white miso ( blended with some hot water to avoid clumping)
Add 3.5 cups of extra firm tofu. ( I used the Silken tofu from the yogi fridge, so there should have been 11 left in the main fridge for the next retreat)
Simmer for another 5 minutes.

Before serving.
Add finely chopped scallions.... I used what we had left from the produce order. The recipe says 7 cups. Pretty sure I had about half that.
