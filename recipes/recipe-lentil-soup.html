Lentil Soup

O-Night                                    Recipe: Lentil Soup                       Page 2 of 3

Instructions

Prepare 

Ingredients

Locate all ingredients

Measure out lentils, check for stones, rinse and set aside

Dice onions, carrots, celery and potatoes

Collards: remove thick stem from center, slice leaf halves crosswise into 1” wide strips

Wash lemons

Zest lemons (=scrape off the thin outermost yellow layer of skin) using either the microplane (sharp!) or box grater (suggest wearing the cut-resistant glove for either)

Juice lemons using electric juicer or wooden reamer

Wash parsley, discard main trunk of stems, finely chop rest

Chop garlic in Mini Cuisinart on chop setting (best if thawed)

Toast and grind cumin using small skillet, watching carefully just till it is fragrant.  Grind in black spice grinder, wipe out grinder before and after.

Sauté

Be sure heat-saver disk (on stove shelf) is placed under stock pot 

Coat the bottom of the pot with olive oil to about ⅛” deep

Sauté (=fry in a little oil uncovered, stirring occasionally) the onions until they are getting soft.

O-Night                                      Recipe: Lentil Soup                         Page 3 of 3

(Sauté, Cont’d)

Add in the carrots & celery and 2t salt.  Sauté for 20 minutes or so to develop the flavor.

Add in the potatoes and sauté for another 10 minutes.

Tip: You may want to stand on a stool and/or use the big paddle to get good stirring leverage.

Cook

Lentils

Add the lentils, bay leaves, marjoram, cumin, garlic, parsley and water to the pot and stir.  Gradually bring to a boil on medium high heat.  Then reduce to an active simmer.   

Let it simmer at least until the lentils are soft (1 hr or more), fine to leave it on 2hrs or more or until we leave for the meeting

 Stirring occasionally, add more water if it is getting too thick.

Prepare

Paprika

Mix

(~4:00) Prepare the final paprika mix to add in later:  chop the extra garlic, sauté it with the smoked paprika in the extra olive oil in the small skillet for about a minute.  Turn the fire off and leave it.

Add Greens

(~4:20) Just before going to the orientation meeting, stir in the greens.  Turn the fire off and put a lid on the pot.

After Meeting:

(~5:10) leave meeting early.  Add lemon juice, zest, sherry vinegar & smoked paprika oil mix.  Add half the suggested salt & pepper, taste and add more as needed.

Put gluten free bread in the oven just to warm up a bit.

Help get the main bread into a basket.
