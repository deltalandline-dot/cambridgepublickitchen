Lentil Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings (14-15 qts)

Equipment

  • 32 qt (largest) stock pot, with pot-saver under it
  • Small skillet
  • Zester
  • Juicer
  • Mini cuisinart

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Prepare ingredients: Locate all ingredients.
  2. Measure out the lentils, check for stones, rinse, and set aside.
  3. Dice the onions, carrots, celery, and potatoes.
  4. Collards: remove the thick stem from the center, slice the leaf halves crosswise into 1" wide strips.
  5. Wash the lemons.
  6. Zest the lemons (scrape off the thin outermost yellow layer of skin) using either the microplane (sharp!) or box grater (suggest wearing the cut-resistant glove for either).
  7. Juice the lemons using the electric juicer or wooden reamer.
  8. Wash the parsley, discard the main trunk of the stems, and finely chop the rest.
  9. Chop the garlic in the mini cuisinart on the chop setting (best if thawed).
  10. Toast and grind the cumin using the small skillet, watching carefully just until it's fragrant. Grind in the black spice grinder, wiping out the grinder before and after.
  11. Sauté: Be sure the heat-saver disk (on the stove shelf) is placed under the stock pot. Coat the bottom of the pot with olive oil, about ⅛" deep. Sauté (fry in a little oil, uncovered, stirring occasionally) the onions until they're getting soft.
  12. Add in the carrots and celery and 2 t salt. Sauté for 20 minutes or so to develop the flavor.
  13. Add in the potatoes and sauté for another 10 minutes. Tip: you may want to stand on a stool and/or use the big paddle to get good stirring leverage.
  14. Cook lentils: Add the lentils, bay leaves, marjoram, cumin, garlic, parsley, and water to the pot and stir. Gradually bring to a boil on medium-high heat, then reduce to an active simmer.
  15. Let it simmer at least until the lentils are soft (1 hour or more) — fine to leave it 2 hours or more, or until you leave for the meeting. Stir occasionally, adding more water if it's getting too thick.
  16. Prepare paprika mix: (~4:00) Prepare the final paprika mix to add in later: chop the extra garlic, sauté it with the smoked paprika in the extra olive oil in the small skillet for about a minute. Turn off the heat and leave it.
  17. Add greens: (~4:20) Just before going to the orientation meeting, stir in the greens. Turn off the heat and put a lid on the pot.
  18. After meeting: (~5:10) Leave the meeting early. Add the lemon juice, zest, sherry vinegar, and smoked paprika oil mix. Add half the suggested salt and pepper, taste, and add more as needed. Put the gluten-free bread in the oven just to warm up a bit. Help get the main bread into a basket.
