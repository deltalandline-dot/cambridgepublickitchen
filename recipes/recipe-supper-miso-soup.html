Supper Miso Soup

Day 6                          Supper Recipe:  Miso Soup         page 1 of 1

Equipment:  20qt stock pot  //or 32qt if 20qt will be used for noodles

Ingredients

For ~48

1 sheet

Kombu (if we have any)

10 sheet pack

Nori, cut into squares ~2”

3lbs 

Tofu, diced small (½”) (if no extra in fridge, use aseptic if any)

3.5 cups 

Miso (ideally red, or whatever we have) (3.5 cups = ~1 ¾ tubs)

⅓ cup

Tamari

13 quarts

Water

3lbs 

Carrots, Small dice

8 bunches

Chopped scallions including ~⅔ of the green part

5lbs

Mushrooms, thick slices (maybe pre-sautéd if time)

             Supper Recipe: Miso Soup             

Instructions 

In the morning:

Add all but 1 qt of water + kombu sheet and simmer ~an hour.  Remove kombu.

Add nori, mushrooms & carrots.

Mix miso with another quart of water, prep scallions & tofu.  Put these in fridge for later.

At 5:00

Add in tofu, tamari, miso/water.

~5:25

Just before serving, add scallions.
