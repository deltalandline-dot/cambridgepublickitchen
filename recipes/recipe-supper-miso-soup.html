Supper Miso Soup

Tags: Vegan, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings

Equipment

  • 20 qt stock pot (or 32 qt, if the 20 qt will be used for noodles)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. (In the morning) Add all but 1 quart of the water, plus the kombu sheet, and simmer for about an hour; remove the kombu. Add the nori, mushrooms, and carrots. Mix the miso with another quart of water, and prep the scallions and tofu — put these in the fridge for later.
  2. (At 5:00) Add in the tofu, tamari, and miso/water.
  3. (~5:25) Just before serving, add the scallions.
