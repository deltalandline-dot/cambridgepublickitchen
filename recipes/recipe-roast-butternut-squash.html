Roast Butternut Squash

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Equipment

  • Large baking sheets (est. 3-4)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Preheat oven to 400° (or okay to put in not pre-heated).
  2. Oil each piece if not done already.
  3. Roast ~2 hours (allow the whole morning, can be unpredictable; okay to serve at room temp if done early). Test doneness with a fork — should be very soft, browning lightly.
