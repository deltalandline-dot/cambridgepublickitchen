Roast Butternut Squash

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Recipe: Roasted Butternut Squash                                  

Equipment  large baking sheets (est 3-4)

Ingredients: 

#servings

1 Case 

Butternut squash (peeling left on, seeds removed, cut into big pieces, typically 4 pcs out of the round end, 2-4 pcs out of the thinner end of each).  For smaller retreat, just do it all, have more for soup later.

Oil to coat

Instructions:

Preheat oven to 400 (or ok to put in not pre-heated)

Oil each piece if not done already

Roast ~2 hours (allow the whole morning, can be unpredictable, ok to serve at room temp if done early),  Test doneness with a fork, should be very soft, browning lightly.
