Instructions

Rinse beans in a strainer, place in 32 oz stock pot and cover with water by a 6-8 inches.  Bring to a boil, lower to a simmer and cook for 1-1 ½ hours until beans are tender. Add water if too dry.

Instructions

Saute 

Heat olive oil in rondo and add onions.  Saute until translucent.  

MAYBE SEPARATE OUT A SECOND NO-BELL PEPPER VERSION HERE (for Andrea or other teacher need)

Add bell peppers and yams until they begin to soften.

Add Ingredients

Add chili powder, oregano, and cumin to saute mixture and cook for a few minutes.

Add tomatoes, bring to a boil and lower to a simmer. 

Add beans

We keep cooking the beans separately because they need to be cooked almost all morning to get soft, might not have enough heat mixed in with the rest too soon.  When the beans are soft enough to eat, mix them in with the rest of the stew.

Finish

Let simmer until serving time, adding bean liquid as needed if getting too thick.     (Or you can cover & turn it off,  it will stay warm).
