Chili

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Note: "supper leftovers" here assumes something like a light sprinkle on top of nachos, not a whole other serving.

Equipment

  • 32 qt (largest) stock pot for the beans
  • Rondo for the chili
  • Mini cuisinart

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Rinse the beans in a strainer, place in the 32 qt stock pot, and cover with water by 6-8 inches. Bring to a boil, lower to a simmer, and cook for 1-1½ hours until the beans are tender. Add water if too dry.
  2. Sauté: Heat the olive oil in the rondo and add the onions; sauté until translucent. (Note: maybe separate out a second no-bell-pepper version here, for Andrea or other teacher needs.) Add the bell peppers and yams/sweet potato and cook until they begin to soften.
  3. Add ingredients: Add the chili powder, oregano, and cumin to the sauté mixture and cook for a few minutes.
  4. Add the tomatoes, bring to a boil, and lower to a simmer.
  5. Add beans: We keep cooking the beans separately because they need to cook almost all morning to get soft — they might not get enough heat mixed in with the rest too soon. When the beans are soft enough to eat, mix them in with the rest of the stew.
  6. Finish: Let simmer until serving time, adding bean liquid as needed if it's getting too thick (or you can cover and turn it off — it will stay warm).
