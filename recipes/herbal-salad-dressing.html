Herbal Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings per batch (recipe can be doubled — see note in Preparation)

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • 2 pitchers
  • Extra 2 qt square container with green lid, for second batch

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Note: you may be asked to make 2 batches — read all steps below first; the quantities above are for ONE batch.
  2. Rinse herbs, no need to chop, vitamix will do it.
  3. If making 2 batches, measure a second batch (except oils) into the square container. Add all except oils to the vitamix container and mix on low, increasing to high.
  4. Drizzle oils into Vitamix, mixing while you do. Gradually increase speed until all emulsified. Divide between 2 small pitchers.
  5. For second batch: pour measured container ingredients into Vitamix and blend. Add second batch of oils as above. Pour back into container, label, date and store in fridge for later.
