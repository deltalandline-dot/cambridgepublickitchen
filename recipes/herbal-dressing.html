Herbal Dressing

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Equipment

  • Vitamix container
  • A second 2- or 4-quart container with lid
  • 2 small pitchers

Ingredients for ONE container (you will put this much in each one)

Preparation

  1. You will double this recipe by putting a set of the above ingredients in each container — it's easiest to add each ingredient to both containers, then go to the next ingredient.
  2. Pick fresh basil leaves until you have a loosely packed cup.
  3. Pick fresh parsley leaves until you have a loosely packed cup.
  4. Rinse both herbs and divide each in half.
  5. Add the above ingredients list to each container.
  6. Blend the contents of the Vitamix container using the Vitamix.
  7. Fill 2 small pitchers with it.
  8. Pour second set of ingredients into the Vitamix container and mix it.
  9. Pour the mixed dressing back in the mixing container, cover, label and put in the fridge.
