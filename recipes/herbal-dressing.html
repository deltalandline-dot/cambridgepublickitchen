Herbal Dressing

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Herbal Vinaigrette (instructions for double recipe)

  • Gather all ingredients below, the Vitamix container and a second 2 or 4 quart container with lid.
  • You will double this recipe by putting a set of the below ingredients in each container.  It’s easiest to add each ingredient to both containers, then go to the next ingredient.

Ingredients for ONE Container (you will put this much in each one)

1 Cup water

¾ tsp garlic powder (on spice shelf far left) 

OR If time: poach in oil or boil in water 6 cloves of garlic

(don’t use just raw garlic…too strong)

3 ¾  Cup Canola Oil

2 Cup Rice Vinegar

¼ Cup Honey

¼ Cup Dijon Mustard

½  cup fresh basil

½  cup fresh parsley

  • Pick fresh basil leaves until you have a loosely packed cup.
  • Pick fresh parsley leaves until you have a loosely packed cup.
  • Rinse both herbs and divide each in half.
  • Add the above ingredients list to each container.
  • Blend the contents of the Vitamix container using the Vitamix.
  • Fill 2 small pitchers with it.  
  • Pour second set of ingredients into the Vitamix container and mix it.
  • Pour the mixed dressing back in the mixing container, cover, label and put in the fridge.
