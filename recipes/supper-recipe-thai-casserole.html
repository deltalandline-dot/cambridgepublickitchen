Thai Casserole

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free

Week 2 Day 6                 Supper Recipe:  Thai Casserole

Equipment  2 2” hotel pans

Ingredients

For ~48

~6qts

Left over rice or make more

~7qts

Left-over thai veggies (or add more from freezer)

Maybe more other leftover veggies if any, or more frozen veg, or canned tomatoes? or more tofu, extra spinach if not needed for salad??  or more coconut milk as needed, 

Instructions

Mix or layer whatever you have of the above into the 2 casseroles.  Cover & fridge.

At 3:50: reheat during talk, done when 165 degrees.
