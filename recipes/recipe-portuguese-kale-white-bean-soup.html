Portuguese Kale White Bean Soup

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Adapted from Moosewood Restaurant Favorites.

Yield: ~48 servings (~14–15 qts), or ~36 servings (~11 qts). This gets eaten — no leftover meal is expected.

Equipment

  • 32 qt (largest) stock pot with pot-saver under it
  • Zester
  • Juicer
  • Spice grinder
  • Mini Cuisinart
  • Separate 12 qt pot to give the beans a head start

Prep ahead: soak 2 qts dry cannellini beans overnight, or quick soak in the morning, or use ~7 x 28oz canned beans (not as good, gets mushy).

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Cook beans: The beans should have been pre-soaked. Rinse soaked beans. In a separate 12 qt pot, cover with fresh water, bring to a boil and right away turn to a low simmer, skim if needed, and toss in a stalk of fresh rosemary. Add to the main soup pot about 10:30.
  2. Locate & prepare ingredients: Strain the oil from the oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes, saving the oil to use for sautéing the onions in the step below. Set aside the tomatoes.
  3. When the beans are done, use bean broth and/or water as needed to make bouillon with Better Than Bouillon.
  4. Dice onions, carrots, fresh fennel, and potatoes (usually veggie choppers).
  5. Kale: remove the thick stem from the center and tear into large, bite-sized pieces.
  6. Wash lemons.
  7. Zest 1-2 lemons (scrape off the thin outermost yellow layer of skin) using either the microplane (sharp!) or box grater (suggest wearing the cut-resistant glove for either). Set aside in case needed at the end.
  8. Juice lemons using the juicer (near the rice cooker).
  9. Lightly toast the fennel seeds dry in a small skillet. Grind using the spice grinder.
  10. Chop garlic in the Mini Cuisinart on the chop setting (best if thawed).
  11. Sauté: Be sure the heat-saver disk (on the stove shelf) is placed under the stock pot.
  12. Using the reserved sun-dried tomato oil if you have it, or else olive oil, sauté the onions until they are getting soft. Add in chopped garlic and 2 tsp salt. Sauté another minute.
  13. Add the ground fennel and diced fennel and cook for ~10 minutes. Stir in the carrots, potatoes, thyme, sun-dried tomatoes, and black pepper and cook for ~20 minutes.
  14. Combine & cook soup: About 10:30, add tomatoes, bouillon, and beans. Stir well, bring to a boil, and reduce to a simmer. Be sure there is a pot-saver/heat disperser under the pot. Simmer until potatoes are just tender.
  15. Add greens: About 11:30, stir in the kale.
  16. Final seasoning: Stir in (some) lemon juice & zest to taste and salt to taste. Serve with a large bowl of parmesan.
