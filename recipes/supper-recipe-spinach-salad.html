Spinach Salad

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Day 1                          Supper Recipe:  Spinach Salad Bar            Page 1 of 1

Equipment  serving bowls, lg baking sheet

Ingredients

For ~48

3 ½ cups

pumpkin seeds (roasted, see below)

3lb (¾ of the bucket)

feta cheese, crumbled (save leftover & rest for lentil salad day)

2

29oz cans garbanzo beans, drained

~4-5 cups

Kalamata Olives, drained (check if leftovers in fridge)

4 pints

cherry tomatoes, rinsed *may be located in pantry, halved if time

1 case

baby spinach (supper yogi will prep this)

2 small pitchers

balsamic dressing (should be leftover from before)

Instructions

Toast Pumpkin Seeds
  • Do this first thing to finish using oven early
  • Turn on oven to 350 (can put seeds in before preheated)
  • Spread seeds on baking sheet and roast for 15-20 mins until they start lightly browning.
  • Check and stir at 10 mins

Setup Rest
  • Set-up leftover balsamic dressing in 2 small pitchers, cover with plastic and put in fridge.
  • Set-up all other sides (except spinach) 
  • Cover and label. Put tomatoes and seeds in the pantry, rest in fridge together on “supper” shelf.
