Spinach Salad

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings

Equipment

  • Serving bowls
  • Large baking sheet

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Toast Pumpkin Seeds:
    • Do this first thing to finish using the oven early.
    • Turn on oven to 350 (can put seeds in before preheated).
    • Spread seeds on a baking sheet and roast for 15-20 mins until they start lightly browning.
    • Check and stir at 10 mins.
  2. Setup Rest:
    • Set up leftover balsamic dressing in 2 small pitchers, cover with plastic and put in fridge.
    • Set up all other sides (except spinach).
    • Cover and label. Put tomatoes and seeds in the pantry, rest in fridge together on the "supper" shelf.
