Artichoke Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Recipe: Artichoke Salad Dressing                                       

Equipment  Vitamix, 2 pitchers

Ingredients

For 1 batch 

(~48 servs)

33 oz jar

marinated artichoke hearts (add all including oil)

1 tsp

tamari

3 Tbsp

dijon mustard

2 ½ tsp

agave

½ tsp

white pepper

1 cup

water

½ cup

rice vinegar

1lemon

Juice and zest of 1 lemon

1 ½ cup

light olive oil 

pinch?

Salt if needed

Instructions

Add all except oil in vitamix container and mix on low, slowly increasing speed until all artichokes are pureed.

Drizzle oil into Vitamix mixing while you do.  Gradually increase speed until all emulsified.  Divide between 2 small pitchers.

Taste and adjust seasonings if needed.

Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
