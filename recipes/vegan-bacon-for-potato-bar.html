Vegan Bacon for Potato Bar

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Equipment

  • 3-4 large baking sheets

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Arrange tofu: Preheat ovens to 400 degrees. Arrange tofu in one layer on baking sheets.
  2. Mix marinade and bake: Mix tamari and liquid smoke and spread evenly over the tofu. Place tofu in oven and bake for 45-60 minutes until tofu is crispyish but not burned. Remove from oven when you reach desired texture.
  3. Chop and finish: Once tofu is cooled, chop into small pieces to put on potatoes and add the nutritional yeast and mix.
