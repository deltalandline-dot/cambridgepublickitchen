Vegan Bacon for Potato Bar

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Recipe: Vegan Bacon  (optional)                            

Equipment  3-4 large baking sheets

Ingredients

#servings

with supper

leftovers

#servings

no supper

leftovers

6 lbs tofu

[qty]

Thinly sliced

1 cup

Liquid smoke

½ - ¾ cup

Nutritional yeast

3 cups

Tamari

Olive oil spray

Instructions

Arrange tofu

Preheat ovens to 400 degrees. Arrange tofu in one layer on baking sheets

Mix marinade and bake

Mix Tamari and liquid smoke and spread evenly over the tofu. Place tofu in oven and bake for 45-60 minutes until tofu is crispyish but not burned. Remove from oven when you reach desired texture.

Chop and finish

Once tofu is cooled chop into small pieces to put on potatoes and add the nutritional yeast and mix.
