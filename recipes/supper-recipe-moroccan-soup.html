Moroccan Soup

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

If you have almost nothing left over, there is a recipe for a Moroccan Red Lentil Soup that may be printed in the soups folder, or online under "Other Recipes."

Yield: ~48 servings

Equipment

  • Largest soup pot, filled to ~15-16 qts

Preparation

  1. If you've added so much that it's bland, sauté some more onions — see the original recipe for ideas on spices to add.
  2. Bring to a full boil, turn off the fire, and put the lid on — it will stay hot until 5:00.
  3. Serve with yogurt if you have some, and cilantro if you have some.
