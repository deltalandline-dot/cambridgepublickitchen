Moroccan Soup

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Week 2 Day 7                 Supper Recipe:  Moroccan Soup

Equipment  largest soup pot fill to ~15-16 qts

Ingredients

Note:  if you have almost nothing left, there is a recipe for a Moroccan Red Lentil Soup maybe printed in the soups folder or online in “Other Recipes”

For ~48

~2qts

Left over rice pilaf or make more

~7qts or more

Left-over moroccan stew

As needed

More canned tomatoes, better than bouillon mixed per jar instructions, any leftover veggies that fit, maybe some raisins or dried apricots.

Up 2-4 qts

If very short, add some dry red lentils

If you’ve added so much that it’s bland, saute some more onions, see original recipe for ideas for spices to add.  

Bring to a full boil, turn off fire, put on lid, it will stay hot until 5:00.

Serve with yogurts if you have some, cilantro if you have some.
