Instructions

Preheat oven to 400.

Toss cabbage wedges with olive oil in large bowl.  We aren’t trying to keep the wedges together, let it all fall apart and get covered in oil.

Spread onto large baking sheets with parchment.

Sprinkle with fennel.

Put in oven about 11:00 and start checking about 11:45.  Done when lightly browning on the edges.  

Transfer to a large bowl to serve.

Toss with orange juice and zest.  Lightly salt to taste.
