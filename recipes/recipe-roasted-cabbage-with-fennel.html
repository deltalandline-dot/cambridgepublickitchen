Roasted Cabbage with Fennel

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings with supper leftovers (or ~36 servings with supper leftovers)

Equipment

  • Large bowl
  • 4 large baking sheets

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Preheat oven to 400°.
  2. Toss cabbage wedges with olive oil in a large bowl. We aren't trying to keep the wedges together — let it all fall apart and get covered in oil.
  3. Spread onto large baking sheets with parchment.
  4. Sprinkle with fennel.
  5. Put in oven about 11:00 and start checking about 11:45. Done when lightly browning on the edges.
  6. Transfer to a large bowl to serve.
  7. Toss with orange juice and zest. Lightly salt to taste.
