Instructions

Prep:

Chop apricots and almonds and/or pistachios, measure out raisins.  Chop onions.  Zest oranges.

Rinse the rice using the large double-mesh strainer .  

Make bouillon with ~2 qts hot tea water water and ~⅓ cup Better than Bouillon.  Add rest of water cold.

Make Rice

Using bouillon & rest of water, make rice in the rice cooker.

Saute

While the rice is cooking,  saute the onions, when translucent, add the fruit and saute a bit. 

Finish

When rice is done, stir in the sauted onions, apricots and raisins.  Let it sit for ~10 mins covered.   Stir in the orange zest.

Maybe set aside a no-nuts portion.

Stir in the nuts just before serving.
