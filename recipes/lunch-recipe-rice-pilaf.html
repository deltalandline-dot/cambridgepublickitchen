Rice Pilaf

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings if there's no supper that day (we make soup from the leftover tagine instead), or ~36 servings otherwise.

Equipment

  • Large strainer
  • Largest skillet or rondo
  • Rice cooker
  • Large bowl to mix & serve (maybe)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Prep: Chop the apricots and almonds and/or pistachios, and measure out the raisins. Chop the onions. Zest the oranges.
  2. Rinse the rice using the large double-mesh strainer. Make bouillon with ~2 qts hot tea water and ~⅓ cup Better Than Bouillon; add the rest of the water cold.
  3. Make Rice: Using the bouillon and the rest of the water, make the rice in the rice cooker.
  4. Sauté: While the rice is cooking, sauté the onions; when translucent, add the fruit and sauté a bit.
  5. Finish: When the rice is done, stir in the sautéed onions, apricots, and raisins. Let it sit for ~10 minutes, covered. Stir in the orange zest. Maybe set aside a no-nuts portion. Stir in the nuts just before serving.
