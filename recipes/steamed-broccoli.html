Steamed Broccoli

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

ALT Week 2 Day 7                         Recipe: Steamed Broccoli                                    Page 1 of 1

Equipment  2-6” hotel pans, 2 steamer pans, 2 lids

You may need to wash the steamer pans used for the tempeh to reuse for the broccoli, or maybe the veggie choppers have set it up already.

We’re doing a whole case, so you may need to do 2 rounds, it can all be finished a bit early, anytime from 11:30 onward or so and be served room temperature if needed. 

Setup 2 steamer assemblies:

  • Get the two 6” deep (deepest) solid pans and fill them with 2” of water.
  • Set the water filled pans across 2 burners each.
  • Divide in the 2 perforated pans as much broccoli as will fit with the lid fully closed
  • Set the perforated pans in the water filled pans and put a lid on each

Instructions

Turn on both burners.  Let the broccoli steam for about 5 minutes after the water comes to a boil.  Carefully lift the lid and check on it.

When it looks done, remove the perforated pans from the base pans and empty the broccoli into a large metal serving bowl.

Refill one or both pans with any remaining broccoli and steam for just 2-3 minutes (we may use some of these for further baking, so leave barely done).  Leave the pans on the counter (out of the water) so we can serve more if needed.
