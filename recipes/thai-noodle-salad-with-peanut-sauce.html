Thai Noodle Salad with Peanut Sauce

Tags: Vegetarian, Dairy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Recipe by Sylvia Fountaine of Feasting at Home. A simple delicious recipe for Thai Noodle Salad with Peanut Sauce, loaded up with healthy veggies. Vegan and gluten free, this make-ahead salad is perfect for midweek lunches or large gatherings. Add Sesame Ginger Tofu for added protein.

Yield: 6-8 servings per the original recipe (prep 15 min, cook 15 min, total 30 min); ingredient quantities below are scaled ×6 for IRC, with the original single-batch amount noted in parentheses

Ingredients

  • Thai Peanut Sauce (you may not need all of it — save extras for another use):

Preparation

  1. Cook Noodles: (see notes for rice noodles) Cook pasta according to package directions. Drain and chill under cold running water, then drain again.
  2. Peanut Sauce: While the noodles are cooking, add all the peanut sauce ingredients to a blender and blend until smooth. (Can be done up to 3 days ahead and refrigerated.)
  3. Prep your veggies: Slice, grate, and chop.
  4. Combine: Place shredded veggies, bell pepper, scallions, cilantro, and jalapeño into a serving bowl. Toss. Add the cold noodles to the bowl and toss again. Pour some peanut sauce over top and toss well to combine — no need to use all of it, just enough to coat it well, generously. (Save any extra sauce for leftover salad, as it can soak up the sauce overnight.)
  5. Taste: Adjust the salt to your liking, add chili flakes if you want, or more lime, and serve, garnishing with roasted peanuts, cilantro, and a lime wedge.
  6. Optional: Add Baked Sesame Ginger Tofu — made with soy sauce (or coconut aminos or tamari), sesame oil, miso, ginger, garlic, and chili paste; marinate, then bake at 400°F for 30-35 minutes.

Notes

  • Rice Noodles: if cooking pad-thai-style rice noodles, pour boiling water over the noodles in a baking dish, let soak until softened, then drain.
  • Salad will keep 4 days in the fridge.
