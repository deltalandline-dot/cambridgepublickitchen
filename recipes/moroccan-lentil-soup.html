Moroccan Lentil Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~12 quarts

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the onions, carrots, and celery and sauté until tender (about 15 minutes). Add the garlic, coriander, cumin, turmeric, paprika, cinnamon, salt, and pepper and continue cooking for another 2-3 minutes, stirring the whole time.
  2. Add the broth, tomatoes, and lentils, stir well, and heat to a boil.
  3. Simmer uncovered on low heat for about 45 minutes to 1 hour, stirring occasionally.
  4. Blend with an immersion blender (doesn't have to be blended all the way — more than half, maybe).
  5. Stir in the lemon juice, red pepper flakes, parsley, and cilantro, cover, and cook for 10 minutes. Garnish with cilantro if you desire.
