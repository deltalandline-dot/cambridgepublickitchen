Moroccan Lentil Soup

Moroccan Red Lentil Soup

Ingredients

For 12 qts

  • 6  Tbsp. extra virgin olive oil
  • 5  large onion, chopped
  • 6 celery stalk, chopped
  • 6  carrot, chopped
  • 16  garlic cloves, chopped
  • 8  tsp. ground coriander
  • 4  tsp. ground cumin
  • 4  tsp. ground turmeric
  • 2 tsp. sweet paprika
  • 1  tsp. ground cinnamon
  • 2  tsp. sea salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 8 qts vegetable broth, (I used 1 jar of BetterThanBouillon dissolved in 1qt tea water+ 7qts water)
  • 1 lg can crushed tomatoes (or diced tomatoes)
  • 8 cups red lentils, picked over, washed and rinsed until water runs clean
  • ½ c. ~6 lemons, juiced
  • ~ ¼ tsp cayenne 
  • 1cup  fresh flat-leaf parsley, chopped
  • (on the side) fresh cilantro, chopped

Instructions

  • Heat olive oil in large pot over medium-high heat. Add the onions, carrots and celery and sauté until tender (about 15 minutes). Add the garlic, coriander, cumin, turmeric, paprika, cinnamon, salt and pepper and continue cooking for another 2-3 minutes stirring the whole time.
  • Add the broth, tomatoes, and lentils stir well and heat to a boil.
  • Simmer uncovered on low heat for about 45 minutes to 1 hour, stirring occasionally.
  • Blend with immersion blender (don’t have to blend all the way, more than half maybe).
  • Stir in the lemon juice, red pepper flakes, parsley and cilantro, cover and cook for 10 minutes. Garnish with cilantro if you desire.
