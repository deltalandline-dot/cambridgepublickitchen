Roast Squash

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Equipment  4 large baking sheets

Ingredients:

25lbs

Mixed yellow squash and zucchini 

5 onions

4 onions

Red onion, slivered

Salt

Olive oil

Instructions

Preheat Oven to 400

Veggie choppers/KM will leave squash cut up and salted in a colander to drain over a bowl.

Lunch cook should thoroughly rinse salt off the squash.

Using white towel(s), pat dry in batches and put into a large bowl.  

Add in slivered red onion if using.

Toss with olive oil.

Spread out on baking sheets (no parchment).

If making along with cornbread, we will cook in two batches to free up half the oven for later and serve with half at room-temp, half hot.

Put tray in oven, check & stir every 20 mins.  Probably takes about 40 minutes.  Done when soft and nicely browning.
