Roast Squash

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Equipment

  • 4 large baking sheets

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Preheat oven to 400°.
  2. Veggie choppers will leave squash cut up and salted in a colander to drain over a bowl.
  3. Lunch cook should thoroughly rinse salt off the squash.
  4. Using white towel(s), pat dry in batches and put into a large bowl. Add in slivered red onion if using. Toss with olive oil. Spread out on baking sheets (no parchment).
  5. If making along with cornbread, cook in two batches to free up half the oven for later, and serve with half at room temp, half hot.
  6. Put tray in oven, check & stir every 20 minutes. Probably takes about 40 minutes. Done when soft and nicely browning.
