White Bean Side

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Assume the kitchen manager soaked the dry beans; if not, either try the quick-soak method during Veggie Chop/first sit period, or use canned beans (or Instant Pot if you know how). This is meant to be offered as a vegan "condiment" to add to the ratatouille, then mix any leftovers into the supper casserole.

Yield: ~50 servings (or ~36 if not doing a leftover batch)

Equipment

  • 12 or 16 qt stock pot

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. For dry beans: (~9:30) start first thing — strain off the soaking water, cover with fresh water.
  2. Toss in 2 rosemary stalks. Bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer. Cook until soft.
  3. Strain into a serving bowl. Remove rosemary, toss with olive oil. Garnish with a fresh rosemary stalk.
  4. For canned beans: same as above, but only cook long enough to reheat — don't overcook. Could start about 11:00.
  5. For Instant Pot (NOT TESTED): cover with water, add oil & rosemary. If pre-soaked, pressure cook on high for ~7 mins; if not, ~50 mins.
