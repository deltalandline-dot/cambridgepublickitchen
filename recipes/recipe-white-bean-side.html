Day 3                            Recipe: White Beans with Rosemary for Side                       Page 1 of 1                             

Equipment  12 or 16 qt stock pot

Ingredients: 

Assume KM soaked the dry beans...if not, either try quick soak method during Veggie Chop/first sit period or use canned (or Instant Pot if you know how)  

For ~50

For no left or ~36 with LO

This idea is to offer this as a vegan “condiment” to add to the ratatouille and then mix any leftovers into the supper casserole.

2.5 qts dry

1 ¾ qts dry

Cannellini beans  

4 fresh sprigs rosemary from the garden

=

2 to cook with, 2 for garnish

~ ¼ cup olive oil

1 T

2t

salt

Instructions

For dry beans:

Start first thing (9:30): Strain off  soaking water.  Cover with fresh water.

Toss in 2 rosemary stalks.  Bring to a boil, reduce to simmer.  Cook until soft.

Strain into a serving bowl.  Remove rosemary, toss with olive oil.  Garnish with fresh rosemary stalk.

For canned beans:  

Same as above, but only cook long enough to reheat, don’t over cook.  Could start about 11:00.

For Instant Pot NOT TESTED

Cover with water, add oil & rosemary.  If pre-soaked, pressure cook on high for ~7mins, if not, ~50 mins.
