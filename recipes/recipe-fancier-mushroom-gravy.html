Fancier Mushroom Gravy

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: 48 servings (larger amounts include a reserve for supper leftovers; smaller amounts in parentheses are for 48 servings with no supper leftovers)

Equipment

  • Large skillet
  • Small rondo (aka "prune pot") or 10 qt pot

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Prepare: In the largest skillet, begin to caramelize the onions. (Caramelize means frying them in oil but on very low heat for a long time — about 30-40 minutes — stirring occasionally until they all turn brown but not burned.) This step is optional; you can just sauté them until soft if short on time.
  2. Start stock: Mix the bouillon, add in the dry shiitakes if using, and simmer (ideal to use the 8 qt "prune pot").
  3. Chop shiitakes: When the shiitakes are soft, fish them out, remove the stems, chop them up, and return them to the stock.
  4. Sauté mushrooms: When the onions are done, set them aside and sauté the mushrooms in the same skillet (you may need more oil). When done, you could "deglaze" the skillet: put a cup or so of stock water in the skillet and stir it up to capture the flavored oil and caramelized bits, then pour it back into the stock.
  5. Add rest: Add the caramelized onions, sautéed mushrooms, and all other seasonings except the cornstarch to the stock.
  6. Thicken: Mix the cornstarch with enough cold water to dissolve it. Add to the gravy. Bring to a boil for 3-5 minutes until it thickens. Return to a simmer. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed.
