Instructions:

Prepare

In largest skillet, begin to carmelize the onions.   [Carmelize means  fry them in oil but on very low heat for a long time (~30-40 mins?) stirring occasionally until they all turn brown but not burned].  

This is optional, can just saute them until soft if short of time.

Start Stock

Mix the bouillon, add in the dry shitakes if using and simmer. (ideal to use the 8qt “prune pot”)

Chop Shiktakes

When shitakes are soft, fish them out, remove stems, chop them up & return them to stock.

Saute mushrooms

When the onions are done, set aside and saute the mushrooms in the same skillet (you may need more oil).  When done, you could “deglaze” the skillet: put  a cup or so of stock water in the skillet and stir it up to capture the flavored oil and carmelized bits, pour it back into the stock.

Add rest

Add the carmelized onions, sauted mushrooms and all other seasonings except the cornstarch to the stock.   

Thicken

Mix cornstarch with enough cold water to dissolve it.  Add to the  gravy.  Bring to a boil for 3-5 minutes until it thickens.  Return to simmer.  Taste and adjust seasoning if needed.
