Vegan Ricotta Cheese

Prep Time: 10 mins

Total Time: 10 mins

This vegan ricotta cheese tastes just as rich and creamy as the real thing! It's a delicious dairy-free, nut-free, and gluten-free substitute for

the ricotta in lasagna, stuffed shells, and more. Find additional uses in the blog post above.
