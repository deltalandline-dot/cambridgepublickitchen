Artichoke Hummus

Artichoke Hummus   from Cook’s Website

INGREDIENTS

3x  6 cups     Orig makes 2 cups

Lg jar 1c drained canned or jarred artichoke hearts 

rinsed and patted dry 

1 c ¼ c lemon juice (save zest ..)

1 tsp ¼ t grated lemon zest

¾ c ¼ c water

1c+2Tbl 6Tbls  tahini, stirred well (see note)

6T 2t extra-virgin olive oil, plus extra 

for drizzling

1 ½ 29oz can  1 14oz cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed 

(or 3 14oz)       

3 1  garlic clove (blender will mince)

2 ½ tsp ½ tsp salt

¼ tsp 0  white pepper

2 Pinches? Pinch  Cayenne

2Tlb 2tsp   fresh parsley or mint

(OVER FOR INSTRUCTIONS—>)

Set aside 4 whole artichoke pieces for garnish.

Combine lemon juice and water in small bowl or measuring cup. 

Whisk together tahini and 2 tablespoons oil in second small bowl or measuring cup.

Combine chickpeas, remaining artichokes, garlic, salt, cayenne, and lemon zest in a bowl.

PROBABLY YOU NEED TO DO THIS IN 2 BATCHES, SO HALF OF EACH PREPARED MIXTURE AS FOLLOWS:

  • Process half the chickpea etc. mixture in food processor until almost fully ground, about 15 seconds. Scrape down bowl with rubber spatula. 
  • With machine running, add half the lemon juice-water mixture in steady stream through feed tube. Scrape down bowl and continue to process for 1 minute. 
  • With machine running, add half the oil-tahini mixture in steady stream through feed tube; continue to process until hummus is smooth and creamy, about 15 seconds, scraping down bowl as needed.

MAKE & taste, PROBABLY NEED MORE LEMON & SALT, maybe some white pepper.

Transfer hummus to serving bowl. Sprinkle reserved artichokes and parsely or mint over surface, cover with plastic wrap, and let stand until flavors meld, at least 30 minutes. 

Drizzle with olive oil and serve.
