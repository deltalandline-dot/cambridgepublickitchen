Artichoke Hummus

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

From Cook's Website.

Yield: 3x batch (~6 cups) or original single recipe (~2 cups)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Set aside 4 whole artichoke pieces for garnish.
  2. Combine lemon juice and water in small bowl or measuring cup.
  3. Whisk together tahini and 2 tablespoons oil in a second small bowl or measuring cup.
  4. Combine chickpeas, remaining artichokes, garlic, salt, cayenne, and lemon zest in a bowl.
  5. You'll probably need to do this in 2 batches, so half of each prepared mixture as follows: process half the chickpea mixture in food processor until almost fully ground, about 15 seconds. Scrape down bowl with rubber spatula.
  6. With machine running, add half the lemon juice-water mixture in a steady stream through the feed tube. Scrape down bowl and continue to process for 1 minute.
  7. With machine running, add half the oil-tahini mixture in a steady stream through the feed tube; continue to process until hummus is smooth and creamy, about 15 seconds, scraping down bowl as needed.
  8. Taste — probably needs more lemon & salt, maybe some white pepper.
  9. Transfer hummus to serving bowl. Sprinkle reserved artichokes and parsley or mint over surface, cover with plastic wrap, and let stand until flavors meld, at least 30 minutes.
  10. Drizzle with olive oil and serve.
