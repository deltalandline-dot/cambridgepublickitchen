Salted Choc Chip Cookies Vegan GF

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Note: for best texture, make the dough the day before and fridge overnight.

Yield: approximately 60 cookies at this batch size (18 cookies at the original 1x recipe size — this batch is scaled 3.5x; usually get 5x6 per sheet on 2 large baking sheets = 60)

Preparation

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Add the chocolate chips to the flour mixture and toss to coat.
  2. In a separate large bowl, whisk (best with an electric hand mixer) the sugars briskly with the oil and water until smooth and incorporated, about 2 minutes. Note: use fresh, soft light brown sugar; if there are clumps, break them up with the back of a spoon or your hand before whisking.
  3. Add the flour mixture to the sugar mixture, then stir with a wooden spoon or rubber spatula until just combined and no flour is visible. Do not overmix.
  4. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate the dough for at least 12 hours and up to a day or two.
  5. Preheat the oven to 350°. Line a rimmed sheet pan with parchment paper. Remove the dough from the refrigerator and use clean bare or gloved hands to portion it into 2-inch mounds. Size guide: aim for 8x5 = 40 per full-sized sheet pan, or 4x5 = 20 per half pan.
  6. Partially flatten each ball with fingers or a fork (they don't spread out much at all). Tip: use a white plastic scraper to make clear rows between cookies.
  7. Sprinkle the dough balls very lightly with coarse-grained sea salt, and bake for 15-20 minutes, or until the edges are golden. Best to use only the top rack of the oven, or watch the bottom rack very carefully — it burns easily. Check starting at about 12 minutes, since ovens vary.
  8. Let cool completely before lifting off the pans to serve.

Variations: this dough is versatile and easy to work with — try adding fresh herbs or spices, substituting flavored oils, or dressing them up with buttercream sandwiched between two cookies. A few favorites:

  • Substitute the chocolate chips with flaked coconut and toasted almonds, and add ¼ teaspoon almond extract to the oil/water mixture.
  • Instead of canola oil, use half hazelnut oil and half safflower oil, and use dark chocolate pieces instead of chocolate chips.
  • Mix one tablespoon of ground cinnamon into the flour mixture, and add milk chocolate chips and currants.
  • Prepare your favorite buttercream recipe and add in ¼ cup bourbon (or even champagne); mix until smooth and sandwich between two cookies to make cookie sandwiches.
