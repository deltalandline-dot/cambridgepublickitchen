Salted Chocolate Chip Cookies (Vegan, GF)

NOTE: FOR BEST TEXTURE, MAKE DOUGH DAY BEFORE & FRIDGE OVERNIGHT 

Yield: Approximately 60  (18) cookies

Usually get 5x6 each on 2 lg baking sheets = 60

3.5x            (1x orig in parens)

7c (2) cups Bob’s (or Trader Joe’s)  1-1 GF is preferred brand
3 ½ t (1) tsp. baking powder
2 ½ t  (3/4) tsp. baking soda
1 ¾ t   (1/2) tsp. salt
4 ¼ c   (1 ¼) cup dark choc chips (= 2 bags, more is fine too)

-----

1 ¾ c    (1/2) cup sugar
1 ¾ c   (1/2) cup (packed) light brown sugar
2c  (1/2 cup + 1 tablespoon) canola or lite olive oil
1c + ½ T     (1/4 cup + 1 tablespoon) water

-----
Sea salt for garnish

In a large bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Add the chocolate chips to the flour mixture and toss to coat.

In a separate large bowl, whisk [best to use electric hand mixer] the sugars briskly with the oil and water until smooth and incorporated, about 2 minutes.  Note: use fresh, soft light brown sugar. If there are clumps, break them up with the back of a spoon or your hand before whisking.

Add the flour mixture to the sugar mixture, and then stir with a wooden spoon or a rubber spatula until just combined and no flour is visible. Do not overmix.

Cover with plastic wrap. B  refrigerate the dough for at least 12 hours and up to a day or two.

Preheat the oven to 350°. Line a rimmed sheet pan with parchment paper.
Remove dough from the refrigerator and use clean bare/gloved hands] to portion dough into 2-inch mounds.  For a size guide: try to get 8x5=40 per full sized sheet pan or 4x5 = 20 per half pan.

Do: partially flatten each ball with fingers or fork (they don’t spread out much at all). Tip: use a white plastic scraper to make clear rows between cookies.

Sprinkle the balls of dough (very lightly!) with coarse-grained sea salt , and bake for 15-20 minutes, or until the edges are golden.   Best  to use only the top rack of the oven, or watch the bottom rack very carefully, it will burn easily.

//Check starting at about 12 mins, our ovens are variable in behavior.

Let cool completely before lifting off the pans to serve.

We love to experiment with our base recipes. This dough is incredibly versatile and easy to work with. Try adding in fresh herbs or spices, substituting flavored oils, or dress them up by sandwiching some buttercream in between two cookies and offer them as party favors. You can get creative and try these combinations or create your own! Here are some of our favorite variations:

*Substitute the chocolate chips with flaked coconut and toasted almonds, and add in ¼ teaspoon almond extract to the oil/water mixture.

*Instead of canola oil, use half hazelnut oil and half safflower oil and add dark chocolate pieces instead of chocolate chips.

*Mix one tablespoon of ground cinnamon into the flour mixture, and add milk chocolate chips and currants.

*Prepare your favorite buttercream recipe and add in ¼ cup bourbon (or even champagne). Mix until smooth and sandwich in between two cookies to make cookie sandwiches.
