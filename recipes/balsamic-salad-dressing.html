Balsamic Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings (1 batch); you may be asked to make 2 batches

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • 2 pitchers
  • Extra 2qt square container with green lid, for a second batch

Ingredients (quantities for ONE batch)

Preparation

  1. If making 2 batches, measure all but the oils for both batches at the same time, putting the second batch's quantities into a 4qt square container.
  2. Mix all ingredients except oil in the Vitamix until smooth.
  3. Drizzle oils into the Vitamix while mixing. Gradually increase speed until all emulsified. Divide between 2 small pitchers.
  4. For a second batch: pour the measured container of ingredients into the Vitamix pitcher and blend. Add the second batch of oils as above. Pour back into the container, label, date, and store in the fridge for later.
