Balsamic Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Scale recipe: or ×

Recipe: Balsamic Salad Dressing                                       

Equipment  Vitamix, 2 pitchers, (extra 2qt square container with green lid for second batch)

Ingredients

For 1 batch 

(~48 servs)

Note: you may be asked to make 2 batches, FIRST READ instructions below for how to do that...these are the quantities for ONE BATCH

1 ½ cup

Balsamic Vinegar

2 cups

water

2 tsp

sherry vinegar

2Tbls + 2 tsp

dijon mustard

2 tsp

agave 

1 tsp

black pepper

½ tsp

salt

4 big cloves

garlic minced in mini cuisinart (or 1 ½  tsp garlic powder– fresh is better, but maybe check diets for sensitivity to raw garlic)

2 cups

olive oil

1 ¼ cups

light olive oil

Instructions

If making 2 batches, measure all but oils of both batches at the same time, putting the  second batch quantities into a 4qt square container.

Mix all except oil in the Vitamix until smooth.

Drizzle oils into Vitamix mixing while you do.  Gradually increase speed until all emulsified.    Divide between 2 small pitchers.

For second batch: pour measured container of ingredients into Vitamix pitcher and blend.  Add second batch of oils as above.

Pour back into container, label, date and store in fridge for later.
