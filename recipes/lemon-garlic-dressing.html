Lemon-Garlic Dressing

For 12

2 garlic cloves

1.5 t. salt

6 T. fresh lemon juice

3 T. white wine vinegar (IRC didn’t have, so I used the “orange muscat” vinegar in the pantry)

¾ t. lemon zest

Ground black pepper to taste

1-1/8 c. extra-virgin olive oil (which I’ve now learned is written “EVOO”)
