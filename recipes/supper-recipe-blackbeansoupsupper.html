Black Bean Soup Supper

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Week 2 Day 3    Supper Recipe:  Leftover Black Bean Soup w/ Corn & Tomato

Equipment:  largest soup pot (to stay warm)

Ingredients:  leftover black bean soup with corn & tomato

Instructions

For Soup:

Add your leftovers to the soup pot.   Augment as needed to have ~16qts (or less if lots of 8 precepts) adding:

  • Canned tomatoes
  • Frozen corn, up to 5 packs makes it interesting
  • Better than Bouillon

Always put thick soups on a flame disperser and stir often but lightly if bottom is getting burned, don’t scrape it up.

Bring to a full boil (or 180), turn off fire, put lid on, the big pots will stay warm until 5:00.

DO NOT serve with chips, they are reserved for other meals.

Sides:

Set up what’s available of sour creams, or cheddar cheese, cilantro.
