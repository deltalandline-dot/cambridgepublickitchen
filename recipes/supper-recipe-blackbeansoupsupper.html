Black Bean Soup Supper

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~16 qts (or less if lots of 8 precepts)

Equipment

  • Largest soup pot (to stay warm)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Add your leftovers to the soup pot. Augment as needed to reach ~16 qts (or less if lots of 8 precepts) by adding canned tomatoes, frozen corn (up to 5 packs), and Better Than Bouillon.
  2. Always put thick soups on a flame disperser and stir often but lightly if the bottom is getting burned — don't scrape it up.
  3. Bring to a full boil (or 180°F), turn off the fire, and put the lid on — the big pots will stay warm until 5:00.
  4. Do not serve with chips — they are reserved for other meals.
  5. Sides: set up whatever's available of sour cream, cheddar cheese, and cilantro.
