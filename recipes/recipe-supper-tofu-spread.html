Supper Tofu Spread

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Crumble the tofu to the texture of crumbled eggs.
  2. Chop the celery and scallions.
  3. Wash and chop the parsley, dill, and tarragon.
  4. Add the other ingredients and mix.
  5. Taste for salt, pepper, and seasoning.
  6. Refrigerate until serving time.
  7. Serve with corn cakes in a towel-lined basket.
