Supper Tofu Spread

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Supper: Tofu Spread

Ingredients:

  • Tofu 6lbs
  • Veganaise 2c
  • Celery, fine dice 4c
  • Tumeric 2T
  • Dijon Mustard ½ cup…more to taste
  • Tamari ½  cup …more to taste
  • Nut. Yeast 6T
  • Parsley 1 bu
  • Dill and/or Taragon 1 bu each
  • Scallions, chopped 3 bu
  • Salt 1 T (start with less, add more to taste)
  • Pepper 1 T (start with less, add more to taste
  • Cayenne ½ t (start with less, add more to taste)
  • Corn cakes 8 packs

Instructions:

  • Crumble the tofu to the texture of crumbled eggs...  
  • Chop celery & scallions
  • Wash & chop the parsley, dill, taragon.
  • Add the other ingredients and mix.
  • Taste for salt, pepper, seasoning.
  • Refrigerate until serving time.
  • Serve with corn cakes in a towel lined basket.
