Chutney Raita

Day 4                                         Recipe: Chutney                                      Page 1 of 1

Note: usually done by 9:30 yogi helper                              

Ingredients

Apricot/Mango Chutney

4 cups

Dried Apricots, chopped TIP: put some oil on a paper towel and oil the knife frequently, cuts sticky apricots more easily!

3 cups

Raisins

4 cups = ~1 lb dry

Dried mango, pre-soaked (SAVE WATER), cut into bite sized pieces.

¾ cup

Coconut

6 cups

Water (include mango water if saved)

 3 T

Rice Vinegar

 1 ½ t

Cloves (ground, see below)

 2 T

Coriander Seeds (ground, see below)

Instructions

Soak the mangos in a metal bowl using hot water (from tea machine) for a few minutes, save water.  Cut into bite sized pieces.

Cut the apricots into fourths or sixths.

Grind the cloves and coriander in the black spice grinder (together is fine).

Mix all the ingredients together in the smallest stock pot.  Bring to a boil, reduce heat to simmer and let simmer for about 15 minutes.  Remove from heat, cool, refrigerate if for next day.  Nice to garnish with nasturtiums or herbs.
