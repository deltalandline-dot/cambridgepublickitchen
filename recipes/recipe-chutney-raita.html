Apricot/Mango Chutney

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Soak the mangoes in a metal bowl using hot water (from the tea machine) for a few minutes; save the water. Cut into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Cut the apricots into fourths or sixths.
  3. Grind the cloves and coriander in the black spice grinder (together is fine).
  4. Mix all the ingredients together in the smallest stock pot. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to a simmer, and let simmer for about 15 minutes. Remove from heat, cool, and refrigerate if making for the next day. Nice to garnish with nasturtiums or herbs.
