Gazpacho Toledano

Tags: Vegetarian, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: a single batch serves 6 to 8; the "X8" batch (scaled ×8, quantities in parentheses below) served a group of ~39 with about 7 qts left over.

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Cut the tomatoes into quarters. Peel and seed the cucumbers and cut into approximately 1-inch pieces. Seed the bell pepper and cut into approximately 1-inch pieces. Peel and coarsely chop the onion. Put the garlic through a press. Cube the bread.
  2. Now put the prepared ingredients, 2 to 3 cups at a time, into a blender, adding a little of the water each time. Blend at medium or high speed until the vegetables and bread are puréed. Pour the purée into a large bowl.
  3. Add the oil and vinegar, the remaining water, and the seasonings, and whisk vigorously until all is blended. If the soup is too thick, it may be thinned with a little more cold water.
  4. Chill the soup in the refrigerator for several hours before serving. Arrange the garnishes attractively on a large platter and pass them around the table as you serve the soup. (We just did cucumber, tomato, and parmesan for garnish last time.)
  5. Optional: set out canned white beans and walnuts as a salad topping, for protein.
