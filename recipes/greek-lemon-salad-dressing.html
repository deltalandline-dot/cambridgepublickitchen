Greek Lemon Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Scale recipe: or ×

Recipe: Greek Lemon Salad Dressing                                       

Equipment  Vitamix, microplane zester (or box grater), electric juicer, 2 pitchers

Ingredients

For 1 batch 

(~48 servs)

~½  Tbsp

zest of 1 lemon //zest is just the outermost colored layer of the peel

½   cup

lemon juice (about 6 lemons)

2 limes

juice of two limes (optional if no limes..)

1  cups

rice vinegar

⅓  cup

Agave 

2 Tbls

dijon mustard

6 cloves (or 1 ½ tsp)

garlic cloves (or powder)

1T

oregano

2 cups

olive oil

2 cups

Light olive oil

¾ cup

water

½ tsp

salt

Instructions

Zest lemons with zester.  BE CAREFUL! VERY SHARP!

Juice lemons with press juicer.

Add all except oil in vitamix container and mix on low.

Drizzle oil into Vitamix mixing while you do.  Gradually increase speed until all emulsified.    Taste & adjust seasoning if needed.

Divide between 2 small pitchers.

Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
