Greek Lemon Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings (1 batch)

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • Microplane zester (or box grater)
  • Electric juicer
  • 2 pitchers

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Zest lemons with zester. Be careful, very sharp!
  2. Juice lemons with press juicer.
  3. Add all except oil in vitamix container and mix on low.
  4. Drizzle oil into Vitamix mixing while you do. Gradually increase speed until all emulsified. Taste & adjust seasoning if needed.
  5. Divide between 2 small pitchers.
  6. Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
