Fennel Leek Potato Soup 

for supper for 36  (created on the fly 10/27/16)

 

saute 4 chopped onion

add 7 chopped garlic cloves & saute

add 4 each chopped carrot and celery stalks

chopped 9 leeks, 6 bulb fennel and 10lbs? yukon gold potatoes

saute all that awhile and add Better Than Bouillon "no-chicken" broth paste & water per jar instructions (enough to look soup-like)

add ~1T oregano

Immersion blend some of the soup & leave some whole.

salt & pepper to taste

 

We served this with a Caesar Salad (parm, croutons, romaine, see Vegan Caeaer dressing).
