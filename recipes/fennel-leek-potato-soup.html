Fennel Leek Potato Soup

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: for supper, ~36 servings (recipe created on the fly, 10/27/16)

Preparation

  1. Sauté 4 chopped onions.
  2. Add 7 chopped garlic cloves and sauté.
  3. Add 4 each chopped carrot and celery stalks.
  4. Chop 9 leeks, 6 bulb fennel, and ~10 lbs Yukon gold potatoes.
  5. Sauté all of that awhile, then add Better Than Bouillon "no-chicken" broth paste and water per the jar instructions (enough to look soup-like).
  6. Add ~1 T oregano.
  7. Immersion-blend some of the soup and leave some whole.
  8. Salt and pepper to taste.
  9. Served with a Caesar Salad (parmesan, croutons, romaine — see the Vegan Caesar Dressing recipe).
