Lemon Tahini Dressing-IF

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Lemon Tahini Dressing

  • Gather all ingredients below and one Vitamix container.
  • If needed, double the recipe using two Vitamix containers. 

Ingredients for ONE Vitamix container (do this twice if making extra)

1 Cup lemon juice (Juice from about 4 fresh lemons)

¾ Cup parsley 

5 Cloves garlic

2 ½ Cups water

1 ½ Tablespoon tamari (if no soy yogis use salt instead)

2 teaspoons ground cumin

½ teaspoon white pepper 

2 ½ Cup Tahini

  • Using electric juicer, juice the lemons.  
  • Wash parsley and cut off stems (some of the upper stems are OK).
  • Add all ingredients in order above to Vitamix container. Repeat if making two batches in second Vitamix container.  
  • Fill two small pitchers with each Vitamix container of salad dressing made.
