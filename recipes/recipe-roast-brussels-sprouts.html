Roast Brussels Sprouts

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Equipment

  • 2 large baking sheets

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Preheat oven to 400°.
  2. Toss in a large bowl with oil, salt & pepper to taste.
  3. Spread out on large baking sheets (no parchment). Put most of them face down if cut in half.
  4. Put tray in oven, check & stir every 20 minutes. Probably takes about 45 minutes. Done when tender and nicely browning.
