Roast Brussels Sprouts

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

                 Roast Brussels Sprouts             Page 1 of 1                     

Equipment  2 large baking sheets

Ingredients:

#servings

with no supper

leftovers

25lbs

Brussels Sprouts, cut in half lengthwise unless tiny

Salt & Pepper

Olive oil

Instructions

Preheat Oven to 400

Toss in large bowl with oil, salt & pepper to taste.

Spread out on large baking sheets (no parchment).  Put most of them face down if cut in half.

Put tray in oven, check & stir every 20 mins.  Probably takes about 45 minutes.  Done when tender and nicely browning.
