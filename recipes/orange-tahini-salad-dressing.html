Orange Tahini Salad Dressing

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: for 1 batch (~48 servings)

Equipment

  • Vitamix
  • 2 pitchers

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Add all except oil in the Vitamix container and mix on low, gradually increasing speed.
  2. Drizzle oil into the Vitamix mixing while you do. Gradually increase speed until all emulsified. Divide between 2 small pitchers.
  3. Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
