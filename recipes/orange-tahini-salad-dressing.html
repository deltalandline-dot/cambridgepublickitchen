Orange Tahini Salad Dressing

Recipe: Orange Tahini Salad Dressing                                       

Equipment  Vitamix, 2 pitchers

Ingredients

For 1 batch 

(~48 servs)

2 ¼ cups

Orange juice (check if we bought some, else squeeze ~4-6 oranges), use at least one or two fresh oranges (ie the 2 you zest below)

2 oranges 

Zest of two oranges

2 Tbsp

Lemon juice

1 cup

Tahini  (1st use up any partial jars on the breakfast tray..) 1c=½ jar

1 cup

water

2 ¾  tsp

salt

1 ½ Tbls

agave

2 ½  Tbls

Finely chopped ginger

5 Tbls

Apple cider vinegar

2 cups

Light olive oil

Instructions

Add all except oil in vitamix container and mix on low, gradually increasing speed.

Drizzle oil into Vitamix mixing while you do.  Gradually increase speed until all emulsified.    Divide between 2 small pitchers.

Cover & store in fridge (if not for today, label and date also).
