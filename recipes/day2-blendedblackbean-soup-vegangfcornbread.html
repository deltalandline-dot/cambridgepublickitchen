Vegan GF Cornbread

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Tab 3

Vegan Gluten Free Cornbread 

Adapted from Nisha Vora rainbowplantlife.com

  • This fits nicely in two  2”deep hotel pans each cut 6x8.
  • This can take a couple of hours of off & on attention from someone (KM?) so consider that.  Some approx. times below.
  • Measurements with “g” need a precision grams scale (we have one or more above the spice shelf, looks like an ipad).

Ingredients:

½ gallon Oatly Full Fat Oat Milk (from Whole Fds)

4 Tbls apple cider vinegar

1170g stone-ground medium corn meal (NOT the polenta, that is too coarse)

564g gluten free all-purpose flour

8Tbls=½ cup baking powder

3 tsp salt

336g vegan butter, melted

336g extra virgin olive oil

240g brown sugar

504g agave

24g=6T finely chopped (use spice grinder) rosemary (from the kitchen garden)

DIRECTIONS (timing aiming for still warm to serve):

  • Preheat oven to 400 (with the Vulcan, try 450 and watch it)
  • ~10:15? Stir vinegar and oat milk and set aside at least 10-20 minutes to curdle
  • In a large bowl combine flour, cornmeal, baking powder and salt. Whisk well.
  • ~10:30? Pour in the melted vegan butter, oil, brown sugar, agave & curdled oatmilk.  Careful not to overmix, some lumps ok.  Should look like pancake mix.
  • Fold in the rosemary.  Allow batter to rest 10min to 1 hour.
  • ~11:10  Heat the empty pans in the over while batter is resting for about 10 minutes.  Remove from over, add more lumps of vegan butter to melt and coat the pan.  Sprinkle pan bottom with cornmeal.   Pour in batter, dividing between 2 pans.
  • ~11:20 Bake 25-35 minutes (in Vulcan, rotate frount/back and top/bottom half way thru, might take longer than 25.  Test with toothpick or knife.   Cool for 15 mins before slicing 6x8 each pan.
