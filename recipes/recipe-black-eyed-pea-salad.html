Black Eyed Pea Salad

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Assumes the Dukkah spice mix is pre-made (see the Dukkah ingredients and steps below) — try to make it ahead.

Yield: ~50 servings, with some leftovers

Equipment

  • If making the peas and rice: 2 10-12 qt sauce pans

Ingredients

Dukkah (spice mix — makes 2 cups, plenty for the salad above)

Preparation

  1. First thing: cook and cool the peas and rice, and toast the walnuts. Assuming pre-soaked peas: rinse and cover with water by ~3" in a 12 qt stock pot, bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer, and cover. Cook ~20-40 minutes or until done (timing varies) — you want them al dente, not mushy, since it's a room-temp salad. Drain and spread out on a sheet pan to cool.
  2. Rinse the rice and add water at a 4:1 ratio in a 10-12 qt stock pot, bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer, and cover. Cook ~40 minutes or until well done (all soft). Drain if needed and spread out on a sheet pan to cool.
  3. Toast the walnuts in a ~250° oven, checking every 10 minutes so they don't get too dark.
  4. Mix sauce: whisk together the EV olive oil, Dukkah, lemon juice, 1 cup of the pomegranate molasses, 1 T salt, and 2 tsp pepper.
  5. Mix salad: combine the room-temp peas and rice with about ¾ of the pomegranate seeds (save the rest for garnish), the parsley, mint, scallions, and sauce. If serving is more than ½ hour away, refrigerate and bring back out about ½ hour before serving.
  6. Just before serving: taste and add — typically the 2nd cup of pomegranate molasses, quite a bit of the lemon zest, and maybe another tablespoon of salt. Mix in the toasted walnuts and garnish with the remaining pomegranate seeds.
  7. Dukkah — roast/toast: rinse and dry one 15 oz can of chickpeas, toss with olive oil, and roast at 350° for ~40-50 minutes, tossing every 10 minutes; set aside to cool. Also toast the pistachios a few minutes in the same oven if not pre-toasted (no oil), watching carefully. Separately, lightly toast the sesame seeds, coriander, and cumin in a skillet on the stove (timing and grinding differ for each).
  8. Dukkah — grind and mix: process the cooled roasted chickpeas in the mini cuisinart until coarse ground; put in a bowl. Pulse the toasted pistachios and sesame to coarse ground and add to the bowl. Pulse the coriander and cumin to fine ground and add to the bowl. Add the pepper and salt and mix it all together. Keeps for about a week in a jar in the fridge.
