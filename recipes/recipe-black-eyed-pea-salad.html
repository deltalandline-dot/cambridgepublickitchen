Instructions

First thing:

Cook & cool peas, rice.

 Toast walnuts.

Assuming pre-soaked:  rinse peas & cover with water by ~3” in a 12qt stock pot, bring to boil, reduce to simmer, cover.  Cook ~20-40 mins or until done (let’s see…never done this…ests vary).  We want them al dente, not mushy for the salad.  Drain, maybe spread out on a sheet pan to let cool off when done.  It’s a room-temp salad.

Rinse rice & add 4:1 water in a 10-12qt stock pot, bring to boil, reduce to simmer, cover.  Cook ~40 mins or until well done (all soft).  Drain if needed and maybe spread out on a sheet pan to let cool off when done.

Put walnuts in to toast in ~250 oven…check every 10 mins, not too dark.

Mix sauce

Whisk the EV olive oil, the Dukkah, lemon juice, 1 c. pomegranate molasses, 1T salt, 2tsp pepper.

Mix salad

Mix the room-temp peas, rice, ~¾ of the pomegranate seeds (saving rest for garnish), parsley, mint, scallions and sauce.

If more than ½ hour before serving: refrigerate and bring back out ~½ hour before serving.

Just before serving:

Taste and add: typically the 2nd cup of pom. molasses, quite a bit of the lemon zest, maybe another T of salt…

Mix in the toasted walnuts.

Garnish with the rest of the pomegranate seeds.

Week 2 Alt                              Recipe: Dukkah                                            p 3 of 3

Makes 2 cups, plenty for above
  • 15 oz can Chickpeas/garbanzo beans
  • 1 t olive oil
  • ½ cup pistachios toasted
  • 1/3 cup black sesame seeds, toasted
  • 2 ½ T coriander seeds, toasted
  • 1 T cumin seeds, toasted
  • 2 t fennel seeds, toasted
  • 1 ½ t pepper
  • 1 ¼ t salt

Roast/toast

Rinse & dry one 15oz can of chickpeas.  Toss with olive oil.  Roast in 350 oven for ~40-50 mins, tossing every 10 mins.   Set aside to cool.

Also toast pistachios a few minutes in the same oven if not pre-toasted (no oil), watch carefully.

Lightly and separately (because of differences in timing and grinding instructions) toast sesame seeds, coriander & cumin on the stove in a skillet.

Grind & mix

Process cooled roasted chickpeas in mini cuisinart until coarse ground.  Put in bowl.

Pulse toasted pistachio & sesame to coarse ground.  Add to bowl.

Pulse coriander & cumin to fine ground.  Add to bowl.

Add to bowl pepper & salt & mix it all together.  

Keeps for a week or so in a jar in fridge.
