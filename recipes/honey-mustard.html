Honey Mustard

Tags: Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~1½ qts (or ~3 qts for 2 meals)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Add all except oil to the blender. Blend.
  2. If room in the Vitamix, drizzle in the oil as you keep blending. If no room, transfer to a larger container and whisk in the oil.
  3. Taste (drizzle a little into a separate spoon) and adjust salt, pepper or other seasonings.
  4. For one meal: fill 2 small pitchers. Serve with smallest ladles.
