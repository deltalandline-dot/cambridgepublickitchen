Banana Nut Cookies

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

An original recipe of the Spirit Rock Kitchen, especially Mary Schjeldahl.

Yield: ~5 dozen cookies (this batch is scaled 2.5× from the original recipe, which made ~2 dozen)

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Using a food processor, finely grind almonds and cashews until slightly coarser than sand — don't over-grind or they'll become nut butter! Remove from the food processor and transfer to a large mixing bowl.
  3. Place the thawed bananas, maple syrup, vanilla, baking powder, and salt into the food processor. Pulse until smooth. Using a spatula, fold this purée into the ground nuts. Once incorporated, fold in the chocolate chips.
  4. Using a cookie scoop (about 2 Tbsp), scoop dough into balls. Keep mounded high for rounder, chewier cookies, or flatten slightly for crispier cookies. Place dough balls onto a cookie sheet lined with parchment paper or a silpat mat.
  5. Bake for 10-15 minutes (or ~20), until cookies are baked through and turning golden.
  6. Kitchen notes: consider swapping racks and turning the trays halfway through baking, since the back and bottom of the oven cook faster. Let cookies cool well before picking them up.
