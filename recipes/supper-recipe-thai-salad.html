Thai Salad

Day 1                            Supper Recipe:  Thai Slaw Salad                      Page 1 of 1

This is to be served with 1 batch of Peanut Lime Dressing (see salad dressings)

Equipment  serving bowls

Ingredients

For ~48

½ large or 1 small

Red cabbage, shredded in Robocoupe or by hand

1 napa cabbage

Shredded (easiest by hand)

2lbs frozen

Green beans, steam until thawed and edible

2 cups

Mint leaves, chopped (check garden if not ordered)

2 cups

Shredded carrots (take from salad bar supply)

Mung bean sprouts

(optional if we bought some)

Baby Kale

Add to above to make a full medium sized salad bowl

2lbs peanuts

Serve whole on side

(also good with 3bu chopped cilantro on side, but we probably offer that with the soup)

Instructions

Mix all except (cilantro) & peanuts.

Serve those and the dressing on the side.
