Instructions

Setup

Preheat oven to 350

(Assume morning yogi has sliced the tofu.)

Mix coating mix.  Use “large flake” nutritional yeast if we have it.

Plan to do this with all 3 cooks: Setup one bowl of oil and 2 bowls of coating mix.  NO PARCHMENT! Efficient layout on the center island:

                                     Uncoated tofu

Sheet-pan   mix-bowl   oil-bowl  mix-bowl   Sheet-pan

                      Person1     Person2  Person3

Coat pieces

Person 2 dips each slice of tofu in oil and places them alternately in one of the 2 mix bowls.  Persons 1 and 3 pat the coating onto both sides of the slices and lay them flat (very close to each other but not overlapping) on the sheet pans.  Takes ~15 mins.

Bake

Allow about 30 mins to bake, but keep an eye on browning process.

No need to flip the pieces, just check for crispy looking golden brownness on both sides of sample pieces.

If done early, maybe turn off oven and leave them in to stay warm.

IF YOU PLAN ON SUPPER, HOLD BACK at least ¾ or 1 whole tray FOR THAT, people tend to just eat this till its gone..;-)

Serve on the baking sheets.
