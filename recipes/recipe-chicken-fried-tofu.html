Chicken Fried Tofu

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free, Soy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Note: set aside the equivalent of one whole sheet if you want to make a leftover batch for a full house — people will over-eat this until it's all gone! You may need to put the last half-sheet in to cook over lunch while you serve the other 3½.

Yield: 48 servings with a leftover supper batch reserved (or the smaller amounts in parentheses below for 48 servings with no leftover supper reserved — about 2.5 slices each — or roughly 36 servings if also serving supper)

Equipment

  • 4 large baking sheets & 1 half sheet (or sometimes 3 large & 3 halves if you roasted the beans)
  • Note: best with NO parchment — hard to serve with it
  • 3 medium metal bowls

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Setup: Preheat oven to 350°. (Assume the morning yogi has sliced the tofu.) Mix the coating mix — use "large flake" nutritional yeast if we have it.
  2. Plan to do this with all 3 cooks: set up one bowl of oil and 2 bowls of coating mix. NO PARCHMENT! Efficient layout on the center island: uncoated tofu, then sheet-pan / mix-bowl / oil-bowl / mix-bowl / sheet-pan, worked by Person 1, Person 2, and Person 3.
  3. Coat pieces: Person 2 dips each slice of tofu in oil and places them alternately in one of the 2 mix bowls. Persons 1 and 3 pat the coating onto both sides of the slices and lay them flat (very close to each other but not overlapping) on the sheet pans. Takes ~15 minutes.
  4. Bake: Allow about 30 minutes to bake, but keep an eye on the browning process. No need to flip the pieces — just check for crispy golden brownness on both sides of sample pieces. If done early, maybe turn off the oven and leave them in to stay warm. For the bigger batch: bake 4 pans for lunch and slide the extra half-pan in as soon as you can, taking it out at the end of lunch (~30 minutes) — this pan goes entirely to the supper (plus hopefully some leftovers from the other 4 pans, so set some aside!). If you plan on supper, hold back at least ¾ or a whole tray for that — people tend to eat this until it's gone.
  5. Serve on the baking sheets.
