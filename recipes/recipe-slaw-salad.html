Slaw Salad

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Yield: ~48 servings (or ~36 servings using the smaller quantities)

Equipment

  • Huge salad bowl to toss, maybe a smaller one to serve

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Thinly shred all the cabbage (Napa by hand; white and purple using the wider blade in the Robo). This does get eaten up and shrinks if pre-dressed, so pile it up and use all 5 cabbages even if it's looking like a lot in the smaller salad bowl.
  2. Use shredded carrots we have, or shred some more.
  3. Wash and chop the mint.
  4. Toss it all together with the spinach if using it to fill a salad bowl. Maybe save some out undressed, and dress the rest. Tip: mix in the huge salad bowl over the pan rack, then move to the smaller bowl to serve.
  5. Serve with a bowl of peanuts on the side.
