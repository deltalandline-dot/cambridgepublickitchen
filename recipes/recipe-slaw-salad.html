Equipment  huge salad bowl to toss, maybe smaller one to serve

48 servings

36 servs

1 med hd

 ¾ med hd

1 ½ lb

1 ¼ lb

Carrot shredded

~2 cups

~1 ½ cup

Baby Spinach (to fill the bowl  & lighten it up)

1 recipe

Slaw dressing: consult KM --options that work from Salad Dressings file:  Peanut Lime is best if no peanut allergies, or save some out undressed or use Sesame Ginger (good with Lime version), Ginger Miso

3lbs?

2  lbs

Peanuts (serve on the side) (~1 lg white serving bowl worth)

Instructions

Thinly shred all cabbage (If Napa by hand, white & purple using the wider blade in the Robo).  (This does get eaten up & shrinks if pre-dressed, so pile it up & do use all 5 cabbages even if it’s looking like a lot in the smaller salad bowl.)

Use shredded carrots we have or do some more

Wash and chop mint

Toss altogether with spinach if using to fill a salad bowl. Maybe save some out undressed, dress the rest.

TIP: mix in huge salad bowl over the pan rack, move to smaller to serve

Serve with bowl of Peanuts on the side
