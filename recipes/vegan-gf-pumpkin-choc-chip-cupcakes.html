Vegan (GF) Pumpkin Choc Chip Cupcakes

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Source: Insight Retreat Center

Scale recipe: or ×

Adapted from Vegan Cupcakes Take Over the World, by Isa Chandra Moskowitz et al.

Yield: ~48 cupcakes (4 dozen); recipe author notes getting 5 dozen out of this batch

Ingredients

Preparation

  1. Preheat oven to 350.
  2. Line 4-5 muffin tins with paper cupcake liners.
  3. In a bowl, mix well the flour, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon and salt.
  4. In a separate bowl, stir together pumpkin, oil, sugar, rice milk and vanilla. Mix well, then stir in chocolate chips.
  5. Add dry to wet and stir with a fork.
  6. Fill liners ⅔ full. You could add a couple of choc chips to top each one, especially if not icing.
  7. Bake for 20-25 minutes (maybe more in our right oven) until they are looking lightly browned and an inserted knife or toothpick comes out clean.
  8. Cool completely on wire rack. (If not for today, store in a lidded ant-proof container!)
  9. Optional Cinnamon Icing: Place sugar and cinnamon in a small bowl. Add the melted Earth Balance, soy milk, and vanilla and stir with a fork until smooth. Let it come back to room temp to judge consistency — the mixture should look opaque; if it looks too liquid, add a little extra powdered sugar. Best kept at room temp unless overnight, then refrigerate and bring back to room temp before using. Pipe on with a sandwich-type plastic bag with one tip-end cut off a small bit. Enough for a squiggle on each of 5 dozen.
