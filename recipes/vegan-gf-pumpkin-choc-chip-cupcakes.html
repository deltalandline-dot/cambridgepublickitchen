Vegan (GF) Pumpkin Choc Chip Cupcakes

Tags: Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free

Vegan (can be GF) Pumpkin Chocolate Chip Cupcakes

Adapted from Vegan Cupcakes Take Over the World, Isa Moskowitz et.al.

Ingredients for 4 dozen = 48 (I got 5 doz out of it...all got eaten!)

4 cups canned pumpkin  (hint: 2 cans=~ 3 ½ cups, sot if you have leftover baked yams, mash 

some of those to save opening a third can)

1 ⅓ cup canola oil

4 cups granulated (regular white) sugar

1 cup rice milk

4 tsp vanilla extract

5 cups all-purpose flour OR Bob’s “1-1” Gluten Free flour

2 tsp baking powder

2 tsp baking soda

2 tsp cinnamon

1 tsp salt

3 cups chocolate chips (about 1 ½ bags, use a few more to put 1-2 on top of each one)

  • Preheat oven to 350
  • Line 4-5 muffin tins with paper cupcake liners.
  • In bowl, mix well the flour, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon and salt.
  • In a separate bowl, stir together pumpkin, oil, sugar, rice milk and vanilla.  Mix well, then stir in 
  • chocolate chips.
  • Add dry to wet and stir with a fork.  
  • Fill liners ⅔ full.  You could add a couple of choc chips to top each one esp. if not icing.
  • Bake for 20-25 minutes (maybe more in our right oven)...until they are looking lightly browned and an inserted knife or toothpick comes out clean.
  • Cool completely on wire rack.  (If not for today, store in lidded ant-proof container!)

Optional Cinnamon Icing:

2 ½  cups confectioners’ (aka powdered) sugar 

1 ½   teaspoons ground cinnamon 

7 tablespoons Earth Balance, melted (~40 secs in microwave)

3 tablespoons soy or rice milk  //check diets

1 ½  teaspoons vanilla extract 

DIRECTIONS 1. Place sugar and cinnamon in a small bowl. Add the melted Earth Bal, soy milk, and vanilla and stir with a fork until smooth. Let it come back to room temp to judge consistency. The mixture should look opaque.   If looks too liquid, add a little extra powdered sugar. Best kept at room temp unless overnight, then fridge and bring back to room temp. Pipe on with a sandwich type plastic bag with one tip-end cut off a small bit.  Enough for a squiggle on each of 5dz.
